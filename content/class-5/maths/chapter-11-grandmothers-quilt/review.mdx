## Key Concepts

- **Perimeter** = total distance around the boundary of a shape (add up all sides)
- **Perimeter of rectangle** = 2 x Length + 2 x Breadth
- **Perimeter of square** = 4 x Side
- **Area** = the amount of flat surface a shape covers (measured in **square units**)
- **Area of rectangle** = Length x Breadth
- **Area of square** = Side x Side
- To find area of any shape on a grid: **count the unit squares** inside it
- For partial squares: count as 1 if more than half is inside, count as 0 if less
- Shapes that tile (cover without gaps): squares, rectangles, triangles
- **Circles do NOT tile** — they always leave gaps
- Same area does NOT mean same perimeter, and same perimeter does NOT mean same area
- Among rectangles with the **same perimeter**, the **square** gives the largest area
- If you know area and one side: missing side = Area / known side
- If you know perimeter and one side of a rectangle: other side = (Perimeter / 2) - known side

## Important Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Perimeter** | The total distance around the boundary of a shape |
| **Area** | The amount of flat surface a shape covers |
| **Square unit (cm²)** | The area of a square with side 1 unit — the basic unit for measuring area |
| **Length** | The longer side of a rectangle |
| **Breadth** | The shorter side of a rectangle (also called width) |
| **Tiling** | Covering a surface with shapes so there are no gaps or overlaps |

## Important Formulas

| Shape | Perimeter | Area |
|-------|-----------|------|
| Rectangle | 2 x (Length + Breadth) | Length x Breadth |
| Square | 4 x Side | Side x Side |

## Remember These

- Shapes with **equal area can have very different perimeters** (9x2 and 6x3 both have area 18, but perimeters 22 and 18)
- Shapes with **equal perimeter can have very different areas** (9x1 and 5x5 both have perimeter 20, but areas 9 and 25)
- The **square** always gives the **maximum area** for a given perimeter
- Increasing area does **not** automatically increase perimeter (and vice versa)
