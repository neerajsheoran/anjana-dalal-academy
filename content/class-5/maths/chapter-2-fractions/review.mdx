## Key Concepts

- A **fraction** describes a **part of a whole** — the parts must be **equal in size**
- The **numerator** (top number) tells how many parts you have; the **denominator** (bottom number) tells how many equal parts the whole is divided into
- **Equivalent fractions** look different but represent the **same amount** (e.g., 1/2 = 2/4 = 3/6)
- To find equivalent fractions, multiply or divide **both** numerator and denominator by the **same number**
- **Same denominator**: the fraction with the **bigger numerator** is greater (5/8 > 2/8)
- **Same numerator**: the fraction with the **smaller denominator** is greater (2/3 > 2/7)
- A fraction is **greater than 1** when the numerator is **bigger** than the denominator (7/4 > 1)
- A fraction **equals 1** when numerator = denominator (3/3 = 1)
- A **mixed number** combines a whole number and a fraction (7/3 = 2 and 1/3)
- Use **1 as a reference** to compare: if one fraction < 1 and the other > 1, the comparison is instant
- Use **1/2 as a reference**: check if the numerator is more than, equal to, or less than half the denominator
- A **unit fraction** has 1 as the numerator (1/4, 1/6, 1/10)
- Fractions can be placed on a **number line** just like whole numbers

## Important Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Fraction** | A number showing a part of a whole (like 1/3, 2/5) |
| **Numerator** | The top number — how many parts you have |
| **Denominator** | The bottom number — how many equal parts the whole is divided into |
| **Equivalent fractions** | Different fractions that show the same amount (1/2 = 2/4 = 3/6) |
| **Mixed number** | A number with a whole part and a fraction part (like 2 and 3/4) |
| **Unit fraction** | A fraction with 1 as the numerator (like 1/4, 1/6) |

## Remember These

- Smaller denominator = **bigger pieces** (thirds are bigger than sevenths)
- For fractions to be fair, parts must be **equal in size** — unequal cuts do not give true fractions
- To convert a fraction > 1 to a mixed number: **divide** numerator by denominator (7 / 3 = 2 remainder 1, so 7/3 = 2 and 1/3)
- Multiplying or dividing both top and bottom by the same number does **not** change the value of the fraction
