## Key Concepts

- **Division** is the reverse of multiplication: if 6 x 8 = 48, then 48 / 8 = 6 and 48 / 6 = 8
- Every multiplication fact gives **two division facts** for free
- **Division check formula**: Dividend = Divisor x Quotient + Remainder (N = D x Q + R)
- Dividing by **10** drops one trailing zero; dividing by **100** drops two zeros
- **Break into friendly parts**: split the dividend into parts that divide easily (1,536 / 4 = 1,200/4 + 336/4 = 384)
- **Nearest round number**: round up, divide, then fix (2,994 / 6 ≈ 3,000/6 = 500, adjust by -1 = 499)
- **Repeated halving**: divide by 4 = halve twice; divide by 8 = halve three times
- The **remainder** is always less than the divisor
- **Partial quotients method**: subtract groups of the divisor step by step, then add all the quotients
- **Standard long division**: divide column by column using place value; write 0 in the quotient when a digit is too small
- Always verify division by using the check formula: D x Q + R = N

## Important Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Dividend** | The number being divided |
| **Divisor** | The number you divide by |
| **Quotient** | The answer after dividing |
| **Remainder** | What is left over when division is not exact |
| **Partial quotients** | A method where you subtract groups of the divisor step by step |
| **Regroup** | Move digits to the next place value when a digit is too small to divide |

## Remember These

- The **remainder must always be smaller** than the divisor — if it is not, you can subtract one more group
- When a digit in the dividend is **too small to divide**, write **0** in the quotient and carry that digit forward
- Multiplying a number by 2 **never** gives an odd number
- Halving a number does **not** always give an even number (half of 10 = 5, which is odd)
- Always **verify** your answer: Divisor x Quotient + Remainder = Dividend
