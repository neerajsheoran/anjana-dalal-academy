## Division — The Reverse of Multiplication

**Division** is the act of sharing a number into equal groups. It works as the **opposite** of multiplication.

If **6 × 8 = 48**, then:
- **48 ÷ 8 = 6** (48 shared among 8 groups gives 6 in each)
- **48 ÷ 6 = 8** (48 shared among 6 groups gives 8 in each)

> **Golden rule:** Dividend (N) = Divisor (D) × Quotient (Q)

## Mental Strategies for Division

### Strategy 1: Break into Friendly Parts

**Example:** 1,536 ÷ 4
- Break it: 1,536 = 1,200 + 336
- 1,200 ÷ 4 = 300
- 336 ÷ 4 = 84
- **Answer: 384**

### Strategy 2: Use the Nearest Round Number, Then Fix

**Example:** 2,994 ÷ 6
- 2,994 is close to 3,000
- 3,000 ÷ 6 = 500
- 3,000 − 2,994 = 6, and 6 ÷ 6 = 1
- 500 − 1 = **499**

### Strategy 3: Repeated Halving (for dividing by 4 or 8)

**Example:** 196 ÷ 4
- Half of 196 = 98
- Half of 98 = 49
- **Answer: 49**

**Example:** 240 ÷ 8
- Half of 240 = 120
- Half of 120 = 60
- Half of 60 = 30
- **Answer: 30**

> **When does halving work?** Use it for dividing by **4** (halve twice) or **8** (halve three times).

## Division with Remainders

Not every number divides perfectly. The leftover part is the **remainder**.

**Example:** 467 ÷ 20
- 20 × 23 = 460
- 467 − 460 = 7
- **Quotient: 23, Remainder: 7**

### The Division Check Formula:
> **N = D × Q + R**
>
> Dividend = Divisor × Quotient + Remainder

**Example:** 853 ÷ 5
- 5 × 170 = 850
- 853 = 5 × 170 + 3
- **Quotient: 170, Remainder: 3**

Always **verify** your answer using this formula!

## Long Division — Partial Quotients Method

![Step-by-step partial quotients division showing 582 divided by 6 with groups being subtracted](partial-quotients-method.png)

This approach removes groups of the divisor one step at a time.

### Example: 738 ÷ 6

**Meera's way (small careful steps):**
- 738 − 120 (6 × 20) = 618
- 618 − 120 (6 × 20) = 498
- 498 − 120 (6 × 20) = 378
- 378 − 120 (6 × 20) = 258
- 258 − 120 (6 × 20) = 138
- 138 − 120 (6 × 20) = 18
- 18 − 18 (6 × 3) = 0
- Total: 20 + 20 + 20 + 20 + 20 + 20 + 3 = **123**

**Anita's way (bigger, faster steps):**
- 738 − 600 (6 × 100) = 138
- 138 − 120 (6 × 20) = 18
- 18 − 18 (6 × 3) = 0
- Total: 100 + 20 + 3 = **123**

> Both approaches lead to the same result! Anita's method is quicker because she picked larger multiples.

## Division Using Place Value

This is the standard long division method that uses place value columns.

### Example: 408 ÷ 4

```
        1 0 2
    ┌────────
4   │ 4 0 8
    │−4        (4 × 1 Hundred)
    │  0 0     (0 Tens ÷ 4 = 0 Tens)
    │    0 8   (regroup: 8 Ones)
    │   −8     (4 × 2 Ones)
    │    0
```
**Answer: 102**

### Example: 245 ÷ 7

```
        0 3 5
    ┌────────
7   │ 2 4 5
    │    2 4   (can't divide 2 Hundreds by 7 → regroup to 24 Tens)
    │   −2 1   (7 × 3 Tens)
    │     3 5  (3 Tens + 5 Ones = 35 Ones)
    │    −3 5  (7 × 5 Ones)
    │      0
```
**Answer: 35**

> **Look out for zeros!** When a digit is too small to divide, write **0** in the quotient and carry it forward.

## Real-Life Problems — Lakshmi's Coconut Grove

### Making Coconut Oil
Lakshmi and her family collected **5,472 coconuts** for oil. They need 8 coconuts to produce 1 litre of oil.
- 5,472 ÷ 8 = **684 litres** of coconut oil!

### Selling Coconut Coir
They earned ₹8,463 from selling coconut coir at ₹21 per kg.
- 8,463 ÷ 21 = **403 kg** of coir sold.

### Fresh Tender Coconuts
Raju bought tender coconuts at ₹15 each and sold them at ₹40 each, earning ₹9,960.
- 9,960 ÷ 40 = **249 tender coconuts** sold
- Cost of buying: 249 × 15 = ₹3,735
- Profit earned: 9,960 − 3,735 = **₹6,225 profit**

## Mathematical Statements — True or False?

Check if both sides of the "=" sign give the same value:

- 7 × 8 = 50 + 6 → 56 = 56 ✓ **True**
- 30 ÷ 7 = 5 − 1 → This does not divide evenly — **False**
- 18 + 12 = 40 − 10 → 30 = 30 ✓ **True**

### Interesting patterns to explore:
- **Adding two odd numbers always gives an even number** → Always true
- **Multiplying a number by 2 can give an odd number** → Never true
- **Halving a number always gives an even number** → Sometimes true (half of 10 = 5, which is odd!)

## Key Words to Remember

| Word | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Dividend** | The number being shared or divided |
| **Divisor** | The number you divide by |
| **Quotient** | The answer you get after dividing |
| **Remainder** | What is left over when division is not exact |
| **Partial quotients** | A method where you subtract groups of the divisor step by step |
| **Regroup** | Move digits to the next place value when a digit is too small to divide |
