## What Is Symmetry?

A design has **symmetry** when one part mirrors or repeats another part in a balanced way. Symmetry is everywhere — in kolam patterns, flower petals, kites, and even the letters you write!

Two main types of symmetry:
1. **Reflection symmetry** (mirror symmetry / line symmetry)
2. **Rotational symmetry** (turn symmetry)

---

## Reflection Symmetry (Line Symmetry)

![Butterfly with a vertical line of symmetry showing both halves as mirror images](reflection-symmetry-butterfly.png)

A shape has **reflection symmetry** when you can draw a line through it and one half becomes the **exact mirror image** of the other half. This special line is called the **line of symmetry**.

### Lines of Symmetry in Letters

| Letter | Vertical line | Horizontal line | Both? |
|--------|:------------:|:--------------:|:-----:|
| A | Yes | No | No |
| E | No | Yes | No |
| H | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| X | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| T | Yes | No | No |
| K | No | Yes | No |
| O | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| V | Yes | No | No |
| N | No | No | No |

### Paper Cutouts Using Symmetry

**For a letter with 1 line of symmetry** (like T):
1. Fold a sheet of paper once along the middle
2. Draw **half** the letter on the fold
3. Cut along the outline and unfold — the full letter appears!

**For a letter with 2 lines of symmetry** (like X):
1. Fold the paper into four equal parts (fold twice)
2. Draw **one-quarter** of the letter
3. Cut and open — the complete letter is ready!

## Rotational Symmetry

A shape has **rotational symmetry** when it looks **exactly the same** after being turned partway around.

### The Chakri (Pinwheel)

![A paper windmill (firki) shown in four positions after each quarter turn, looking identical each time](rotational-symmetry-firki.png)

A four-blade chakri looks identical after every **quarter turn**. During one complete spin, it matches its starting look **4 times**.

| Turn | Looks the same? |
|------|:--------------:|
| 1/4 turn | Yes |
| 1/2 turn | Yes |
| 3/4 turn | Yes |
| Full turn | Yes (always) |

### Rotational Symmetry in Letters

| Letter | Rotational symmetry? | At which turn? |
|--------|:-------------------:|:--------------:|
| H | Yes | 1/2 turn |
| N | Yes | 1/2 turn |
| S | Yes | 1/2 turn |
| O | Yes | every turn |
| A | No | — |
| E | No | — |

> **Key idea:** Some shapes have only reflection symmetry, others have only rotational symmetry, and some lucky shapes have **both**!

## Symmetry in Digits

| Digit | Reflection symmetry? | Rotational symmetry? |
|-------|:-------------------:|:-------------------:|
| 0 | Yes (vertical and horizontal) | Yes (1/2 turn) |
| 1 | Yes (vertical) | No |
| 8 | Yes (vertical and horizontal) | Yes (1/2 turn) |
| 3 | Yes (horizontal) | No |

> **Fun puzzle:** The number **1001** has horizontal reflection symmetry and rotational symmetry (1/2 turn). Can you discover other numbers like this?

## Making Symmetrical Designs

Here is how to create beautiful balanced designs:

1. **Begin with a simple pattern** in one corner of a square.
2. **Reflect it** across the vertical centre line to fill the opposite corner.
3. **Reflect the top half** downward to complete the bottom.
4. Your design now has **both horizontal and vertical** reflection symmetry!

To add **rotational symmetry**, make sure the design looks unchanged when you turn it by a quarter spin.

## Block Printing — Traditional Indian Art

**Block printing** is a centuries-old craft from Rajasthan. Skilled artisans carve patterns into wooden blocks and press them onto cloth.

- A single carved block can be **stamped again and again** across a fabric.
- Many traditional patterns use **symmetry** — turning the same block creates designs that look identical after a quarter or half turn.
- Some block prints show **reflection symmetry**, some show **rotational symmetry**, and some display **both**!

## Comparing the Two Types of Symmetry

![Side by side comparison: reflection symmetry in letter A vs rotational symmetry in letter S](reflection-vs-rotational-symmetry.png)

| Feature | Reflection Symmetry | Rotational Symmetry |
|---------|:------------------:|:------------------:|
| **How to test** | Fold along a line — do the halves overlap perfectly? | Spin the shape — does it look identical before a full turn? |
| **Example** | Butterfly, letter A | Pinwheel, letter S |
| **Line of symmetry** | Yes | Not required |
| **Can have both?** | Yes! (like letter H, digit 8, many rangoli designs) | Yes! |

## Key Words to Remember

| Word | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Reflection symmetry** | One half mirrors the other half exactly |
| **Line of symmetry** | The line that splits a shape into two matching mirror halves |
| **Rotational symmetry** | A shape looks the same after being turned partway around |
| **Vertical line** | A line running top to bottom (like the letter I) |
| **Horizontal line** | A line running left to right (like the horizon) |
