## Key Concepts

- Length is measured in **mm, cm, m, km** depending on the size of the object
- **10 mm = 1 cm**; **100 cm = 1 m**; **1,000 m = 1 km**
- "**Kilo**" means thousand — 1 kilometre = 1,000 metres
- **Millimetres** are for tiny things (SIM card thickness, pencil lead width)
- **Centimetres** are for small things (crayon, notebook width)
- **Metres** are for medium things (door height, swimming pool length)
- **Kilometres** are for long distances (between cities)
- To **compare lengths**, first convert to the **same unit**
- To **convert mm to cm**: divide by 10 (38 mm = 3 cm 8 mm)
- To **convert cm to m**: divide by 100 (346 cm = 3 m 46 cm)
- To **convert m to km**: divide by 1,000 (5,340 m = 5 km 340 m)
- When adding/subtracting lengths, add like units separately or convert everything to one unit first
- When multiplying/dividing lengths, convert to the smaller unit, calculate, then convert back
- **1 foot = 12 inches ~ 30 cm**; **1 inch ~ 2.54 cm**

## Important Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Millimetre (mm)** | A tiny unit of length; 10 mm = 1 cm |
| **Centimetre (cm)** | A small unit of length; 100 cm = 1 m |
| **Metre (m)** | A standard unit of length; 1,000 m = 1 km |
| **Kilometre (km)** | A unit for long distances; 1 km = 1,000 m |
| **Convert** | Change from one unit to another |

## Remember These

- **7 m 8 cm is NOT the same as 7 m 80 cm** — 7 m 8 cm = 708 cm, while 7 m 80 cm = 780 cm
- Between every cm mark on a ruler there are **10 small lines** — each gap is 1 mm
- Always convert to the **same unit** before comparing, adding, or subtracting lengths
- The Statue of Unity (182 m) is India's tallest structure and the world's tallest statue
