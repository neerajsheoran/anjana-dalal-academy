## Measuring Time

Different units of time suit different situations:

| Unit | Used for |
|------|----------|
| **Seconds** | Very brief moments — clapping, a heartbeat |
| **Minutes** | Short tasks — brushing teeth, making tea |
| **Hours** | Longer activities — a school session, baking a cake |

---

## Relationships Between Time Units

```
60 seconds = 1 minute
60 minutes = 1 hour
24 hours   = 1 day
```

> The **seconds hand** on a clock sweeps one full circle every minute (60 seconds). The **minute hand** completes one circle every hour (60 minutes).

## Why Do We Need Seconds?

In a 100m dash at sports day, three students finished in:
- Aarav: **14 seconds**
- Ishaan: **15 seconds**
- Kabir: **16 seconds**

All three finished in under a minute — but **Aarav won** by just 1 second! Without seconds, we would not be able to tell the winner.

> Seconds let us measure **tiny differences** in time with precision.

## 12-Hour and 24-Hour Time

### 12-Hour Format
Uses **AM** (midnight to noon) and **PM** (noon to midnight):
- 6:45 AM = early morning
- 3:15 PM = afternoon
- 10:20 PM = night

### 24-Hour Format
Counts hours from 0 to 23 (no AM/PM confusion):
- **06:45 hours** = 6:45 AM
- **15:15 hours** = 3:15 PM (12 + 3 = 15)
- **22:20 hours** = 10:20 PM (12 + 10 = 22)

### Quick conversion rules:
- **AM times** stay as they are: 7:30 AM = 07:30 hours
- **PM times** add 12: 4:45 PM = 16:45 hours (4 + 12 = 16)
- **12:00 PM** (noon) = 12:00 hours
- **12:00 AM** (midnight) = 00:00 hours

| 12-Hour | 24-Hour |
|---------|---------|
| 5:15 AM | 05:15 |
| 10:40 AM | 10:40 |
| 3:30 PM | 15:30 |
| 7:50 PM | 19:50 |
| 11:25 PM | 23:25 |

## Converting Hours and Minutes

### Hours to Minutes:
Multiply by 60.
- 3 hours = 3 × 60 = **180 minutes**
- 5 hours = **300 minutes**
- 2 hours 35 minutes = (2 × 60) + 35 = 120 + 35 = **155 minutes**

### Minutes to Hours:
Divide by 60.
- 90 minutes = 1 hour 30 minutes (90 ÷ 60 = 1 remainder 30)
- 175 minutes = 2 hours 55 minutes
- 250 minutes = 4 hours 10 minutes

---

## Converting Minutes and Seconds

### Minutes to Seconds:
Multiply by 60.
- 4 minutes = 4 × 60 = **240 seconds**
- 7 minutes 25 seconds = (7 × 60) + 25 = **445 seconds**

### Seconds to Minutes:
Divide by 60.
- 200 seconds = 3 minutes 20 seconds (200 ÷ 60 = 3 remainder 20)
- 500 seconds = 8 minutes 20 seconds

## Finding Time Duration

### How long did something take?

**Method: Count forward from the start time.**

**Example:** Meena left home at 07:30 and reached school at 08:20.
- 07:30 → 08:00 = 30 minutes
- 08:00 → 08:20 = 20 minutes
- Total: **50 minutes**

### What time did it end?

**Example:** Arjun started studying at 5:30 PM and studied for 1 hour 50 minutes.
- 5:30 + 1 hour = 6:30 PM
- 6:30 + 50 minutes = **7:20 PM**

### What time did it start?

**Example:** Priya finished her music practice at 8:15 PM. She practised for 1 hour 25 minutes.
- 8:15 − 1 hour = 7:15
- 7:15 − 25 minutes = **6:50 PM**

## Quick Time Activities

Things you can do in **seconds**:
- Blink both eyes
- Clap your hands
- Turn a light switch on and off

Things that take **minutes**:
- Pour and drink a glass of water
- Wash your hands properly
- Walk to the end of your street
- Count from 1 to 30

> **Try this:** How many times can you bounce a ball in 15 seconds? How many sit-ups can you do in 1 minute?

## Reading Clocks

![Analog clock with hour hand, minute hand, and seconds hand labeled](reading-a-clock.png)

A clock has three hands:
- **Hour hand** (short, thick) — shows the hour
- **Minute hand** (long) — shows the minutes
- **Seconds hand** (thin, moves fast) — shows the seconds

The seconds hand goes all the way around once in **60 seconds** (1 minute).

Each small mark on the clock face = **1 second** (or 1 minute, depending on which hand you are reading).

## Key Words to Remember

| Word | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Second** | A very short time unit; 60 seconds = 1 minute |
| **Minute** | A short time unit; 60 minutes = 1 hour |
| **Hour** | A time unit; 24 hours = 1 day |
| **AM** | Ante Meridiem — the period from midnight to noon |
| **PM** | Post Meridiem — the period from noon to midnight |
| **24-hour format** | Time shown as 00:00 to 23:59, used by railways and airports |
| **Duration** | How long something lasts from start to finish |
| **Elapsed time** | The amount of time that has passed between two events |
