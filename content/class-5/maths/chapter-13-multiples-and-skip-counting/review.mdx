## Key Concepts

- A **factor** divides a number exactly with no remainder; a **multiple** is the result of multiplying a number by a whole number
- Every number has **1** and **itself** as factors
- **Factors** of a number are limited in count; **multiples** are endless
- **Prime numbers** have exactly 2 factors: 1 and themselves (e.g., 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13)
- **Composite numbers** have more than 2 factors (e.g., 4, 6, 8, 9, 12)
- **1 is neither prime nor composite** (it has only 1 factor)
- A **common multiple** appears in the multiple lists of two or more numbers
- The **Least Common Multiple (LCM)** is the smallest common multiple
- A **common factor** divides two or more numbers exactly
- The **Highest Common Factor (HCF)** is the largest common factor
- **Divisibility by 2**: last digit is even (0, 2, 4, 6, 8)
- **Divisibility by 5**: last digit is 0 or 5
- **Divisibility by 10**: last digit is 0
- Any two **consecutive numbers** share only **1** as a common factor
- The product of two consecutive numbers is always a common multiple of both

## Important Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Factor** | A number that divides another number exactly (no remainder) |
| **Multiple** | What you get when you multiply a number by any whole number |
| **Prime number** | A number with exactly two factors — 1 and itself |
| **Composite number** | A number with more than two factors |
| **Common multiple** | A multiple shared by two or more numbers |
| **Common factor** | A factor shared by two or more numbers |
| **LCM** | Least Common Multiple — the smallest common multiple |
| **HCF** | Highest Common Factor — the largest common factor |
| **Divisible** | A number is divisible by another if division leaves no remainder |

## Remember These

- Factors of an **odd** number are always **odd** — they can never be even
- Factors of an **even** number can be either odd or even (e.g., factors of 6: 1, 2, 3, 6)
- Multiples of odd numbers are **not** always odd (3 x 4 = 12, which is even)
- **0 cannot be a factor** of any number (you cannot divide by 0)
- Every multiple of 8 is automatically a multiple of 4 (because 8 = 2 x 4)
