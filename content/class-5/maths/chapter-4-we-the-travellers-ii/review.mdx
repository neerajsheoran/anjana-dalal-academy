## Key Concepts

- To **add** large numbers, line up digits by **place value** (Ones below Ones, Tens below Tens) and add from right to left
- When a column sum is **10 or more**, write the ones digit and **carry** the rest to the next column
- To **subtract**, work column by column from right to left; if the top digit is smaller, **regroup** (borrow) from the column on its left
- Addition and subtraction are **opposite operations**: if A + B = C, then C - B = A and C - A = B
- Always **verify subtraction** by adding back: answer + subtracted number should equal the original
- **Consecutive numbers** follow one after another without gaps (1, 2, 3, 4...)
- Sum of **2 consecutive numbers** is always **odd**
- Sum of **3 consecutive numbers** = **3 x the middle number** (always a multiple of 3)
- Sum of **5 consecutive numbers** = **5 x the middle number**
- Shortcut to subtract 9: subtract 10, then add 1 back
- Shortcut to subtract 99: subtract 100, then add 1 back
- **Even + Even = Even**; **Odd + Odd = Even**; **Even + Odd = Odd**
- A **palindrome number** reads the same forwards and backwards (e.g., 272, 7667)
- **Even numbers** end in 0, 2, 4, 6, 8; **Odd numbers** end in 1, 3, 5, 7, 9

## Important Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Regroup** | Exchange 10 of one unit for 1 of the next bigger unit (or reverse) during addition/subtraction |
| **Consecutive numbers** | Numbers that follow one after another without gaps |
| **Palindrome** | A number that reads the same forwards and backwards |
| **Even number** | A number divisible by 2 (ends in 0, 2, 4, 6, 8) |
| **Odd number** | A number not divisible by 2 (ends in 1, 3, 5, 7, 9) |
| **Estimate** | A rough calculation or guess close to the real answer |

## Remember These

- When borrowing in subtraction, the digit you borrow **from** decreases by 1 and the digit you borrow **for** increases by 10
- Two odd numbers added together always give an **even** result (the two "leftovers" pair up)
- To find what number + 47 = 100: first find what makes 99 (52), then add 1 more = **53**
- Always double-check by using the **reverse operation** (add to check subtraction, subtract to check addition)
