## Key Concepts

- **Tessellation** means covering a surface with shapes that fit together with **no gaps or overlaps**
- Only three regular shapes tessellate alone: **equilateral triangles, squares, and regular hexagons**
- Triangles classified by sides: **Equilateral** (all 3 equal), **Isosceles** (2 equal), **Scalene** (none equal)
- A **square** has all sides equal and all angles are right angles
- A **rectangle** has opposite sides equal and all angles are right angles
- A **rhombus** has all sides equal but angles are NOT necessarily right angles
- A **parallelogram** has opposite sides equal and parallel, opposite angles equal
- A **kite** has two pairs of neighbouring (adjacent) sides equal
- A square is a special rectangle AND a special rhombus
- A rectangle is a special parallelogram
- A **tangram** is a puzzle of 7 pieces cut from a square (2 large triangles, 1 medium, 2 small, 1 square, 1 parallelogram)
- Drawing two diameters in a circle and joining the 4 endpoints always creates a **rectangle**; if diameters cross at right angles, it creates a **square**
- A **cube** has 6 faces, 12 edges, 8 vertices
- In a 3x3x3 painted cube: 8 cubes have 3 painted faces, 12 have 2, 6 have 1, 1 has 0

## Important Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Tessellation** | Covering a flat surface with shapes, leaving no gaps or overlaps |
| **Equilateral triangle** | A triangle with all 3 sides and angles equal |
| **Isosceles triangle** | A triangle with 2 equal sides and 2 equal angles |
| **Scalene triangle** | A triangle with no equal sides |
| **Parallelogram** | A 4-sided shape with opposite sides equal and parallel |
| **Rhombus** | A parallelogram with all 4 sides equal |
| **Kite** | A 4-sided shape with 2 pairs of neighbouring equal sides |
| **Tangram** | A puzzle of 7 pieces that form a square |

## Remember These

- A **rhombus is NOT a square** unless its angles are all right angles
- Regular **pentagons** and **octagons** do NOT tessellate alone (but octagons + squares can tile together)
- The number of shapes meeting at a point in tessellation: 6 triangles, 4 squares, or 3 hexagons
- Every square is a rectangle and a rhombus, but not every rectangle or rhombus is a square
