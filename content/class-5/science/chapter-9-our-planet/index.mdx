Earth - Blue Planet

The Earth is called the **Blue Planet** because most of its surface is covered with water.

When astronauts look **at the Earth** from outer space, the large oceans and seas make it look **blue in colour**.

How are we connected?

We are connected because we all live on the same Earth and share its resources.&#x20;

* We get **air to breathe, water to drink**, and **food to eat** from the Earth.
* Plants, animals, and humans **depend on the same natural resources**.
* What we do in one place can **affect people, animals, and nature in other places**.
* If we **pollute air or water**, everyone is affected.
* If we **protect nature**, it helps all living beings.

**Ayurveda**

Long ago in India, people observed **nature carefully**. They noticed that plants, herbs, sunlight, food, and daily habits could keep the body healthy.
