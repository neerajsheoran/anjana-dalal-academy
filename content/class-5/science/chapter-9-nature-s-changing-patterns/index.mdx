## What is Change?

Change means any difference we see in people, things, or nature with time.

* Some changes happen **very fast**, like switching on a light.
* Some changes happen **slowly** over time, like growing up or changing seasons.

## How Day and Night Occur

Day and night are **natural changes** that happen every day.&#x20;

The Earth moves around the Sun. It takes **one year** for the Earth to complete one round around the Sun.\
The Earth also spins on its **own axis**. It takes **24 hours** to complete one spin. Because of this spinning, we have day and night. The part of the Earth that faces the Sun has day, while the other part has night.
