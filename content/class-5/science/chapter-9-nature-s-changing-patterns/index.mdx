## What is Change?

Change means any difference we see in people, things, or nature with time.

* Some changes happen **very fast**, like switching on a light.
* Some changes happen **slowly** over time, like growing up or changing seasons.

## How Day and Night Occur?

Day and night are **natural changes** that happen every day. \
The Earth spins on its **own axis**. It takes **24 hours** to complete one spin. Because of this spinning, we have day and night. The part of the Earth that faces the Sun has day, while the other part has night.

![](Screenshot%202026-03-30%20055116.png)

As the Earth continues to rotate, day and night follow each other in a regular pattern called a **rhythm of nature**.

## Seasons

Seasons are changes in weather that occur at different times of the year. These changes follow a regular pattern.

Each season has **different weather, plants, clothes, and activities**.

### Why do seasons change?

* The **Earth revolves around the Sun.** This movement is called **revolution**.
* The Earth takes **one year** to complete one round around the Sun.
* The path followed by the Earth during revolution is not a perfect circle; it is slightly oval and is called the **Earth’s orbit**.
* The **Earth is tilted** on its axis. During revolution, this tilt causes different parts of the Earth to receive different amounts of sunlight at different times.

Because of this, different seasons occur during the year.
