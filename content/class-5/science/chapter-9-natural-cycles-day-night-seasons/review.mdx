## Key Concepts

- **Day and night** are caused by the **Earth spinning (rotating)** on its axis -- the Sun does not move
- The Earth completes one full rotation in about **24 hours** (one day)
- The side facing the Sun has **day**; the opposite side has **night**
- The Earth is shaped like a sphere; it spins on an imaginary line called its **axis**
- A **globe** is a small model of the Earth; blue = oceans (cover 3/4 of Earth), rest = land
- India has **six seasons**: Vasanta (spring), Grishma (summer), Varsha (monsoon), Sharad (autumn), Hemant (pre-winter), Shishir (winter)
- **Plants change** with seasons: blossoms in spring, fruits in summer, green growth in monsoon, bare trees in winter
- **Animals respond** to seasons: frogs croak in monsoon, migratory birds arrive in winter, ants march before rain
- **Crop seasons**: Kharif (monsoon -- rice, maize, cotton), Rabi (winter -- wheat, mustard, peas), Zaid (summer -- watermelon, cucumber)
- Many Indian **festivals** are tied to seasons and harvests: Pongal/Sankranti (winter), Holi (spring), Baisakhi (spring harvest), Diwali (autumn)
- India celebrates over **1,000 festivals** throughout the year
- Food and clothing change with seasons: light cotton in summer, sweaters in winter, raincoats in monsoon
- A **Seasons Journal** helps observe and record changes in plants, birds, weather, and people across seasons

## Important Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Rotation** | The Earth spinning on its own axis, causing day and night |
| **Axis** | The imaginary line through the centre of the Earth around which it spins |
| **Season** | A period of the year with distinct weather patterns that repeats annually |
| **Kharif crops** | Monsoon crops needing rain and warmth (rice, maize, cotton, sugarcane) |
| **Rabi crops** | Winter crops needing cooler, drier weather (wheat, mustard, peas, gram) |
| **Zaid crops** | Short-season summer crops (watermelon, cucumber, moong dal) |
| **Globe** | A small spherical model of the Earth |

## India's Six Seasons

| Season | Sanskrit Name | Months |
|--------|--------------|--------|
| Spring | Vasanta | Feb - Mar |
| Summer | Grishma | Apr - Jun |
| Monsoon | Varsha | Jul - Aug |
| Autumn | Sharad | Sep - Oct |
| Pre-winter | Hemant | Nov - Dec |
| Winter | Shishir | Jan - Feb |

## Remember These

- The **Sun does not move** across the sky -- it is the **Earth that spins**
- **Dong village** (Arunachal Pradesh) is India's "First Village of the Sunrise" -- first place to see sunlight each day
- When it is afternoon in India, it is nighttime on the other side of the world
- **Mawsynram** (Meghalaya) is the wettest inhabited place on Earth; the **Thar Desert** is one of the driest
- The **koel's (cuckoo's) call** signals farmers in Odisha that rains are coming and it is time to sow seeds
