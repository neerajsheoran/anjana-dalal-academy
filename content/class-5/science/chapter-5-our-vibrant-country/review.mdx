## Key Concepts

- India gained **independence on 15 August 1947**; the **Constitution** was adopted on **26 January 1950** (Republic Day)
- **Dr B.R. Ambedkar** led the team that wrote India's Constitution
- India is a **republic** -- a nation governed by its people through elected leaders
- The **tricolour** flag has saffron (bravery), white (peace), green (growth), and a blue **Ashoka Chakra** with **24 spokes** (progress)
- Indian currency notes feature **Mahatma Gandhi's portrait**, text in many languages, and famous **monuments** on the back
- The **National Emblem** has **three lions** symbolising power, bravery, and self-assurance, with the Ashoka Chakra beneath
- India has over **1,000 languages** spoken across the country
- India has **8 classical dance forms**: Bharatanatyam, Kathak, Odissi, Kathakali, Manipuri, Kuchipudi, Mohiniyattam, Sattriya
- India's heritage includes **Ayurveda, yoga, and meditation**
- Modern achievements: Chandrayaan/Mangalyaan (space), **UPI** (digital payments), **Aadhaar** (biometric ID), high-speed trains

## Important Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Republic** | A country where the people govern through elected leaders |
| **Constitution** | A book of laws, rights, and duties for all citizens of a country |
| **Ashoka Chakra** | Blue wheel with 24 spokes on the national flag, representing progress and righteousness |
| **National Emblem** | India's official symbol showing three lions, found on government documents and Aadhaar cards |
| **MANI app** | Mobile application that helps visually impaired people identify currency notes |

## Remember These

- India has the **longest written Constitution** of any country
- India is the world's **largest democracy** with over 140 crore people
- **Statue of Unity** (Gujarat) is the world's tallest statue at **182 metres**
- **Narendra Modi Stadium** (Ahmedabad) is the world's largest cricket stadium (1,32,000 capacity)
- The Ashoka Chakra has exactly **24 spokes** representing the 24 hours of the day
