| In weaving, threads are passed **over and under each other** in a regular way to form a strong fabric or object. | ![](Screenshot%202026-03-29%20074527.png) |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |

## Fibre

A fibre is a thin strand obtained from plants(cotton) or animals(wool from sheep).

![](Screenshot%202026-03-29%20075455.png)

Thread (or yarn) is made by twisting fibres together so they become strong. This process called Spinning.

Spinning can be done by hand and by machines.

* **By hand**, spinning is done using spindles and the charkha.

![](Screenshot%202026-03-29%20075653.png)

* **By machines**, spinning is done using electric or factory machines.

  ![](Screenshot%202026-03-29%20075921.png)

### Types of Fibre

* Synthetic Fibres
* Natural Fibres

Synthetic fibres are man-made fibres prepared in factories using chemicals. These fibres are strong, easy to wash, and dry quickly. Examples of synthetic fibres: Nylon, Polyester, Rayon, Terylene.

**Natural fibres** are fibres obtained from **plants and animals**. They are used to make clothes and are comfortable to wear.&#x20;

Examples:&#x20;

| **Cotton** – obtained from the cotton plant. | **Wool** – obtained from sheep.           | **Silk** – obtained from the cocoon of the silkworm. | **Linen** – obtained from the flax plant. | **Bamboo** – obtained from bamboo plants. |

## Life Cycle of the Silkworm

**Silk** is a natural fibre obtained from the **cocoon of** **silkworm**.

![](Screenshot%202026-03-29%20080710.png)

1. Eggs hatch into silkworms (caterpillars).
2. The silkworm spins a cocoon around itself.
3. These cocoons are put in hot water to soften and clean them.
4. Silk thread is then gently pulled out from the cocoon and woven into silk fabric.

## Stitching

**Stitching is** way of joining pieces of cloth together using a **needle and thread**.

* A **needle** is a small pointed tool with a hole.
* A **thread** is passed through the needle and used to stitch cloth.

---

## Embroidery

Embroidery is the **art of decorating cloth** using needle and thread. Stitches are made on fabric to create **patterns, designs, and pictures.** &#x20;

A variety of traditional stitches are used to decorate clothes, bags, shawls, and home items, showing the creativity and skill of people.

**Traditional Embroideries of India**

India has many famous **traditional embroidery styles**, each from a different region:

| **Chikankari** – delicate white embroidery from Uttar Pradesh   | **Phulkari** – colourful flower designs from Punjab | **Kantha** – simple running-stitch embroidery from West Bengal  |
