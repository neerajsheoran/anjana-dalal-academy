## Pattern - Introduction

A **pattern** is a design or arrangement that repeats in a regular way. Patterns can be seen in shapes, lines, colours, or threads.

## Weaving

**Weaving** is the process of **joining strips or threads** **together** to make useful items like **cloth, mats, and baskets.**

| In weaving, threads are passed **over and under each other** in a regular way to form a strong fabric or object. | ![](Screenshot%202026-03-29%20074527.png) |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |

Materials Used for Weaving

Different materials are used for weaving, depending on what is being made:

| **Grass and palm reeds** – used to make mats and baskets                                                               | ![](Screenshot%202026-03-29%20074716.png) |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| **Bamboo** – used to make baskets, trays, storage boxes, chair, tables, handicraft and decorative items and many more. | ![](Screenshot%202026-03-29%20074724.png) |
| **Jute** – used to make bags, rugs, mats, ropes and many more items.                                                   | ![](Screenshot%202026-03-29%20074729.png) |
| **Cotton and silk** – used to make cloth and clothes                                                                   | ![](Screenshot%202026-03-29%20074733.png) |

Importance of Weaving

Weaving is very important in our daily life and culture.

* **Provides clothes and useful items:**&#x20;

  | Weaving helps us make **cloth, mats, baskets, and many other items** by crossing threads or strips over and under each other. | ![](Screenshot%202026-03-29%20075304.png) |
  | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
* **Supports livelihood of people:**&#x20;

  | Weaving provides **work and income to many families**, especially weavers, women, and rural artisans. The handloom sector employs a large number of people in India and helps them earn a living | ![](Screenshot%202026-03-29%20075309.png) |
  | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------- |

- **Preserves culture and tradition:**

  | Handloom weaving shows the **rich culture and traditions of India**. Different regions have their own weaving styles, patterns, and designs that are passed from one generation to the next. | ![](Screenshot%202026-03-29%20075316.png) |
  | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
