## Key Concepts

- **Andaman and Nicobar Islands**: chain of 500+ islands in the Bay of Bengal; capital is **Sri Vijaya Puram**
- **Indira Point** is the **southernmost point of India** (in Andaman and Nicobar Islands)
- Islands develop **unique species** because they are isolated from the mainland (e.g., Andaman wood pigeon, Andaman hornbill)
- **Coral reefs** are underwater structures built by tiny living creatures; they protect coastlines and house diverse sea life
- **North Sentinel Island**: home to an isolated tribe living as their ancestors did; visiting is **banned by law**
- **Sundarbans** (West Bengal) is the **world's largest mangrove forest**, where Ganga and Brahmaputra meet the sea
- **Mangrove trees** have roots that stick up above water to breathe, grip soil tightly, and survive in saltwater
- **Royal Bengal Tigers** of Sundarbans are excellent **swimmers** and hunt in water
- **Northeast India** has 8 states: 7 "Seven Sisters" (Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Tripura) + **Sikkim**
- **Living root bridges** in Meghalaya are made from rubber fig tree roots by the **Khasi people**; take 15-20 years to grow and last centuries
- **Hargila** (Greater Adjutant Stork) was saved by determined women in Assam
- **Western Ghats** pass through 6 states (Gujarat, Maharashtra, Goa, Karnataka, Kerala, Tamil Nadu) -- a **biodiversity hotspot**
- Rivers like **Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri** originate in the Western Ghats
- **Silent Valley** (Kerala) was saved from a dam project by the **Save Silent Valley Movement**
- **Protected areas** (national parks, wildlife sanctuaries, tiger reserves) ban cutting trees and harming animals

## Important Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Island** | A piece of land completely surrounded by water |
| **Coral reef** | Underwater structure built by millions of tiny coral creatures over centuries |
| **Mangrove** | A tree that grows in salty, muddy, waterlogged coastal areas with special breathing roots |
| **Delta** | Area where a river fans out into channels before meeting the sea |
| **Biodiversity hotspot** | A region with an exceptionally high variety of plant and animal species |
| **Endemic species** | Plants or animals found only in one specific area and nowhere else |
| **Protected area** | National parks and sanctuaries where wildlife is legally safeguarded |

## Remember These

- The Sentinelese people survived the **2004 tsunami** by reading natural warning signs
- Sundarbans honey collectors wear **masks on the back of their heads** to trick tigers
- Living root bridges grow **stronger** with age, unlike concrete bridges
- **Mawsynram, Meghalaya** is the wettest inhabited place on Earth
- **Bhut Jolokia** (ghost pepper) from Northeast India is one of the hottest chillies in the world
- India has approximately **75% of all wild tigers** left on Earth
- **Sikkim** is India's first **fully organic state**
