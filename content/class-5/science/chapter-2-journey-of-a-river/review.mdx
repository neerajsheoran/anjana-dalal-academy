## Key Concepts

- The **Godavari** is the **second longest river in India** (1,465 km), also called **"Dakshina Ganga"**
- It originates at **Trimbakeshwar** in the **Brahmagiri Hills**, Western Ghats, Maharashtra
- It flows through Maharashtra, Telangana, Chhattisgarh, Odisha, and Andhra Pradesh, ending at the **Bay of Bengal**
- **Tributaries** are smaller streams that join a main river, making it wider and deeper
- Key tributaries of Godavari: **Manjira, Indravati, Sabari**
- A **perennial river** flows all year; a **seasonal river** flows only during/after the monsoon
- At its mouth, the Godavari fans out into a **delta** where freshwater meets saltwater
- The **Coringa Mangrove Forest** lies at the Godavari delta
- Rivers support people through drinking water, **irrigation**, cooking, industry, fishing, and transport
- **Dams** store water in **reservoirs** for year-round supply, farming, and **hydroelectricity**
- Dams cause problems: flooding of land, displacement of families, destruction of wildlife habitats
- **River pollution** comes from plastic waste, factory chemicals, household soap/detergent, and farm fertilisers
- Excess **fertilisers** cause **algal blooms** -- thick green layers that block sunlight and kill aquatic life
- **Floods** happen when heavy monsoon rains make rivers overflow, damaging crops, roads, and homes
- **Trees prevent floods** by absorbing rainwater through their roots; deforestation worsens flooding
- **Droughts** occur when monsoon rains fail, leading to severe water shortages

## Important Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Tributary** | A smaller river or stream that flows into a larger river |
| **Delta** | A wide, flat area where a river splits into channels before meeting the sea |
| **Perennial river** | A river that carries water throughout the year |
| **Seasonal river** | A river that flows only during and shortly after the rainy season |
| **Reservoir** | A large body of stored water created by a dam |
| **Hydropower** | Electricity generated by the force of flowing water through dams |
| **Algal bloom** | Rapid overgrowth of algae in water, caused by excess fertilisers |
| **Namami Gange** | National programme to clean the Ganga river |

## Remember These

- There are over **900 dams** on the Godavari river alone
- The **Hirakud Dam** (on Mahanadi, Odisha) is over **25 km long** -- one of the longest earthen dams in the world
- Clear-looking river water **can still contain** harmful invisible chemicals and bacteria
- The **Ganges river dolphin** (India's national aquatic animal) is **blind** and navigates using echolocation (natural sonar)
- In 2019, **Chennai** needed special water trains; in 2022, **Bengaluru** faced severe urban flooding
