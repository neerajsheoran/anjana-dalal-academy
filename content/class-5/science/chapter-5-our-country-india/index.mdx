Republic Day - 26 January

Some days in our country are special and are celebrated with joy and pride. **Republic Day** is such important day.

![](Screenshot%202026-03-27%20143909.png)

**26 January** is celebrated as **Republic Day** every year. On this day in 1950, the **Constitution of India** came into effect. It marks the day when India became a republic and began to be governed by its own laws.
