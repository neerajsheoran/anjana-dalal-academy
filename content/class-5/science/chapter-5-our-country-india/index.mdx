## Republic Day - 26 January

Some days in our country are special and are celebrated with joy and pride. **Republic Day** is such important day.

![](Screenshot%202026-03-27%20143909.png)

**26 January** is celebrated as **Republic Day** every year. On this day in 1950, the **Constitution of India** came into effect. It marks the day when India became a republic and began to be governed by its own laws.

---

## Constitution of India

The **Constitution of India** is a set of rules and laws that guide our country. It explains the rights and duties of citizens and helps the government run the country fairly and smoothly.

| **Dr. B. R. Ambedkar** helped to write the Constitution. | ![](Screenshot%202026-03-27%20144334.png) |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |

**Freedom Struggle and Rules for a Country**

After gaining freedom on **15 August 1947**, India needed rules to govern itself. The Constitution provides these rules and helps maintain peace, justice, and equality in the country.

---

## Republic Day Parade

The **Republic Day Parade** is held every year on **26 January at New Delhi**. It is a grand celebration that shows the strength, unity, and diversity of India.

The parade takes place along **Kartavya Path** (formerly Rajpath). The President of India takes the salute, and people from all over the country watch the parade with pride.
