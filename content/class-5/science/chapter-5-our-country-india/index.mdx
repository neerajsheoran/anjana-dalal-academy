## Republic Day - 26 January

Some days in our country are special and are celebrated with joy and pride. **Republic Day** is such important day.

![](Screenshot%202026-03-27%20143909.png)

**26 January** is celebrated as **Republic Day** every year. On this day in 1950, the **Constitution of India** came into effect. It marks the day when India became a republic and began to be governed by its own laws.

---

## Constitution of India

The **Constitution of India** is a set of rules and laws that guide our country. It explains the rights and duties of citizens and helps the government run the country fairly and smoothly.

| **Dr. B. R. Ambedkar** helped to write the Constitution. | ![](Screenshot%202026-03-27%20144334.png) |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |

**Freedom Struggle and Rules for a Country**

After gaining freedom on **15 August 1947**, India needed rules to govern itself. The Constitution provides these rules and helps maintain peace, justice, and equality in the country.

## Republic Day Parade

The **Republic Day Parade** is held every year on **26 January at New Delhi**. It is a grand celebration that shows the strength, unity, and diversity of India.

The parade takes place along **Kartavya Path** (formerly Rajpath). The President of India takes the salute, and people from all over the country watch the parade with pride.

![](Screenshot%202026-03-27%20144515.png)

## Republic Day Celebration at School

Republic Day is celebrated on 26 January every year. On this day, schools organise assemblies, speeches, cultural programmes, and parades. Students take part in activities to remember the importance of our Constitution.

The National Flag has **three colours**:&#x20;

* **Saffron** stands for courage and sacrifice.
* **White** stands for peace and truth.
* **Green** stands for growth and prosperity.

The **Ashoka Chakra** in the centre shows the importance of justice and progress.

## National Symbols

National symbols represent the **identity, values, and pride** of our country. They remind us of our history and unite us as Indians.

**National symbols help us**:

* feel proud of our country,
* remember our history and values,
* stay united as one nation.

### National Emblem (Lion Capital)

The **National Emblem of India** is the **Lion Capital** **of Ashoka**. It has four lions standing back to back, showing **power, courage, and confidence**.

The words “**Satyameva Jayate**” below the emblem mean *Truth Alone Triumphs*.

## State Symbols

Symbols help represent the **identity and culture** of a place. Our country India has both **national symbols** and **state symbols**.

Each state in India has its own **animal, bird, tree, and flower**. These symbols show the **natural life and culture** of that state and help people learn about its environment.

---

## Indian Currency Notes

Indian currency notes show many features that represent the **culture, history, and diversity of India.** All Indian currency notes have the image of **Mahatma Gandhi**. He is remembered as the **Father of the Nation** and stands for **truth, peace**, and **non-violence**.

Indian currency notes show many features that represent the **culture, history, and diversity of India.**&#x20;

Currency notes display:

* **Languages** from different parts of India, showing our unity in diversity.
* **Monuments** like historical buildings, showing India’s rich heritage.
* **Symbols and animals**, which reflect the country’s culture and natural life.
