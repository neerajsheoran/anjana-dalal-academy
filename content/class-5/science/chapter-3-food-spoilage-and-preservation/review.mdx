## Key Concepts

- Food spoils because of **microbes** -- tiny living creatures invisible to the naked eye, visible only under a **microscope**
- Microbes are found everywhere: air, soil, water, and even inside our bodies
- **Food spoilage** occurs when microbes land on food, feed on it, and change its colour, smell, and taste
- Microbes need three things to grow: **moisture, air, and warmth**
- **Food preservation** = removing any one of these three conditions to stop microbe growth
- **Drying/Dehydration**: removes moisture (chillies, papads, aam papad, dried fish)
- **Pickling/Oiling**: oil layer blocks air from reaching food (mango pickle, lime pickle)
- **Refrigeration/Freezing**: low temperature slows down microbe growth
- **Salting**: draws moisture out (salted fish, salted raw mango)
- **Adding sugar**: pulls water away from microbes (jams, murabbas)
- **Canning**: food sealed in airtight containers with no air (tinned juices, canned vegetables)
- **Roasting**: removes moisture through dry heat (khakhra, roasted chana)
- **Good microbes** help make **curd** (bacteria in warm milk), **idli/dosa batter** (fermentation), and aid **digestion**
- We have **four types of teeth**: incisors (cut), canines (tear), premolars (crush), molars (grind)
- **Saliva** starts breaking down food in the mouth before it reaches the stomach
- **Seasonal fruits** taste best in their natural season and are fresher when locally grown
- Dental care: brush twice daily, rinse after meals, limit sweets, visit dentist every 6 months

## Important Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Microbes** | Extremely tiny living organisms visible only under a microscope |
| **Food spoilage** | The process of food going bad due to microbe activity |
| **Food preservation** | Methods used to keep food fresh longer by stopping microbe growth |
| **Fermentation** | Process where microbes break down food, producing gas or changing texture (e.g., curd, idli batter) |
| **Dehydration** | Removing water/moisture from food to prevent spoilage |
| **Incisors** | Flat front teeth that bite and cut food |
| **Canines** | Sharp pointed teeth that tear food |
| **Premolars** | Wider teeth that crush food into smaller bits |
| **Molars** | Big flat back teeth that grind food into paste |
| **Cavity** | A tiny hole in a tooth caused by bacteria eating away at it |

## Remember These

- **Black pepper** was once called **"black gold"** -- Vasco da Gama sailed to India's Malabar coast to find it
- **Matkas** (clay pots) cool water naturally through evaporation -- no electricity needed
- **Pakhala** (Odisha) and **neeragaram** (Tamil Nadu) are traditional fermented rice dishes full of good bacteria
- India is the world's largest producer of **millets** and **pulses (dal)**
- Pickle stays fresh for months because oil blocks air, salt removes moisture, and spices slow microbe growth
