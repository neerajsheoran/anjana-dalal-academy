## What Makes School a Happy Place?

![What makes school a happy place — clean, safe, green, and full of kindness](what-makes-school-a-happy-place.png)

School is so much more than textbooks and exams. It is where you discover new ideas, build friendships, play your favourite games, and grow into the person you want to be. But a truly happy school needs to be **clean, safe, green, and full of kindness** -- for students, teachers, and every living thing on the campus.

Two enthusiastic students, **Priya and Arjun**, had a dream: they wanted to turn their school into a **"green school."** But what exactly is a green school?

> A **green school** is one where everyone -- students, teachers, and staff -- works as a team to **handle waste responsibly, conserve water, use electricity wisely**, and make the campus full of **trees, birds, and butterflies**.

## The Swachh Bharat Swachh Vidyalaya Mission

![The Swachh Bharat Swachh Vidyalaya Mission — clean toilets, safe water, hygiene](the-swachh-bharat-swachh-vidyalaya-mission.png)

The Indian government launched the **Swachh Bharat Swachh Vidyalaya** (Clean India Clean Schools) programme to make schools across the country better places to learn. It focuses on:
- Providing **clean, working toilets** in every school
- Ensuring access to **safe drinking water**
- Setting up proper **waste collection and disposal**
- Teaching students healthy **hygiene habits**

> Every child deserves to study in a **clean, healthy environment**!

> **Inspiring example:** The village of **Mawlynnong in Meghalaya** has earned the title of **"Asia's Cleanest Village."** Every resident -- young and old -- takes pride in keeping their surroundings spotless!

## Waste Management

Every day, we generate waste. But throwing everything into one bin is not the answer. The smart approach is **separating waste** into different types so each can be handled properly:

![Green bin for wet waste and blue bin for dry waste with examples of items for each](waste-management.png)

| **Type** | **Bin Colour** | **Examples** |
|---|---|---|
| **Wet waste** | Green bin | Fruit peels, leftover food, tea leaves, vegetable scraps |
| **Dry waste** | Blue bin | Paper, empty plastic bottles, candy wrappers, cardboard |

**What can be recycled?**

![What can be recycled — paper, metal cans, glass bottles, plastic containers](waste-management-what-can-be-recycled.png)

Many items we toss away can actually get a **second life through recycling**. Old newspapers, metal cans, glass bottles, and plastic containers are often collected by **recyclers** (sometimes called kabadiwalas in India). They send these materials to processing units where they are transformed into brand new products -- an old newspaper could become a fresh notebook!

> **A reminder:** We should always appreciate and respect the work of **sanitation workers and recyclers**. Without their effort, our streets, schools, and neighbourhoods would be buried in waste.

**What about wet waste?**

![What about wet waste — composting fruit peels and food scraps](waste-management-what-about-wet-waste.png)

Wet waste like fruit peels, vegetable scraps, and leftover food can be converted into **compost** -- a rich, natural fertiliser that helps plants grow beautifully. Many schools set up a **compost pit** on campus where wet waste is collected and allowed to decompose naturally over a few weeks.

## Keeping Cool Without Wasting Energy

Have you noticed that some classrooms feel pleasant and breezy while others feel stuffy and hot? Here are some clever ways to keep places cool:

- **Trees** cast shade and release moisture that naturally cools the air around them
- **Large windows** let in natural light and allow cross-breezes to flow through
- **Light-coloured roofs and walls** (white or cream) reflect sunlight instead of absorbing heat
- **Dark-coloured surfaces** soak up the sun's heat and make things warmer

> **Try this experiment:** On a sunny day, place a white cloth and a black cloth side by side in the sunlight. After 15 minutes, touch both. The white cloth will feel **noticeably cooler** because it reflects light, while the black one absorbs heat!

> **Traditional Indian cooling:** **Matkas** (earthen clay pots) have been used across India for centuries to cool drinking water naturally. Water seeps through the clay's tiny pores and evaporates on the outer surface, pulling heat away and chilling the water inside -- all without any electricity!

> **Mountain innovation:** In the cold heights of Ladakh, students help build **"ice stupas"** -- tall cone-shaped towers of ice that are created during winter. As summer arrives, these ice towers melt slowly, providing water for school gardens during the dry months!

## Saving Water at School

![Saving water at school — fixing taps, harvesting rain, keeping tanks clean](saving-water-at-school.png)

Water is one of our most valuable resources, and even a small dripping tap adds up to a huge amount of waste over time.

**Practical ways to save water at school:**
- **Report dripping or broken taps** to the maintenance staff right away
- **Turn off taps fully** after every use -- make sure they are not left half-open
- **Keep water storage tanks clean and covered** to prevent contamination
- **Collect rooftop rainwater** and channel it to the school garden
- **Avoid creating stagnant puddles** that can become breeding grounds for mosquitoes

> The **Jal Shakti Abhiyan** is a nationwide campaign encouraging everyone to harvest rainwater, plant trees near water sources, and use every drop of water wisely.

## Greenery Around Us

![Greenery around us — trees, shade, fruits, and homes for creatures](greenery-around-us.png)

Trees are some of the most important living things on a school campus:

- They clean the air by absorbing **carbon dioxide** and releasing fresh **oxygen** for us to breathe
- Their broad canopies provide **cool shade** on hot days
- They produce **fruits, flowers, and seeds** that attract birds, squirrels, and butterflies
- Their branches and trunks serve as **homes** for many creatures

> **Simple test:** Fill two identical bottles with water. Leave one under the open sun and the other under the shade of a big tree. After an hour, the water under the tree will be **noticeably cooler**. Trees genuinely lower the temperature around them!

## Traffic Safety Near School

![Traffic safety near school — zebra crossings, slow vehicles, helmets](traffic-safety-near-school.png)

The area around a school buzzes with activity -- children crossing roads, parents dropping off kids, auto-rickshaws and school buses moving about. Accidents can happen if we are not careful:

- Always use the **zebra crossing or pedestrian crossing** to cross the road
- **Look left, then right, then left again** before stepping onto the road
- Never dash or sprint across the road
- Vehicles should **slow down to a crawl** in school zones
- Anyone riding a two-wheeler must **wear a helmet** -- it is the law, and it saves lives

## Fire Safety at School

![Fire safety at school — exits, low crawling, assembly area](fire-safety-at-school.png)

Knowing what to do during a fire can save lives:

1. **Walk briskly toward the nearest exit** -- stay calm, do not push or run
2. **Stay low if there is smoke** -- crouch down or crawl, and cover your nose and mouth with a cloth. Cleaner air is found near the floor.
3. **Never hide** in cupboards, under desks, or inside bathrooms during a fire
4. **Help others** if it is safe to do so, especially younger children who may be frightened
5. Go to the designated **assembly area** and stay with your class group
6. **Immediately inform a teacher** if anyone is missing or hurt

> Every school should have **fire extinguishers** in accessible spots and a clearly displayed **emergency exit map**. Practising fire drills regularly makes sure everyone knows exactly what to do!

## Being Kind and Respectful

![Being kind and respectful — habits that make a school wonderful](being-kind-and-respectful.png)

A happy school is not built only with clean classrooms and green gardens. What truly makes a school special is how the people inside it **treat each other**.

**Habits that make a school a wonderful place:**
- **Wait patiently in line** instead of pushing and shoving
- **Use polite and encouraging words** with everyone -- classmates, teachers, and support staff
- **Welcome someone new** who might be feeling lonely or shy
- **Never tease or bully** anyone -- words can hurt just as much as actions
- **Keep shared spaces tidy** -- classrooms, the playground, library, and washrooms belong to everyone
- **Show respect to all** -- every person in the school, regardless of their role, deserves to be treated with dignity

> The way we behave tells the world what kind of person we are. When we choose kindness, **everyone around us feels happier and safer**.
