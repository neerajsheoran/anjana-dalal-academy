## Key Concepts

- A **green school** focuses on responsible waste handling, water conservation, wise electricity use, and greenery
- The **Swachh Bharat Swachh Vidyalaya** programme ensures clean toilets, safe drinking water, proper waste disposal, and hygiene habits in schools
- **Waste segregation**: separate waste into **wet waste** (green bin) and **dry waste** (blue bin)
- **Wet waste** (fruit peels, leftover food, vegetable scraps) can be turned into **compost** -- a natural fertiliser
- **Dry waste** (paper, plastic bottles, wrappers, cardboard) can be **recycled** through kabadiwalas/recyclers
- Trees cool the air, provide shade, absorb **carbon dioxide**, and release **oxygen**
- **Light-coloured surfaces** reflect sunlight and stay cooler; **dark-coloured surfaces** absorb heat
- Water conservation at school: report leaky taps, turn off taps fully, keep tanks clean and covered, collect rainwater
- **Jal Shakti Abhiyan** is a national campaign for rainwater harvesting and water conservation
- **Fire safety rules**: walk to nearest exit, stay low in smoke, never hide, go to assembly area
- **Traffic safety**: use zebra crossings, look left-right-left, never run across roads, wear helmets
- Positive school behaviour: be kind, wait in line, welcome newcomers, never bully, respect all staff

## Important Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Green school** | A school where everyone works to manage waste, conserve water, save energy, and maintain greenery |
| **Waste segregation** | Separating waste into different types (wet and dry) for proper disposal |
| **Wet waste** | Biodegradable waste like food scraps and vegetable peels |
| **Dry waste** | Non-biodegradable or recyclable waste like paper, plastic, and cardboard |
| **Compost** | Rich natural fertiliser made from decomposed wet waste |
| **Recycling** | Converting used materials into new products |
| **Swachh Bharat Swachh Vidyalaya** | Government programme for clean schools across India |

## Remember These

- **Mawlynnong, Meghalaya** is known as **"Asia's Cleanest Village"**
- **Matkas** (clay pots) cool water naturally through evaporation without electricity
- **Ice stupas** in Ladakh are cone-shaped ice towers built in winter that melt slowly to provide water in summer
- White cloth stays **noticeably cooler** than black cloth in sunlight because it reflects light
- Sanitation workers and recyclers deserve **appreciation and respect** for keeping our surroundings clean
