## School - Introduction

A school is a place where children come to **learn, play, and grow**. It provides knowledge, teaches good habits, and helps children develop skills. A school helps children feel **safe**, **cared for**, **and confident**, making it a happy place for learning.

## Green School

A green school is an eco-friendly school that take care of the environment by managing waste, saving water, using electricity carefully, and making the campus greener by planting and caring for trees.

To make our school a green school, we need to take care of certain important points.

### Saving Electricity

Electricity is an important resource that we use every day in our school. We must use it carefully and wisely.

* Lights and fans should be switched off when they are not in use.

Using sunlight and fresh air during the day helps save electricity.![](Screenshot%202026-03-27%20050748.png)

### Green and Healthy School Environment

Plants and trees help keep our school clean, cool, and healthy by giving fresh air, shade, and a pleasant environment.

* Planting more trees increases greenery and improves air quality.

![](Screenshot%202026-03-27%20050928.png)

Compost pits collect wet waste like food peels and leaves and turn it into manure, which is used to grow healthy plants and reduce waste.

![](image.png)

### Water Conservation

Water is a precious resource, and we must use it carefully to avoid wastage.&#x20;

* By stopping leaks and turning off taps, we can save a large amount of water.

  ![](image.png)
* Water tanks should be kept clean and covered to prevent dirt and insects from entering.

![](Screenshot%202026-03-27%20051257.png)

* Rainwater can be collected and stored for later use. This helps reduce the use of fresh water.

![](Screenshot%202026-03-27%20051349.png)

Waste Management

Waste management means handling waste properly to keep our surroundings clean and healthy. It helps reduce pollution and protects the environment.

* We should use only what we need and avoid wasting things.
* Some items can be used again instead of being thrown away.

  ![](image.png)

Waste should be separated into **wet waste**(food peels and leftovers) and **dry waste**(paper, plastic, and metal) and put into the **correct dustbins** to keep the surroundings clean and easy to manage.

![](Screenshot%202026-03-27%20051609.png)

### Traffic Safety Near School

Traffic rules help maintain order and prevent accidents near the school.

* Signboards near the school gate warn drivers to slow down and drive carefully.

  ![](Screenshot%202026-03-27%20051903.png)
* Pedestrian safety, such as using footpaths and zebra crossings, helps keep students safe.

  ![](Screenshot%202026-03-27%20051930.png)
