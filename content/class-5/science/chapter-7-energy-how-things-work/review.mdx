## Key Concepts

- **Energy** is the invisible force that makes things move, glow, heat up, cool down, and produce sound
- Forms of energy: **movement, light, sound, heat, electrical**
- **Food** gives our body energy -- skipping breakfast makes us sluggish; a balanced meal keeps us active
- **Fuels** (petrol, diesel, CNG, LPG) store energy that is released when burned
- Burning wood/coal produces harmful **smoke** -- LPG and electric stoves are cleaner alternatives
- Most of India's electricity comes from **burning coal**, which causes air pollution
- **Clean/renewable energy** sources: Sun (solar), wind, and water (hydro) -- they do not pollute
- **Solar energy** uses: drying clothes/food, solar panels for electricity, solar water heaters
- **Wind energy**: captured by **wind turbines** with spinning blades
- **Water energy**: dams generate **hydroelectricity**; traditional **gharaats** (water mills) grind grain
- **Energy efficiency** = getting the same result using less energy (e.g., **LED bulbs** vs old bulbs)
- **Batteries** store electrical energy for later use (torches, phones, remotes)
- **Vaastu Shastra** uses natural sunlight and breezes through room and window placement
- In Himachal/Uttarakhand/Ladakh, cattle on ground floor **warm upper rooms** naturally

## Important Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Energy** | The ability to make things move, glow, heat up, or produce sound |
| **Fuel** | A substance (petrol, diesel, LPG, wood) that stores energy and releases it when burned |
| **Solar energy** | Energy from the Sun, used for heating and electricity |
| **Wind energy** | Energy from moving air, captured using wind turbines |
| **Hydroelectricity** | Electricity generated by the force of flowing water through dams |
| **Clean energy** | Energy from Sun, wind, or water that does not cause air pollution |
| **Energy efficiency** | Getting the same work done while using less energy |
| **Battery** | A device that stores electrical energy for later use |

## Energy Sources Summary

| Source | Type of Energy | Example |
|--------|---------------|---------|
| Sun | Heat and Light | Solar panels, drying clothes |
| Wind | Movement | Wind turbines, sailing boats |
| Water | Movement | Hydroelectric dams, water mills |
| Food | Chemical | Body running, playing, thinking |
| Fuel | Heat and Movement | Vehicles, cooking |
| Electricity | Light, Heat, Sound, Movement | Fans, bulbs, fridges |

## Remember These

- In just **one hour**, the Sun sends more energy to Earth than all humans use in an **entire year**
- **Bhadla Solar Park** (Rajasthan) is among the world's biggest solar power stations
- **Cochin International Airport** (Kerala) was the world's first airport to run entirely on solar power
- **Gharaats** are traditional water mills in Himachal Pradesh and Uttarakhand used to grind grain
- Never touch electrical switches with **wet hands** and stay away from fallen wires
