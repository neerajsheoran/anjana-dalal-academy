## Key Concepts

- **70% of the human body** is made up of water -- every living creature depends on it
- Out of all water on Earth, only about **3% is freshwater**, and only a tiny fraction is easily usable
- **Freshwater sources**: rivers, lakes, ponds, underground water, glaciers, rainfall
- **Saltwater sources**: oceans and seas (not drinkable directly)
- Water exists in **three states**: liquid (tap water), solid (ice), and gas (water vapour/steam)
- The **water cycle** is nature's way of recycling water: evaporation, condensation, precipitation, collection
- **Evaporation**: sun heats water, turning it into invisible water vapour that rises
- **Condensation**: water vapour cools high up and forms tiny droplets that make **clouds**
- **Precipitation**: heavy clouds release water as rain, snow, or hail
- **Groundwater** forms when rainwater seeps through soil and collects deep underground
- People access groundwater using **wells, handpumps, borewells, and tube wells**
- Concrete and tar in cities **block rainwater** from reaching underground, causing groundwater depletion
- **Johads** (Rajasthan) and **check dams** (Hiware Bazar, Maharashtra) are traditional rainwater harvesting methods
- Rivers flow from **higher to lower ground** due to gravity
- Aquatic **food chains** link tiny plants to small fish to big fish to birds -- every link matters
- **Aquatic animals**: fish (gills, fins), frogs (amphibians), turtles, dragonflies, kingfishers
- **Aquatic plants**: free-floating (water hyacinth), fixed-floating (lotus), edge plants (reeds)
- Water plants have **waxy coatings** on leaves to prevent waterlogging

## Important Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Evaporation** | Water turning into water vapour due to heat from the Sun |
| **Condensation** | Water vapour cooling and forming tiny water droplets (clouds) |
| **Precipitation** | Water falling from clouds as rain, snow, or hail |
| **Groundwater** | Water stored deep underground in soil and rock layers |
| **Water cycle** | The continuous natural process of water moving between Earth and sky |
| **Food chain** | A linked chain showing who eats whom in an ecosystem |
| **Freshwater** | Water with no or very low salt content, suitable for drinking |
| **Aquatic animals** | Animals that live in water (fish, turtles, water snakes) |
| **Semi-aquatic animals** | Animals that live near water and depend on it (herons, dragonflies) |

## Remember These

- Only about **half a glass out of 100** represents the freshwater easily available to us
- The **Luni river** in Rajasthan never reaches any sea -- it disappears into the Rann of Kutch
- **Keibul Lamjao National Park** (Manipur) is the world's only floating national park, home to the rare **Sangai deer**
- The **Fishing Cat** has partially webbed feet and dives headfirst to catch fish
- Salt is harvested from seawater in the **Rann of Kutch**, Gujarat
