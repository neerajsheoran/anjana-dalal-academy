## Food Spoilage

Food spoilage means that **food goes bad and becomes unsafe to eat**. This happens because tiny living organisms called **microbes** grow on the food. When microbes grow, they change the **smell, colour, taste, and texture** of the food.

![](Screenshot%202026-03-26%20163308.png)

**How Food Changes When It Spoils**

When food spoils, several changes can be seen:

* It may become **soft and mushy.**
* It may feel **sticky or slimy.**
* It may turn **watery or dry.**
* Its **colour may change.**
* It may give a **bad smell.**

These changes show that the food is no longer fresh and should not be eaten.

### Microbes

Microbes are **tiny living organisms** that are so small we cannot see them with our eyes.

![](Screenshot%202026-03-26%20164000.png)

They can only be seen under a **microscope**.

![](Screenshot%202026-03-26%20164033.png)

Microbes cause many changes in food, such as:

* Bad smell
* Colour change
* Change in taste
* Food becoming sticky or mushy
* Fungus or coloured patches on the surface

### Why Food Spoils

Microbes need three things to grow:

1. **Air:** Microbes grow faster when food is kept open or not covered properly. Air helps them multiply quickly.
2. **Moisture (Water):** Wet or moist food spoils faster than dry food. Milk, cooked rice, fruits, and vegetables spoil quickly because they contain a lot of water.
3. **Warm Temperature:** Microbes grow best in warm places. That is why food spoils faster in summer than in winter.

![](Screenshot%202026-03-26%20170740.png)

### Useful Microbes

Not all microbes are harmful. Some are **helpful**:

* They help make **curd** from milk.
* Idli and dosa **batter rise** because good microbes help in fermentation.
* Our stomach has **good microbes** that help us digest food properly. They:
  * help break down food
  * keep our stomach healthy
  * protect us from harmful germs

That is why curd or buttermilk is given when someone has stomach problems.

## Food Preservation

Food preservation means keeping food safe and fresh for a longer time. Different methods are used to stop microbes from growing on food.

Some methods are:

1. **Drying**

In drying, water is removed from food by keeping it in the sun or air. Without moisture, microbes cannot grow.&#x20;

Examples:

![](Screenshot%202026-03-26%20171028.png)

2. **Adding Oil, Salt, and Sugar**

Oil, salt, and sugar prevent microbes from growing.

* Oil keeps air out
* Salt removes moisture
* Sugar prevents spoilage in jams and sweets

Examples:

![](Screenshot%202026-03-26%20171140.png)

3. **Refrigeration**

Keeping food in a refrigerator makes it cold.

Cold temperature slows down the growth of microbes.

Examples:

* Milk
* Cooked food
* Fruits and vegetables

![](Screenshot%202026-03-26%20171258.png)

4. **Airtight Packaging**

Food is stored in sealed containers or packets so air cannot enter. This helps food stay fresh for a longer time.

Examples:

![](Screenshot%202026-03-26%20171357.png)

5. **Fermentation**

In fermentation, good microbes are used to make food better. They help food rise and improve taste.

Examples:

![](Screenshot%202026-03-26%20171712.png)

## Indigestion

Indigestion is a stomach problem that makes us feel **uneasy, uncomfortable, or sick**. It may cause stomach pain, vomiting, or a feeling of heaviness.

![](Screenshot%202026-03-26%20171957.png)

**Bad eating habits can upset our stomach,** such as:

* Eating **too much or too fast.**
* Eating **very oily or junk food.**
* Eating **spoiled food.**
* Not chewing food properly.
* Eating at irregular times.

These habits make it difficult for the stomach to digest food properly, leading to indigestion.

![](Screenshot%202026-03-26%20172101.png)

**Healthy Eating Habits to Prevent Indigestion**

* Eat food slowly and chew well.
* Eat fresh and clean food.
* Avoid too much junk food.
* Eat meals at the right time.
