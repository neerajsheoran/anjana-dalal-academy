## Key Concepts

- From space, Earth looks like a **glowing blue ball** with no visible country borders -- one shared home
- **Group Captain Shubhanshu Shukla** was the first Indian to reach the International Space Station
- **Wing Commander Rakesh Sharma** was the first Indian in space; he described India as **"Saare Jahaan Se Achcha"**
- **Rosy Starlings** migrate from Russia/Mongolia to India every winter; they eat locusts and help Indian farmers
- **Yoga** has been practised in India for over **3,000 years**; now practised in nearly every country
- **21 June** is the **International Day of Yoga**, declared by the United Nations in 2014
- **Chillies** came to India from **South America** (Mexico) about 400-500 years ago via Portuguese sailors
- Before chillies, Indians used **black pepper** (kali mirch) for heat in food
- **Sugar** was India's gift to the world -- India first made jaggery and refined sugar from sugarcane
- **Marigold** flowers originated in **Mexico** but became central to Indian celebrations
- Indian cow breeds (**Gir, Kankrej, Ongole**) were taken to Brazil and now produce over 3/4 of Brazil's milk
- **"Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam"** = "The entire world is one family" -- ancient Indian wisdom
- **DIGIPIN** is a modern 10-character digital address code system for every location in India
- Earth is the **only planet** known to support life

## Important Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Migration** | Animals travelling long distances between regions, usually with changing seasons |
| **Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam** | Ancient Sanskrit phrase meaning "The entire world is one family" |
| **DIGIPIN** | A 10-character digital code that gives every location in India a unique address |
| **Trade routes** | Ancient paths over land and sea used to exchange goods between countries |

## Things That Came to India vs Things India Shared

| Came to India | From Where |
|---------------|-----------|
| Chillies, tomatoes, potatoes | Americas (Portuguese sailors) |
| Marigold flowers | Mexico |
| Paper | China |
| Dates and lemons | Arab traders |

| India Shared | What |
|-------------|------|
| Sugar and jaggery | Sugarcane processing |
| Yoga | Ancient body-mind practice |
| Cow breeds | Gir, Kankrej, Ongole to Brazil |
| Spices | Pepper, cardamom ("black gold") |
| Rice, mangoes, bananas | Food crops |
| Chess, Snakes & Ladders | Board games |
| Indigo dye | Deep blue dye |
| Ayurveda | Ancient healing system (3,000+ years) |
| Concept of zero | Transformed mathematics |

## Remember These

- Indian cow breeds appear on Brazilian **postage stamps and coins** -- that is how valuable they are to Brazil
- **Potatoes, tomatoes, peanuts, and cashew nuts** all originally came from the Americas, not India
- India invented **chess** and **Snakes & Ladders** -- board games now played worldwide
- The **concept of zero** was first written in India, changing mathematics forever
- **Ayurveda** is one of the oldest healing systems in the world, over 3,000 years old
