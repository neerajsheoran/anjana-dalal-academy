## Energy - Introduction

**Energy is the ability to do work**.

It helps us make things **move, produce light, make sound**, and **change temperature**.

For example:

| We use energy from food to **walk, run, and play**.           | ![](Screenshot%202026-03-28%20153829.png) |
| ------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| A bulb lights up because it gets **energy from electricity**. | ![](Screenshot%202026-03-28%20154045.png) |
| A radio produces **sound** using energy.                      | ![](Screenshot%202026-03-28%20154053.png) |

**Energy in Daily Life**

Energy is needed to do many activities in our everyday life.

* We use **energy for cooking** food on a gas stove or electric cooker.
* We need **energy to move**, such as walking, running, cycling, and playing.
* **Lighting** homes and streets needs energy from electricity.
* **Cooling** devices like fans, coolers, and refrigerators use energy.
* **Heating** devices like heaters and geysers use energy to keep us warm or heat water.

## Energy from Fuels
