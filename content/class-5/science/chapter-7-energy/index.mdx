## Energy - Introduction

**Energy is the ability to do work**.

It helps us make things **move, produce light, make sound**, and **change temperature**.

For example:

| We use energy from food to **walk, run, and play**.           | ![](Screenshot%202026-03-28%20153829.png) |
| ------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| A bulb lights up because it gets **energy from electricity**. | ![](Screenshot%202026-03-28%20154045.png) |
| A radio produces **sound** using energy.                      | ![](Screenshot%202026-03-28%20154053.png) |

**Energy in Daily Life**

Energy is needed to do many activities in our everyday life.

* We use **energy for cooking** food on a gas stove or electric cooker.
* We need **energy to move**, such as walking, running, cycling, and playing.
* **Lighting** homes and streets needs energy from electricity.
* **Cooling** devices like fans, coolers, and refrigerators use energy.
* **Heating** devices like heaters and geysers use energy to keep us warm or heat water.

## Clean Energy

Clean energy is energy that **does not pollute the environment**. It is obtained from **natural sources** like the **Sun, Wind,** and **Water**.

* Clean energy does not produce **smoke or harmful gases**, so it is safe for people and nature.
* Using clean energy helps **protect the Earth** and is good for our future.

  ---

## Energy Efficiency and Energy Saving

Energy efficiency and energy saving both help us use energy wisely and protect the environment.

**Energy efficiency** means using less energy to do the same work, such as using LED bulbs that give the same light but use less electricity.

**Energy saving** means reducing energy use by our daily habits, like switching off lights, fans, and appliances when they are not in use. When we save energy and use efficient devices, we reduce pollution and conserve natural resources.
