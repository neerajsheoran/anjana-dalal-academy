## Andaman and Nicobar Island

The **Andaman and Nicobar Islands** are a group of islands located in the **Bay of Bengal**. They are far away from the mainland of India and are surrounded by the sea on all sides.

![](Screenshot%202026-03-27%20171235.png)

### Natural Features

* These islands have **dense forests, green hills, long beaches, and clear blue sea water**.
* The climate is **hot, humid, and rainy**.
* The sea around the islands has **coral reefs**, which are homes to many sea animals and plants.

![](Screenshot%202026-03-27%20171531.png)

### Plants and Animals

* The forests of the Andaman and Nicobar Islands are rich in **biodiversity**, which means there are many types of plants and animals that live here.
* The sea is home to fish, turtles, corals, and other marine life.

![](Screenshot%202026-03-27%20171654.png)

### People and Lifestyle

* People living on the islands depend mainly on the **sea and forests** for their livelihood. **Fishing** is an important occupation.
* Some people grow crops, while others work in tourism.
* There are also **indigenous tribes** who live close to nature and follow traditional ways of life.

### Travel

Since the Andaman and Nicobar Islands are not connected by road, people can reach them only by aeroplane or ship.

![](Screenshot%202026-03-27%20171823.png)

### Protection of Nature

* The **Andaman and Nicobar Islands have a very delicate environment**. This means nature there can be easily harmed. The forests, animals, and coral reefs need special care and protection.
* Many areas are marked as **protected zones** to keep wildlife and sea life safe. People are advised not to cut trees, harm animals, or damage the sea.
* Protecting nature is important to keep the islands clean, safe, and beautiful.

![](Screenshot%202026-03-27%20171929.png)

## The Sundarbans

The **Sundarbans** is a large forest area located in the **delta of the rivers Ganga and Brahmaputra**, near the sea. It is one of the most unique natural regions of India.

![](Screenshot%202026-03-27%20172038.png)

### Natural Features

* The Sundarbans has **muddy land, many small rivers**, and **tall mangrove forests**.
* The area is affected by tides, and sea water enters and leaves the land regularly.
* The climate is **hot and humid**, with heavy rainfall.

![](Screenshot%202026-03-27%20172326.png)

### Plants and Animals

* Mangrove trees grow here. These trees have **special roots** that come out of the soil to breathe in muddy water.

![](Screenshot%202026-03-28%20141929.png)

* The Sundarbans is famous for animals like the **Royal Bengal Tiger**, deer, crocodiles, birds, and fish.

### People and Lifestyle

People living in the Sundarbans depend on **fishing, farming, and forest products**. Life is difficult because of floods, wild animals, and changing tides. People live carefully and respect nature.

### Travel

* The Sundarbans has many **rivers, creeks, and muddy land**, so people mostly travel by **boats**.
* Roads are limited in many areas.
* People move carefully in forest areas because of wild animals and follow forest rules to stay safe.

### Protection of Nature

* The Sundarbans is a **protected forest area**. Cutting trees and harming animals is not allowed.
* Protecting mangroves is important because they protect land from storms and floods.
* Mangrove trees absorb a lot of carbon dioxide and release oxygen, helping protect the environment.
