## Shapes in Our Kitchen

Look around your kitchen! You will find shapes hiding everywhere.

A steel **dabba** (container) looks like a cylinder. A **roti** is round like a circle. A **bread slice** looks like a square. An **ice cream cone** is shaped like a cone. And a round **lemon** looks like a sphere!

Even outside the kitchen, shapes are everywhere. A **cricket ball** is a sphere. A **brick** is a cuboid. A **dice** from your board game is a cube.

## Solid Shapes (3D Shapes)

The things around us are not flat like drawings. They have **thickness** -- you can hold them, turn them around, and feel every side. These are called **solid shapes** or **3D shapes** because they have three dimensions: **length**, **width**, and **height**.

Here are the five most common solid shapes:

| Solid Shape | What It Looks Like |
|-------------|-------------------|
| **Cube** | A dice, a sugar cube, a Rubik's cube |
| **Cuboid** | A brick, a shoebox, a tiffin box |
| **Cylinder** | A steel glass, a rolling pin, a candle |
| **Cone** | A birthday cap, an ice cream cone, a traffic cone |
| **Sphere** | A cricket ball, a marble, a globe |

![Five 3D shapes - cube, cuboid, cylinder, cone, and sphere - with labels and real-life examples](solid-shapes-3d.png)

## Faces, Edges, and Corners

Every solid shape has special parts:

- **Face** -- a flat or curved surface. Imagine painting each side of a shape -- each painted side is one face.
- **Edge** -- the line where two faces touch. Think of running your finger along the border between two sides.
- **Corner** -- the sharp point where edges come together. You can feel corners with your fingertip.

### Counting Faces, Edges, and Corners

| Shape | Faces | Edges | Corners |
|-------|-------|-------|---------|
| **Cube** | 6 | 12 | 8 |
| **Cuboid** | 6 | 12 | 8 |
| **Cone** | 2 (1 flat + 1 curved) | 1 | 1 |
| **Cylinder** | 3 (2 flat + 1 curved) | 2 | 0 |
| **Sphere** | 1 (curved) | 0 | 0 |

> **Tip:** A sphere has zero corners and zero edges! That is why a ball rolls freely in every direction.

## Corners -- Who Has How Many?

**Zero corners:** Sphere and cylinder -- their surfaces are smooth and round.

**One corner:** Cone -- it has a single pointy tip at the top.

**Eight corners:** Cube and cuboid -- they have corners at every spot where three edges meet.

> **Activity idea:** Without looking, put your hand in a bag and pick up an object. Can you guess its shape just by feeling the corners and edges? Try it with a ball, a box, and a birthday cap!

## Flat Shapes (2D Shapes)

If you press a solid shape onto wet paint and then stamp it on paper, the mark you get is a **flat shape**. These are called **2D shapes** because they have only **length** and **width** -- no height.

| If you stamp the face of... | You get this flat shape |
|-----------------------------|----------------------|
| A dice | **Square** |
| A tiffin box lid | **Rectangle** |
| A steel glass (the bottom) | **Circle** |
| A folded samosa wrapper | **Triangle** |

## Where Do Flat Shapes Hide?

Flat shapes are hiding everywhere in your world!

- **Circle** -- wall clock, one-rupee coin, bicycle wheel, steel plate, bangle
- **Rectangle** -- classroom door, notebook, TV screen, window
- **Square** -- floor tile, handkerchief, carrom board square, biscuit
- **Triangle** -- samosa, tiled rooftop, traffic yield sign, paper airplane nose

## Key Words to Remember

| Word | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Solid shape (3D)** | A shape with length, width, and height -- you can hold it |
| **Flat shape (2D)** | A shape drawn on paper with only length and width |
| **Face** | A flat or curved surface of a solid shape |
| **Edge** | The line where two faces of a solid shape meet |
| **Corner** | The point where two or more edges meet |
| **Cube** | A solid shape with 6 equal square faces |
| **Cuboid** | A box-shaped solid with 6 rectangular faces |
| **Cylinder** | A solid shape like a pipe or can with 2 circular faces |
| **Cone** | A solid shape with 1 circular face and 1 pointed tip |
| **Sphere** | A perfectly round solid shape like a ball |
