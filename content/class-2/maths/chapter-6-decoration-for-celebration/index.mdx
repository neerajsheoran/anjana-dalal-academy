## Getting Ready for a Festival

Ananya and her cousins are making paper flower chains to decorate their house for Diwali. Each chain holds **10 flowers**, and some extra loose flowers are left over.

This is exactly how numbers work! A chain of 10 flowers is like the **tens place**, and the loose flowers are like the **ones place**.

| Child | Chains (Tens) | Loose Flowers (Ones) | Total Flowers |
|-------|--------------|---------------------|---------------|
| Ananya | 2 | 5 | 25 |
| Vikram | 3 | 4 | 34 |
| Neha | 4 | 1 | 41 |
| Kabir | 5 | 6 | 56 |

## What is Addition?

When we combine two groups **into one**, we are doing **addition**. We use the **plus sign (+)** to show addition.

If Ananya has **25 flowers** and Vikram has **34 flowers**, how many do they have altogether?

```
  25  +  34  =  59
```

Together they have **59 flowers**!

## Addition of Two-Digit Numbers (Without Carrying)

When the ones digits add up to **less than 10**, the addition is straightforward.

**Step 1:** Add the **ones** first.
**Step 2:** Add the **tens** next.

### Example: 32 + 45

```
   T  O
   3  2
 + 4  5
 ------
   7  7
```

- Ones: 2 + 5 = 7
- Tens: 3 + 4 = 7
- Answer: **77**

### Example: 25 + 34

```
   T  O
   2  5
 + 3  4
 ------
   5  9
```

- Ones: 5 + 4 = 9
- Tens: 2 + 3 = 5
- Answer: **59**

## Adding on the Mala (Bead String)

You can use a mala (bead string) for addition too. To find **21 + 15**:

1. Start at bead **21**.
2. Slide **15 beads forward** (count 15 more).
3. You land on bead **36**.

So, 21 + 15 = **36**.

## Adding on the Number Line

A number line is a handy tool for addition.

To find **32 + 24** on a number line:

1. Start at **32**.
2. Take a big jump of **20 steps** forward to reach **52**.
3. Take **4 small steps** more to reach **56**.

So, 32 + 24 = **56**.

> **Tip:** When adding on a number line, jump by tens first, then by ones. It is much quicker!

## Addition of Two-Digit Numbers (With Carrying)

Sometimes, the ones digits add up to **10 or more**. Then we need to **carry** 1 ten over to the tens column.

### Example: 28 + 35

```
     T  O
     2  8
  +  3  5
  -------
     6  3
```

**Step 1:** Add the ones: 8 + 5 = **13**. That is 1 ten and 3 ones. Write **3** in the ones place. **Carry 1** to the tens place.

**Step 2:** Add the tens: 2 + 3 + 1 (carried) = **6**. Write **6** in the tens place.

**Answer: 63**

![Step-by-step visual showing carrying in addition - ones column adding to more than 10](addition-with-carrying-visual.png)

### Example: 47 + 38

```
     T  O
     4  7
  +  3  8
  -------
     8  5
```

- Ones: 7 + 8 = 15. Write 5, carry 1.
- Tens: 4 + 3 + 1 = 8.
- Answer: **85**

### Example: 55 + 25

```
     T  O
     5  5
  +  2  5
  -------
     8  0
```

- Ones: 5 + 5 = 10. Write 0, carry 1.
- Tens: 5 + 2 + 1 = 8.
- Answer: **80**

> **Remember:** When the ones add up to 10 or more, we "carry" the extra ten to the tens column. Think of it like making a new flower chain when you collect 10 loose flowers!

## Addition with Blocks

Priya has **35 blocks** (3 tens and 5 ones).
Sahil has **27 blocks** (2 tens and 7 ones).

Together:
- Ones: 5 + 7 = 12 ones = 1 ten and 2 ones
- Tens: 3 + 2 + 1 = 6 tens

Total: **62 blocks**

## Addition in Real Life

Addition is useful every single day! Here are some examples:

- Deepa scored **38 marks** in one test and **45 marks** in another. Total marks = 38 + 45 = **83 marks**.
- Rahul had **15 colour pencils** and bought **23 more**. Total = 15 + 23 = **38 colour pencils**.
- Meera spent **48 rupees** on bangles and **37 rupees** on ribbons at the mela. Total = 48 + 37 = **85 rupees**.

## Subtraction of Two-Digit Numbers

**Subtraction** means taking something away from a group. We use the **minus sign (-)** to show subtraction.

A potter made **68 diyas** for Diwali and sold **35** of them. How many are left?

```
  68 - 35 = 33
```

He has **33 diyas** left.

## Subtracting on the Mala

To find **40 - 15** on the mala:

1. Start at bead **40**.
2. Slide **15 beads backward**.
3. You land on bead **25**.

So, 40 - 15 = **25**.

## Subtracting on the Number Line

To find **26 - 13** on the number line:

1. Start at **26**.
2. Jump **10 steps backward** to reach **16**.
3. Jump **3 more steps backward** to reach **13**.

So, 26 - 13 = **13**.

> **Tip:** When subtracting on a number line, jump back by tens first, then by ones.

## Subtracting Without Borrowing

When the ones digit on top is **bigger** than the ones digit below, subtraction is simple.

### Example: 57 - 24

```
   T  O
   5  7
 - 2  4
 ------
   3  3
```

- Ones: 7 - 4 = 3
- Tens: 5 - 2 = 3
- Answer: **33**

## Subtracting With Borrowing

Sometimes, the ones digit on top is **smaller** than the ones digit below. Then we need to **borrow** 1 ten from the tens place.

### Example: 52 - 27

```
     T  O
     5  2    -->   4  12  (borrow 1 ten = 10 ones)
  -  2  7         -2   7
  -------         ------
     2  5          2   5
```

**Step 1:** We cannot take 7 away from 2. So we borrow 1 ten from the tens place. Now we have **4 tens** and **12 ones**.

**Step 2:** Ones: 12 - 7 = **5**.

**Step 3:** Tens: 4 - 2 = **2**.

**Answer: 25**

![Step-by-step visual showing borrowing in subtraction - opening a garland of 10 to get more ones](subtraction-with-borrowing-visual.png)

> **Think of it this way:** Neha has flower chains. She needs to give away 7 loose flowers, but she only has 2. So she opens 1 chain (1 ten) to get 10 more loose flowers. Now she has 12 loose flowers and can give away 7.

### Example: 63 - 38

```
     T  O
     6  3    -->   5  13
  -  3  8         -3   8
  -------         ------
     2  5          2   5
```

- Borrow: 6 tens becomes 5 tens, 3 ones becomes 13 ones.
- Ones: 13 - 8 = 5
- Tens: 5 - 3 = 2
- Answer: **25**

## Special Subtraction Facts

- When you subtract a number from **itself**, the answer is always **0**.
  - Example: 44 - 44 = **0**

- When you subtract **0** from a number, the number stays the same.
  - Example: 29 - 0 = **29**

## Addition and Subtraction Are Related

Addition and subtraction belong to the same **family**! They are connected to each other.

If we know that **15 + 25 = 40**, then we also know:
- 40 - 25 = 15
- 40 - 15 = 25

This is called a **fact family**.

| Fact Family for 15, 25, 40 |
|----------------------------|
| 15 + 25 = 40 |
| 25 + 15 = 40 |
| 40 - 25 = 15 |
| 40 - 15 = 25 |

## Key Words to Remember

| Word | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Addition** | Combining two or more numbers to find the total |
| **Plus (+)** | The sign used for addition |
| **Sum / Total** | The result you get after adding |
| **Subtraction** | Taking one number away from another |
| **Minus (-)** | The sign used for subtraction |
| **Difference** | The result you get after subtracting |
| **Carry** | Moving extra ones to the tens place when ones add up to 10 or more |
| **Borrow** | Taking 1 ten from the tens place to make more ones when subtracting |
| **Fact family** | A set of related addition and subtraction facts using the same three numbers |
