## The Wonder of Shadows

Have you ever noticed your shadow walking beside you on a sunny day? In Rajasthan, there is a traditional art called **Kathputli** where artists use puppets to tell stories. When light shines on these puppets, their shadows dance on a white screen!

You can make shadow puppets too! Hold your hands between a torch and a wall. Curl your fingers to create shapes of a **butterfly**, a **crocodile**, a **peacock**, or an **elephant**.

> **Fun Fact:** Shadows appear when something blocks the path of light. The object stops the light, and a dark shape forms on the other side -- that is the shadow!

## Shadows and Tracing

Your shadow does not stay the same throughout the day!

- In the **morning**, the sun hangs low in the sky, and your shadow stretches out very **long**.
- At **noon**, the sun climbs right above your head, and your shadow shrinks very **short**.
- In the **evening**, the sun sinks low again, and your shadow grows **long** once more.

If you hold a torch very close to a toy, its shadow becomes **bigger**. Move the torch farther away, and the shadow becomes **smaller**.

### Tracing Shapes

When you place an object flat on paper and draw a line around its outline, you are **tracing** its shape.

| Object | Shape You See After Tracing |
|--------|-----------------------------|
| One-rupee coin | Circle |
| Eraser | Rectangle |
| Bangle | Circle |
| Notebook cover | Rectangle |
| Neem leaf | Curved shape |

> **Try this:** Gather objects from around the house -- a coin, an eraser, a lid, a leaf. Place them on paper, trace around them, and see what shapes appear!

## Flat Shapes (Circle, Rectangle, Square, Triangle)

When we trace objects or look at their shadows, we discover flat shapes. Here are the four main ones:

### Circle
- A circle is perfectly **round** with no straight sides.
- It has **no corners** and **no straight edges**.
- You can spot circles in bangles, coins, wheels, lids, and plates.

### Rectangle
- A rectangle has **4 sides** and **4 corners**.
- Two sides are **longer** and two sides are **shorter**.
- Look for rectangles in doors, books, phones, and windows.

### Square
- A square has **4 sides** and **4 corners**.
- All four sides are **exactly equal** in length.
- Find squares in floor tiles, carrom boards, and handkerchiefs.

### Triangle
- A triangle has **3 sides** and **3 corners**.
- Spot triangles in samosas, tent rooftops, and traffic warning signs.

| Shape | Sides | Corners |
|-------|-------|---------|
| **Circle** | 0 (curved) | 0 |
| **Triangle** | 3 | 3 |
| **Square** | 4 | 4 |
| **Rectangle** | 4 | 4 |

## Shapes Hide Everywhere!

Shapes love to play hide-and-seek! Can you spot them?

- **Circle** hides in a steering wheel, a clock, a plate, and a one-rupee coin.
- **Rectangle** hides in a photo frame, a TV, a chocolate bar wrapper, and a blackboard.
- **Triangle** hides in a samosa, a sandesh slice, a boat sail, and a warning road sign.
- **Square** hides in a chessboard square, a Ludo die face, a napkin, and a small window pane.

## Corners and Edges in Flat Shapes

Some shapes have **straight edges** and some have **curved edges**.

- Objects with **curved edges** (like a circle) have **no corners**.
- A **triangle** has 3 straight edges and 3 corners.
- A **square** and a **rectangle** both have 4 straight edges and 4 corners.

Some objects have **both** straight and curved edges. For example, the shape of a half-moon has one straight edge and one curved edge.

## Patterns with Shapes and Numbers

You can build beautiful **patterns** by repeating shapes in a set order. A pattern is something that follows a rule and repeats.

### Shape Patterns

```
Triangle, Circle, Triangle, Circle, Triangle, Circle ...
Square, Square, Triangle, Square, Square, Triangle ...
```

### Colour Patterns

You can also use colours to make patterns:
```
Yellow circle, Green circle, Yellow circle, Green circle ...
```

### Rangoli Patterns

During **Diwali**, families draw **rangoli** at their doorsteps using coloured powder. Rangolis use repeating shapes like circles, petals, and dots arranged in beautiful patterns. This is a wonderful example of patterns from Indian tradition!

![Shape and colour patterns repeating in a sequence - circles and squares alternating](repeating-shape-colour-patterns.png)

### Number Patterns

Patterns are not only about shapes. Numbers can follow patterns too!

| Pattern | Rule |
|---------|------|
| 4, 8, 12, 16, 20 | Add 4 each time |
| 6, 12, 18, 24, 30 | Add 6 each time |
| 2, 8, 14, 20, 26 | Add 6 each time |
| 3, 7, 11, 15, 19 | Add 4 each time |

### Jumping Patterns

Imagine Chintu the squirrel. He starts on step 2 and hops to step 5. That means he jumps 3 steps at a time.

```
2, 5, 8, 11, 14, 17, 20, 23, 26, 29
```

His friend Motu the frog jumps 4 steps at a time starting from step 1:

```
1, 5, 9, 13, 17, 21, 25
```

### Letter Patterns

Letters can form patterns too:

```
AB, CD, EF, GH, IJ ...
AA, BB, CC, DD, EE ...
```

> **Tip:** To spot a pattern, look at what changes each time. Ask yourself: "What number or shape is being added? What is being repeated?"

## Key Words to Remember

| Word | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Shadow** | A dark shape that forms when an object blocks light |
| **Tracing** | Drawing around the outline of an object to get its shape |
| **Circle** | A round flat shape with no corners |
| **Rectangle** | A flat shape with 4 sides and 4 corners, with two sides longer than the others |
| **Square** | A flat shape with 4 equal sides and 4 corners |
| **Triangle** | A flat shape with 3 sides and 3 corners |
| **Pattern** | Something that repeats in a regular and predictable way |
| **Corner** | The point where two edges of a shape meet |
| **Edge** | The side or boundary line of a shape |
| **Curved edge** | An edge that bends smoothly, like the edge of a circle |
