## Key Concepts

- A **fractal** is a shape where the same pattern repeats at progressively smaller scales (**self-similarity**)
- Natural fractals: cauliflower, broccoli, rivers/tributaries, snowflakes, blood vessels
- **Sierpinski Carpet:** start with a square, divide into 9 parts, remove the centre; repeat on each surviving square
- Sierpinski Carpet surviving squares at step n: **8^n**
- **Sierpinski Triangle:** start with equilateral triangle, connect midpoints, remove centre triangle; repeat
- Sierpinski Triangle surviving triangles at step n: **3^n**
- **Koch Snowflake:** start with equilateral triangle, add a bump on each side's middle third; repeat
- Koch Snowflake sides at step n: **3 x 4^n**; boundary grows infinitely but area stays finite
- **Faces** = flat surfaces, **Edges** = boundary lines, **Vertices** = corner points of a solid
- **Euler's formula:** V - E + F = 2 for any convex polyhedron
- Prism with n-sided base: F = n+2, E = 3n, V = 2n
- Pyramid with n-sided base: F = n+1, E = 2n, V = n+1
- A **net** is a flat layout that folds into a 3D solid
- A cube has **11 distinct nets**; a sphere has **no net**
- **Shortest path** on a cuboid surface: unfold to a net and draw a straight line

## Important Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Fractal | A geometric figure with self-similarity at every scale |
| Self-similarity | The property of looking the same when zoomed in |
| Face | A flat polygonal surface of a solid |
| Edge | A line segment where two faces meet |
| Vertex | A corner point where edges converge |
| Net | A flat arrangement of shapes that folds into a solid |
| Euler's formula | V - E + F = 2 for any convex polyhedron |

## Remember These

- Verify Euler's formula for any solid: V - E + F should always equal **2** (e.g., cube: 8 - 12 + 6 = 2)
- The Koch Snowflake's boundary grows infinitely long but encloses a **finite** area -- a fractal paradox
- A sphere cannot be flattened into a net without distortion (no perfect flat map of Earth exists)
- Fractal counts grow as **powers** (8^n, 3^n, 4^n) -- connecting fractals to exponents
