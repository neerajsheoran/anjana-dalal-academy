## Key Concepts

- A **quadrilateral** is a closed figure with exactly four straight sides
- The sum of angles in any quadrilateral is always **360 degrees**
- A **rectangle** has all angles 90°, opposite sides equal and parallel, diagonals equal and bisecting each other
- A **square** is a rectangle with all four sides equal; diagonals bisect each other at 90° and bisect vertex angles
- Every square is a rectangle, but not every rectangle is a square
- A **parallelogram** has both pairs of opposite sides parallel and equal
- In a parallelogram, opposite angles are equal and adjacent angles are supplementary (sum to 180°)
- Parallelogram diagonals bisect each other but are NOT necessarily equal
- A **rhombus** has all four sides equal; it is a special parallelogram
- Rhombus diagonals bisect each other at **90 degrees** and bisect the vertex angles
- Every square is both a rectangle and a rhombus; the square sits at the intersection
- A **trapezium** has exactly one pair of parallel sides (called bases)
- A **kite** has two pairs of consecutive equal sides; its diagonals are perpendicular
- Two equal-length strips joined at their midpoints always form a **rectangle**
- For a **square**, the strips must also be perpendicular to each other

## Important Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Quadrilateral | A closed figure bounded by exactly four straight sides |
| Rectangle | Quadrilateral with all angles 90° |
| Square | Rectangle with all four sides equal |
| Parallelogram | Quadrilateral with both pairs of opposite sides parallel |
| Rhombus | Quadrilateral with all four sides equal |
| Trapezium | Quadrilateral with exactly one pair of parallel sides |
| Kite | Quadrilateral with two pairs of consecutive equal sides |
| Diagonal | A line segment connecting two non-adjacent vertices |

## Properties Comparison

| Property | Rectangle | Square | Parallelogram | Rhombus |
|----------|-----------|--------|---------------|---------|
| All angles 90° | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| All sides equal | No | Yes | No | Yes |
| Opposite sides equal & parallel | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Diagonals equal | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Diagonals bisect each other | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Diagonals perpendicular | No | Yes | No | Yes |
| Diagonals bisect angles | No | Yes | No | Yes |

## Remember These

- If three angles of a quadrilateral are 90°, the fourth **must** also be 90° (since 90+90+90+x = 360)
- Parallelogram diagonals bisect each other but are NOT equal (unlike rectangle diagonals)
- The family tree: Square ⊂ Rectangle ⊂ Parallelogram, and Square ⊂ Rhombus ⊂ Parallelogram
- In a parallelogram, knowing just **one angle** gives you all four angles
