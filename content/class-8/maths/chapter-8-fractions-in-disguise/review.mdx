## Key Concepts

- **Per cent** means "per hundred" -- z% = z/100
- Percentages are fractions with denominator 100
- To convert a fraction to a percentage: **multiply by 100**
- To convert a percentage to a fraction: **write over 100 and simplify**
- To find z% of a quantity Q: **(z/100) x Q**
- Percentages create a **uniform base (100)** for easy comparison of fractions
- **Cost Price (CP):** price paid to buy; **Selling Price (SP):** price received when selling
- **Profit** = SP - CP (when SP > CP); **Loss** = CP - SP (when CP > SP)
- **Profit %** = (Profit / CP) x 100; **Loss %** = (Loss / CP) x 100
- SP = CP x (1 + Profit%/100) for profit; SP = CP x (1 - Loss%/100) for loss
- **Simple Interest (SI)** = (P x R x T) / 100
- Total Amount = Principal + SI
- **Marked Price (MP)** is the tag price; **Discount** = (Discount% / 100) x MP
- Selling Price after discount = MP - Discount
- **GST** is added to the selling price: Bill = SP + (GST% / 100) x SP
- 20% of a large quantity can exceed 30% of a smaller quantity -- the **whole** matters

## Important Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Percentage (%) | A fraction expressed out of 100 |
| Cost Price (CP) | The amount paid to purchase an item |
| Selling Price (SP) | The amount received on selling an item |
| Profit / Loss | Difference between SP and CP |
| Principal (P) | The original amount deposited or borrowed |
| Rate (R) | Annual percentage of interest charged |
| Simple Interest | Interest calculated only on the original principal each year |
| Marked Price (MP) | The price printed on the product tag (MRP) |
| Discount | Reduction offered on the marked price |
| GST | Goods and Services Tax added to the selling price |

## Key Formulas

| Formula | Use |
|---------|-----|
| z% of Q = (z/100) x Q | Finding percentage of a quantity |
| Profit % = (Profit/CP) x 100 | Calculating profit percentage |
| Loss % = (Loss/CP) x 100 | Calculating loss percentage |
| SI = (P x R x T) / 100 | Simple interest calculation |
| Discount = (Discount%/100) x MP | Finding discount amount |

## Remember These

- Profit and loss percentages are always calculated on **Cost Price**, not Selling Price
- With simple interest, the interest each year stays the **same** (does not compound)
- Percentages are relative -- always check what the "whole" is before comparing
- 42% = 0.42 as a decimal -- percentages connect directly to our decimal system
