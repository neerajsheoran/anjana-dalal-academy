## Key Concepts

- Every **odd number** can be written as the sum of two consecutive numbers (e.g., 11 = 5 + 6)
- **Parity** (even/odd nature): switching + to - or vice versa changes the result by an even amount, so parity is preserved
- For any 4 consecutive numbers with any arrangement of + and - signs, the result is always **even**
- Parity rules: even + even = even, odd + odd = even, even + odd = odd, even x anything = even
- Two even numbers sum to a multiple of 4 only when **both** are multiples of 4, or **neither** is
- If a divides M and a divides N, then a divides **M + N** and **M - N**
- If A is divisible by k, then A is divisible by every **factor** of k
- Numbers with remainder r when divided by n can be written as **nk + r**
- **Divisibility by 2:** last digit is even
- **Divisibility by 3:** digit sum divisible by 3
- **Divisibility by 4:** last two digits form a number divisible by 4
- **Divisibility by 5:** last digit is 0 or 5
- **Divisibility by 8:** last three digits form a number divisible by 8
- **Divisibility by 9:** digit sum divisible by 9
- **Divisibility by 10:** last digit is 0
- **Divisibility by 11:** alternating sum of digits (from units place: +, -, +, -...) is divisible by 11

## Important Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Parity | The even or odd nature of a number |
| Divisibility | A number is divisible by d if it leaves remainder 0 when divided by d |
| Digit sum | The sum of all digits of a number (used for tests of 3 and 9) |
| Alternating sum | Digits alternately added and subtracted from the right (used for test of 11) |
| Remainder | What is left over after division; numbers with same remainder form a group |

## Divisibility Rules at a Glance

| Divisor | What to Check |
|---------|---------------|
| 2 | Last digit: 0, 2, 4, 6, or 8 |
| 3 | Sum of all digits divisible by 3 |
| 4 | Last two digits divisible by 4 |
| 5 | Last digit: 0 or 5 |
| 6 | Divisible by both 2 and 3 |
| 8 | Last three digits divisible by 8 |
| 9 | Sum of all digits divisible by 9 |
| 10 | Last digit: 0 |
| 11 | Alternating digit sum divisible by 11 |

## Remember These

- Divisibility by 9 uses digit sum because every place value = (multiple of 9) + 1
- Divisibility by 11 uses alternating sum because place values alternate between +1 and -1 relative to multiples of 11
- "Divisible by all factors of k" is always true, but "divisible by all multiples of k" is only sometimes true
- You can keep adding digits repeatedly until you get a single digit -- that is the remainder when dividing by 9
