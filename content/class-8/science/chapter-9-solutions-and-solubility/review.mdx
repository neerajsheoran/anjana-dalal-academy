## Key Concepts

- A **solution** is a uniform mixture where components are evenly blended throughout
- **Solute** = substance that dissolves; **solvent** = substance that does the dissolving
- When two liquids mix, the one in **greater amount** is the solvent
- Air is a **gaseous solution** -- nitrogen is the solvent, other gases are solutes
- **Unsaturated solution**: can still dissolve more solute at that temperature
- **Saturated solution**: holds the maximum possible solute at a given temperature
- **Solubility** = maximum quantity of solute that dissolves in a fixed amount of solvent at a specific temperature
- **Concentration** describes how much solute is in a solution (dilute vs concentrated -- relative terms)
- For most solids: solubility **increases** with temperature
- For gases in liquids: solubility **decreases** with temperature (cold water holds more oxygen)
- **Density = Mass / Volume**; SI unit: **kg/m3** (also g/mL or g/cm3)
- Density is a property of the **material**, not the size/shape of the object
- Objects **float** if less dense than the liquid; **sink** if denser
- **Relative density** = substance's density / water's density (no units); >1 sinks, <1 floats
- Heating a substance generally **decreases** its density (particles move apart, volume increases)
- **Exception**: water is densest at **4°C**; ice at 0°C is less dense than liquid water, so ice floats
- Ice floating insulates water below, keeping it at ~4°C -- essential for aquatic life survival in winter
- **Gases** are significantly compressed by pressure (density increases); liquids and solids are barely affected
- Volume of irregular solids measured by **water displacement method**
- Read meniscus at the **bottom** for colourless liquids, **top** for coloured liquids
- Earth's layers (crust to inner core) are arranged by **increasing density**

## Important Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Solution** | A uniform mixture of solute dissolved in a solvent |
| **Solute** | The substance that dissolves in a solution |
| **Solvent** | The substance that does the dissolving |
| **Saturated solution** | A solution holding the maximum solute at a given temperature |
| **Unsaturated solution** | A solution that can still dissolve more solute |
| **Solubility** | Maximum amount of solute that dissolves in a fixed amount of solvent at a specific temperature |
| **Density** | Mass per unit volume (D = M/V) |
| **Relative density** | Ratio of a substance's density to water's density (unitless) |
| **Meniscus** | The curved surface of a liquid in a measuring cylinder |

## Key Formulas

| Formula | Units |
|---------|-------|
| **Density = Mass / Volume** | kg/m3 or g/cm3 |
| **Volume (regular solid) = Length x Breadth x Height** | cm3 or m3 |

## Remember These

- Solid solubility goes **UP** with temperature; gas solubility goes **DOWN** with temperature
- Water is densest at **4°C** -- ice floats because it is LESS dense than liquid water (exam favourite!)
- Hot air rises because it is **less dense** than surrounding cooler air (hot air balloons, sea breeze)
- Use the **water displacement method** for irregularly shaped solids -- difference in water level = volume
- When reading a measuring cylinder, keep your eyes at the **same level** as the liquid surface to avoid parallax error
