## Key Concepts

- Earth is the **only known planet** that supports life; all life exists on a thin outer layer (the crust)
- Eight planets in order: **Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune**
- Inner 4 planets are **rocky**; outer 4 are **gas/ice giants**
- **Venus** is hotter than Mercury despite being farther from the Sun -- due to runaway **greenhouse effect** (thick CO2 atmosphere)
- **Greenhouse effect** on Earth: moderate CO2 traps just enough heat to maintain ~15°C average temperature
- Earth sits in the **habitable zone (Goldilocks zone)** -- just the right distance from the Sun for **liquid water**
- ~70% of Earth's surface is water -- hence called the **Blue Planet**
- Earth's **nearly circular orbit** prevents extreme temperature swings
- Earth's **gravity** is strong enough to retain a thick atmosphere with **oxygen**
- **Ozone layer** absorbs harmful **UV radiation** from the Sun
- Earth's **magnetic field** (from molten iron in outer core) deflects cosmic rays and solar wind
- Three factors making Earth liveable: habitable zone position, suitable size/atmosphere, magnetic field
- Four interconnected systems: **atmosphere**, **hydrosphere** (all water), **geosphere** (rocks, soil, minerals), **biosphere** (all living things)
- **Reproduction** ensures continuity of life; introduces small **genetic variations** enabling adaptation
- **Asexual reproduction**: one parent, genetically identical offspring (vegetative propagation, binary fission, budding)
- Examples: tulsi from cuttings, potato from eyes, bacteria splitting, Hydra budding
- **Sexual reproduction**: two parents contribute **gametes** (half genetic material each) → unique offspring
- In plants: **pollen** (male) + **ovule** (female) → **pollination** → **fertilisation** → **zygote** → **seed** → **fruit**
- In animals: **sperm** (male) + **egg** (female); external fertilisation (fish, frogs) or internal (birds, mammals)
- **Triple planetary crisis**: climate change, biodiversity loss, pollution
- Burning fossil fuels releases excess greenhouse gases → **global warming** → melting glaciers, extreme weather, species loss
- Key agreements: **Montreal Protocol** (1987, ozone), **Earth Summit** (1992), **Kyoto Protocol** (2005), **Paris Agreement** (2015, limit warming to 1.5°C)
- ISRO missions: Mangalyaan (Mars), Chandrayaan (Moon), Aditya L1 (Sun)

## Important Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Habitable zone (Goldilocks zone)** | Region around a star where temperatures allow liquid water |
| **Greenhouse effect** | Atmospheric gases trap heat radiation, warming the planet's surface |
| **Ozone layer** | Layer of ozone (O3) that absorbs harmful UV radiation |
| **Atmosphere** | Blanket of gases surrounding Earth |
| **Hydrosphere** | All water on Earth (oceans, rivers, glaciers, groundwater) |
| **Geosphere** | Solid Earth -- rocks, soil, and minerals |
| **Biosphere** | All living organisms and their environments |
| **Asexual reproduction** | Reproduction by one parent; offspring are genetically identical |
| **Sexual reproduction** | Reproduction involving two parents and fusion of gametes; offspring are genetically unique |
| **Gamete** | Specialised reproductive cell carrying half the parent's genetic material |
| **Pollination** | Transfer of pollen from anther to another flower |
| **Fertilisation** | Fusion of male and female gametes to form a zygote |
| **Triple planetary crisis** | Climate change + biodiversity loss + pollution |

## Remember These

- Venus is hotter than Mercury because of the **greenhouse effect** -- a favourite exam question
- Three things that make Earth uniquely liveable: **habitable zone** + **right-sized atmosphere (with ozone)** + **magnetic field**
- Asexual = **identical** offspring from one parent; Sexual = **unique** offspring from two parents
- Pollination → Fertilisation → Zygote → Seed → Germination -- know this sequence
- **Paris Agreement (2015)**: goal to limit warming to below **1.5°C** -- most commonly asked agreement
