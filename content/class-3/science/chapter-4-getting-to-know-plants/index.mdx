## Types of Plants

![Types of plants — trees, shrubs, herbs, grasses, climbers, creepers](types-of-plants.png)

**Tara**, **Dhruv**, and **Neha** walk past a beautiful park every morning on their way to school. One day, their teacher tells them to look carefully at the plants around them.

They notice that plants come in so many sizes! Some are shorter than their knees, some reach their shoulders, and some are so tall they have to crane their necks to see the top. Some leaves feel smooth like butter, others feel rough like sandpaper. Plants like **tulsi**, **pudina** (mint), **dhaniya** (coriander), and **lemon grass** have a lovely smell when you crush their leaves gently.

> The world of plants is full of surprises! Let us learn about the different kinds of plants that grow around us.

**Trees** are the giants of the plant world. They have one thick, hard **trunk** made of wood, many spreading **branches**, and **roots** that reach deep into the earth.

![Trees are the giants of the plant world — thick trunk, branches, deep roots](trees.png)

Some well-known trees in India:

| Tree | What Is Special About It |
|---|---|
| **Mango** | Gives us the king of fruits, found all over India |
| **Neem** | Its leaves are used as natural medicine |
| **Peepal** | Often found near temples, gives a lot of fresh air |
| **Banyan** | Famous for its hanging roots that grow down from branches |
| **Coconut** | Grows in coastal areas, gives us coconut water and oil |
| **Jackfruit** | Bears the largest fruit that grows on a tree |
| **Khejri** | Survives in the hot deserts of Rajasthan |
| **Chinar** | The pride of Kashmir, with beautiful star-shaped leaves |
| **Amaltas** | Covered in golden yellow flowers during summer |

![Well-known trees of India — mango, neem, peepal, banyan, coconut and more](indian-trees.png)

**Shrubs** are medium-sized plants. They do not have one thick trunk. Instead, many **woody brown stems** branch out near the ground, making them look bushy.

Common shrubs:

- **Hibiscus** (gudhal) -- bright red trumpet-shaped flowers
- **Rose** -- the queen of flowers, comes in many colours
- **Tulsi** (Holy Basil) -- kept in almost every Indian home, used in prayers and as medicine
- **Curry leaf** -- its leaves give a wonderful aroma to South Indian dishes

![Shrubs — hibiscus, rose, tulsi, curry leaf](shrubs.png)

**Did you know?** The dal we eat every day comes from shrubs!

| Pulse (Dal) | Another Name |
|---|---|
| **Arhar** | Toor dal (pigeon peas) |
| **Masoor** | Red lentils |
| **Moong** | Green gram |
| **Urad** | Black gram |

![Dal from shrubs — arhar, masoor, moong, urad](dal-from-shrubs.png)

**Herbs** are the smallest plants with **soft, green stems** that never turn woody. They are much shorter than shrubs.

Some herbs you know:

- **Pudina** (Mint)
- **Tamatar** (Tomato)
- **Dhaniya** (Coriander)
- **Sarson** (Mustard)

![Herbs — pudina, tomato, dhaniya, sarson](herbs.png)

**Grasses** are a special group of herbs. Their leaves are **long and narrow**, and their stems are **hollow** inside.

> Here is something amazing: the grains we eat daily -- **chawal** (rice), **gehu** (wheat), **bajra**, **jowar**, and **ragi** -- are all seeds of tall grasses!

**Sugarcane** and **bamboo** are also grasses. Bamboo is the **tallest grass on Earth**!

![Grasses — long narrow leaves, hollow stems; includes rice, wheat, bamboo](grasses.png)

**Climbers** have thin, bendable stems and grow upward by holding on to walls, poles, or other plants:

- **Money plant** -- climbs up walls and poles
- **Chameli** (Jasmine) -- climbs fences with sweetly scented flowers

![Climbers — money plant, jasmine](climbers.png)

**Creepers** also have thin stems, but they spread along the ground instead of climbing:

- **Kaddu** (Pumpkin) -- spreads its vines on the soil
- **Tarbooz** (Watermelon) -- creeps along the ground
- **Lauki** (Bottle gourd) -- can creep or climb

![Creepers — pumpkin, watermelon, bottle gourd](creepers.png)

| Type | Direction of Growth | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Climber** | Upward, using support | Money plant, Jasmine, Snake gourd |
| **Creeper** | Sideways, along the ground | Pumpkin, Watermelon, Bottle gourd |

> Some plants can act as both climbers and creepers! If they find a wall or pole, they climb. If not, they spread along the ground.

## Parts of a Plant and Leaves

Every plant has important parts that help it live and grow:

![A labeled diagram of a plant showing roots, stem, leaves, flower, fruit, and seeds](parts-of-plant-labeled.png)

| Part | Its Job |
|---|---|
| **Roots** | Grip the soil and soak up water and nutrients from the ground |
| **Stem** | Acts like a pipeline, carrying water from roots up to the leaves; holds the plant upright |
| **Leaves** | The kitchen of the plant -- they use sunlight to cook food for the plant |
| **Flowers** | Help the plant make new baby plants (reproduction) |
| **Fruits** | Wrap around and protect the seeds inside |
| **Seeds** | Tiny packets of life -- they grow into new plants |

**Leaves come in amazing shapes and sizes:**

| Leaf | What Makes It Unique |
|---|---|
| **Peepal** | Shaped like a heart with a long pointed tip |
| **Mango** | Long and smooth; rub it gently and smell a lovely scent |
| **Lotus** | Big and round; water drops roll off like tiny silver balls |
| **Khejri** | Very small and feathery, like tiny green fingers |

![Different leaf shapes: heart-shaped peepal, long mango, round lotus, and feathery khejri](leaf-shapes-comparison.png)

Neha's brother cannot see, but he can tell which fruit is near him just by its **smell**! He can identify mango, guava, amla, and jamun without touching them. Our nose is a wonderful tool for knowing plants.

**Bark** is the tough outer skin of a tree trunk. Every tree has a different bark pattern. Press a piece of paper against the bark and rub a crayon over it -- you will get a beautiful print! This activity is called a **bark rubbing**. If you look closely at bark, you might spot **ants, beetles, tiny spiders, and even small plants** growing on it.

## Fun Facts about Indian Plants

- **Sugar** that we put in our chai comes from the stem of sugarcane.
- **Bamboo** looks like a tree but is actually the tallest grass in the world!
- **Rafflesia**, found in Mizoram, produces the **largest flower on Earth** -- it can be as wide as an open umbrella!
- The **banyan tree** in Kolkata's botanical garden is so big that it looks like an entire forest -- but it is just one tree!

## Key Points to Remember

- Plants are grouped into **trees, shrubs, herbs, grasses, climbers, and creepers** based on their size and stem type.
- **Trees** have a thick trunk and deep roots. **Shrubs** have many woody stems near the ground.
- **Herbs** have soft green stems. **Grasses** are herbs with hollow stems and narrow leaves.
- **Climbers** grow upward using support. **Creepers** spread along the ground.
- Every plant has **roots, stem, leaves, flowers, fruits, and seeds** -- each with a special job.
- Leaves differ in **shape, size, colour, texture, and smell** from plant to plant.
- Plants give us **food, shade, fresh air, medicine, wood, and much more**.
