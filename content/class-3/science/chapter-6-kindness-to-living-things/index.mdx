## Animals and Plants in Our Homes

Our homes are shared spaces -- not just for people, but for many creatures and plants too! Some we **invite in**, and some simply **show up on their own**.

**Creatures we welcome home:**

- **Dogs** and **cats** as loving pets
- **Cows** and **goats** kept for fresh milk
- **Parrots** and **fish** for company
- **Tulsi**, **aloe vera**, and other plants in our courtyard or balcony

**Uninvited guests that appear on their own:**

- **Chhipkali** (lizards) on the walls
- **Cheetiyan** (ants) marching across the kitchen floor
- **Makdi** (spiders) weaving webs in corners
- **Gilhari** (squirrels) sitting on the window grille
- **Kabutar** (pigeons) cooing on the balcony railing
- **Machhar** (mosquitoes) and **makkhiyan** (flies) buzzing around

> We should **never hurt any animal**, even the ones that annoy us. Most animals will not harm us unless they feel scared or trapped.

## How Plants and Animals Help Each Other

One afternoon, a boy named **Vivek** was walking through a grove of trees with his grandmother. They rested under the shade of a huge **aam ka ped** (mango tree). Birds chirped above, squirrels chased each other on the branches, and a monkey sat high up eating a ripe mango.

The monkey bit into the mango and tossed the seed far away. Vivek's grandmother smiled and said, "You see that seed? One day, a brand new mango tree will grow from it. This is how monkeys plant trees without even knowing!"

Vivek picked up a fallen mango. His grandmother explained, "We get so much from plants -- not just delicious fruits, but also medicines from neem and tulsi, fibre from cotton for our clothes, and wood for building our homes."

Plants provide us many treasures:

| What We Get | How It Helps Us |
|---|---|
| **Fruits and vegetables** | We eat them every day for nutrition |
| **Shade** | Trees cool us down on scorching summer days |
| **Wood** | Used to make furniture, houses, and cricket bats |
| **Medicine** | Neem, tulsi, haldi -- nature's pharmacy |
| **Fibre** | Cotton plants give us cloth for our clothes |
| **Fresh air** | Plants breathe out the oxygen we need to live |
| **Homes for animals** | Birds, insects, and animals live in and around trees |

And animals help plants in return:

- **Gilhariyan** (squirrels) bury nuts in the soil and forget where they hid them. Those nuts sprout into new trees!
- **Bandar** (monkeys) and **chidiya** (birds) eat fruits and drop seeds far from the parent tree. New plants grow from those seeds. This is called **seed dispersal**.
- **Keenchue** (earthworms) and tiny insects make the soil **fertile** (rich and healthy) through their droppings, helping plants grow stronger.
- **Madhumakkhiyan** (honeybees) and **titliyan** (butterflies) carry **pollen** from one flower to another, helping plants produce fruits and seeds.

> Thousands of trees are accidentally planted by squirrels every year! They hide nuts for later and then forget where they buried them.

![A bee carrying pollen from one flower to another, helping the plant](bee-pollination.png)

Plants, animals, and humans -- we all **depend on each other**:

| Who Helps Whom | How They Help |
|---|---|
| **People help animals** | By giving them food, water, and safe shelter |
| **People help plants** | By watering them, planting new trees, and protecting forests |
| **Animals help plants** | By spreading seeds and making soil fertile |
| **Plants help animals** | By giving food, shelter, shade, and clean air |
| **Plants help people** | By giving food, wood, medicine, fibre, and fresh oxygen |
| **Animals help people** | By giving milk, eggs, honey, wool, and warm companionship |

## The Grey Wagtails and Caring for Nature

**Make a Plant Friend!**

You can pick a plant near your home or school and make it your own special **plant buddy**:

1. **Choose** a tree, shrub, or pot plant near you.
2. **Give it a name** -- just like you would name a pet!
3. **Water** it regularly and pull out weeds around it.
4. **Watch** it closely -- notice its leaves, buds, flowers, and fruits change with the seasons.
5. **Sit near it** when you feel happy, sad, or worried -- spending time near plants can make you feel calm and peaceful.

> Your plant friend will never judge you. Whether you are happy or upset, you can always sit beside it and feel better!

**Busy, Busy Ants!**

Want to discover what ants like best? Try this fun experiment:

1. Place three small piles of food on the ground -- a pinch of **cheeni** (sugar), a bit of **papad**, and a small piece of **roti**.
2. Wait patiently and watch!
3. Notice which pile the ants visit first.
4. Count how many ants come and see how they carry the food.

Ants almost always rush to **sweet things** first!

**The Grey Wagtails of Valparai**

Here is a heartwarming true story from **Valparai**, a small hill town in **Tamil Nadu**. Every year, schoolchildren and their teachers eagerly watch for a special visitor -- a small bird called the **Grey Wagtail** (known as **Vaalatti Kuruvi** in Tamil).

During the chilly winter months, Grey Wagtails fly down from the cold mountains to the warmer towns of south India. When the very first Grey Wagtail is spotted in Valparai, the children and teachers **celebrate**! They put up colourful welcome posters in the school and hand out **mithai** (sweets) to everyone.

> These birds are called "Wagtails" because they are always **bobbing their tails** up and down, like a happy little wave!

## Key Points to Remember

- **Plants, animals, and people** are all connected and depend on one another.
- Plants give us **food, shelter, fresh air, medicine, wood, and fibre**.
- Animals return the favour by **spreading seeds** and keeping the **soil healthy**.
- We should be **gentle and kind** towards every animal and plant.
- We should **never hurt any creature**, even the uninvited ones in our homes.
- Adopting a **plant friend** is a lovely way to feel close to nature.
- **Living in harmony** means understanding that we need nature, and nature needs us.
