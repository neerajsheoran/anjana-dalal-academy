## Travelling to the Mela

A big **mela** (fair) has been set up near the river bank! **Kavya** and her brother **Rohan** can hardly wait to go. Their parents, **Amma** and **Baba**, decide to take the whole family -- including **Dadi** (grandmother).

Dadi walks slowly because her knees ache. Rohan holds her hand carefully as they walk to the bus stop. On the way, they stay on the footpath and look both ways before crossing the road.

**Bus number 312** arrives. Baba helps Dadi climb the steps. The conductor shows them the **reserved seat** for senior citizens, and Dadi sits comfortably. Baba buys the tickets:

| Ticket Type | Count | For Whom |
|---|---|---|
| **Full ticket** | 3 | Amma, Baba, Dadi |
| **Half ticket** | 2 | Kavya, Rohan |

**Important rules to follow on a bus:**

- Sit properly in your seat -- do not stand on it
- Keep your hands and head inside the bus at all times
- Do not push others while getting on or off

People travel in many different ways:

| Transport | When We Use It |
|---|---|
| **Bus** | Going around the city or to nearby towns |
| **Train** | Travelling far-away distances |
| **Auto-rickshaw** | Quick trips inside the city |
| **Bicycle** | Going short distances, like to a nearby shop |
| **Car** | Family outings and trips |
| **Boat** | Crossing rivers or travelling through backwaters |

> Before any trip, always carry a water bottle, stay close to your family, and be ready on time!

## Fun and Food at the Mela

When they reach the mela ground, it is buzzing with colour and sound! Giant **ferris wheels** spin in the sky. A **merry-go-round** plays cheerful music. Children hold cotton candy and laugh.

The children had a wonderful time exploring:

| Activity | What They Enjoyed |
|---|---|
| **Toy stalls** | Wind-up toys, wooden puppets, clay whistles, and colourful pinwheels |
| **Rides** | The ferris wheel and the merry-go-round |
| **Shopping** | Kavya picked glass bangles; Rohan chose a bamboo flute |
| **Puppet show** | A funny puppet show about a clever fox and a lazy lion |

After all the excitement, it was time to eat! But first, Amma made everyone **wash their hands** at the tap near the food stalls.

They sat down and enjoyed:

- **Chole bhature** (spicy chickpea curry with fluffy fried bread)
- **Aloo tikki** with tangy chutney
- **Gol gappe** (crispy hollow shells with spicy water)
- **Hot jalebis** dripping with syrup
- **Kulfi** on a stick (creamy Indian frozen treat)

After eating, Rohan put all the plates and cups into the **dustbin**. Keeping the mela clean is everyone's job!

## Safety and Helpers at the Mela

At the entrance of the mela, the family noticed a **map board** showing where all the stalls, rides, and food areas were. Nearby stood:

- An **ambulance** -- ready to help if anyone got hurt or felt unwell
- A **police van** -- with officers keeping everyone safe
- A **fire truck** -- in case of any fire emergency
- A **Lost and Found tent** -- with kind volunteers helping reunite lost children with their families

Baba quickly got a **wheelchair** for Dadi so she could move around the mela without tiring her knees.

| Helper at the Mela | What They Do |
|---|---|
| **Police officers** | Keep order and help with directions |
| **Sniffer dogs** | Help police detect any danger |
| **Firefighters** | Ready to put out fires with their fire truck |
| **First-aid workers** | Treat injuries and help sick visitors |
| **Volunteers** | Help lost children find their parents at the Lost and Found tent |

> The **Kumbh Mela** is one of the biggest gatherings of people on our planet! It takes place on the banks of sacred rivers like the **Ganga**, **Yamuna**, **Godavari**, and **Shipra**. It is held every 12 years at **Haridwar**, **Prayagraj**, **Nashik**, and **Ujjain**.

---

## Key Points to Remember

- A **mela** is a fair where families enjoy rides, food, shopping, and shows together.
- We should always **respect and help our elders** -- hold their hand while walking, get them a wheelchair if needed.
- Follow **safety rules on the bus** -- sit properly and keep hands inside.
- **Ambulances, fire trucks, and police** are present at a mela to keep everyone safe.
- The **Lost and Found tent** helps children who get separated from their families.
- Always **wash your hands** before eating and **throw waste in the dustbin**.
- Many hardworking people run the mela -- **toy sellers, cooks, puppeteers, bangle sellers, ride operators**, and more.
