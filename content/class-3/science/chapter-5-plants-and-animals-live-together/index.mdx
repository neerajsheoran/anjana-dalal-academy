## Animals and Plants Living Together

Have you ever sat quietly under a tree and watched what happens around you? If you stay still for just five minutes, you will be amazed!

A **squirrel** darts up the trunk. A **butterfly** lands softly on a flower. A line of **ants** marches along a branch. A **woodpecker** taps away at the bark, looking for insects hidden inside. Up in the branches, a **crow** sits in its messy twig nest. Down below, **earthworms** tunnel through the damp soil near the roots.

| Animal | What It Does Near Plants |
|---|---|
| **Elephants** | Munch on grass, leaves, and bamboo shoots |
| **Moths** | Rest on the underside of leaves during the day |
| **Caterpillars** | Nibble on soft, tender leaves |
| **Earthworms** | Burrow through the soil near roots |
| **Tailorbirds** | Perch on branches and sing sweet songs |
| **Woodpeckers** | Hammer tree trunks to dig out insects |
| **Ants** | Build nests using leaves glued together |
| **Frogs** | Sit on leaves near ponds and puddles |
| **Butterflies** | Visit flowers to sip nectar |
| **Squirrels** | Store nuts and rest inside tree hollows |

> Wherever there are plants, life follows! Plants offer food and a safe home to countless animals, birds, and insects.

![A tree with birds nesting in branches, squirrel in a hollow, ants on bark, and butterfly on flowers](tree-with-animals.png)

Animals depend on different parts of plants:

**For food:**
- **Elephants** eat grass, leaves, and bamboo.
- **Cows and buffaloes** chew grass and fodder all day.
- **Monkeys** grab fruits like bananas and mangoes from branches.
- **Butterflies and bees** drink sweet nectar from flowers.
- **Caterpillars** feast on soft, fresh leaves.
- **Parrots** crack open seeds and nibble on fruits.

**For shelter:**
- **Birds** weave nests from twigs, dry leaves, and grass in tree branches.
- **Squirrels** make their homes inside hollow tree trunks.
- **Owls** settle into dark holes in old trees to lay their eggs.
- **Insects** like ants and beetles live on bark and under fallen leaves.
- **Leopards** stretch out on thick branches of tall trees to rest.

## Life in the Soil and Monsoon

The **soil** beneath your feet is bursting with hidden life! Soil is the thin top layer of the Earth. It is made from:

- **Broken bits of rocks**, ground down over thousands of years
- **Decayed leaves, roots, and twigs** that have crumbled
- **Tiny living creatures** and remains of dead insects

If you scoop up a handful of earth near a plant, you might spot:

- **Ants** and **termites** scurrying about
- **Small beetles** and **grasshoppers** hopping
- **Earthworms** and **millipedes** (especially after a good rain)

The soil can feel **gritty, smooth, hard, dry, damp, or crumbly** depending on the weather and the place. After a rainstorm, the same patch of earth feels completely different -- wet, soft, and full of crawling creatures.

**What happens when the monsoon arrives?**

During the **monsoon** season, something magical takes place:

- **Seeds** that have been lying quietly in the soil for months suddenly **sprout** when they get rainwater.
- **Insects** that were hiding underground come crawling out after the first rain.
- **New grasses and tiny plants** start popping up everywhere in the damp soil.

> That is why the world looks so **green and alive** during the rainy season! The rain wakes up seeds and creatures that were waiting for just the right moment.

## Bird Sounds and the Life Cycle of a Butterfly

If you close your eyes and listen carefully, you can hear a concert of **bird songs** around you! Each bird has its own special call:

| Bird | The Sound It Makes |
|---|---|
| **Kabutar** (Pigeon) | Gutargu, gutargu |
| **Kauwa** (Crow) | Kaav, kaav |
| **Gauraiya** (Sparrow) | Chee-chee, chee-chee |
| **Koel** (Cuckoo) | Ku-hoo, ku-hoo |

**When can you hear birds the best?**

- **Early morning** -- birds are the busiest at sunrise, singing and searching for food
- **Evening** -- birds call out to each other as they return to their nests for the night

If you cup your hands behind your ears and point towards a bird sound, you can hear it even more clearly!

> If your neighbourhood has become quiet with no bird calls, it might be because there are not enough trees for birds to live in.

**The Amazing Journey from Caterpillar to Butterfly**

One afternoon, a girl named **Priya** found a tiny green **caterpillar** on a champa plant in her garden. She watched it every day. Here is what happened:

1. The caterpillar ate leaves hungrily, day after day.
2. It grew bigger and fatter.
3. One morning, it **stopped eating** and attached itself to a twig.
4. The caterpillar wrapped itself in a hard shell called a **cocoon**.
5. Days passed. Then one day, the cocoon cracked open!
6. Out came a gorgeous **butterfly** with bright orange and black wings.
7. It spread its wings in the sun and **flew away** gently.

> This incredible change from a crawling caterpillar to a flying butterfly is one of nature's most beautiful secrets!

## Key Points to Remember

- **Plants and animals live side by side** and depend on each other for survival.
- **Soil** is alive -- it holds insects, worms, roots, and tiny creatures.
- Animals use plants for **food** (fruits, leaves, nectar) and for **shelter** (nests, hollows, branches).
- When the **monsoon rains** arrive, new plants sprout and hidden creatures come out.
- Different birds have **different songs**, and we hear them best in the early morning.
- A **caterpillar** transforms into a **butterfly** inside a cocoon -- a wonderful process of nature.
- Where there are **more plants**, there will always be **more animals, birds, and insects**.
