## Why Our Body Needs Food

Imagine a car without petrol or a phone without charging. It simply will not work! Our body is the same way. **Food is the fuel** that keeps our body running. Without food, we would have no energy to walk, talk, think, or even breathe.

Food does three big jobs for us:

1. **Gives energy** -- so we can run, play, study, and do our daily work
2. **Helps us grow** -- our bones get longer, muscles get stronger, and we grow taller
3. **Protects us from illness** -- the right food keeps germs away and helps us recover quickly

We enjoy food in many **tastes**:

- **Sweet** -- like gud (jaggery), kheer, and ripe banana
- **Sour** -- like imli (tamarind), raw mango, and nimbu
- **Spicy** -- like mirchi and pepper
- **Salty** -- like papad and chana jor garam
- **Bitter** -- like karela and methi

> In many Indian temples, a **bhog** of different dishes is offered during festivals. Some grand temples prepare over 50 dishes -- covering every taste you can imagine!

## Aarav Learns About Good Food

**Aarav** was in Class 3 and loved playing cricket. He dreamed of becoming a fast bowler like his hero. But lately, he felt tired quickly and could not bowl for long.

His father took him to a doctor. The doctor asked, "What do you eat every day, Aarav?" Aarav said he mostly ate **plain rice and chips**.

The doctor smiled and said, "Aarav, imagine building a house with only bricks and no cement, no doors, no windows. It would not be complete! Your body also needs **many different types of food** to work properly."

The doctor helped Aarav plan better meals:

- **Different grains** -- not just rice, but also roti, idli, dosa, and millets like ragi and jowar
- **Colourful vegetables** -- palak, gajar, beans, and lauki
- **Fresh fruits** -- every day at least one fruit
- **Pulses and sprouts** -- moong, chana, and masoor dal
- **Milk and curd** -- for strong bones

Within two months, Aarav was bowling faster than ever. He told his friends, "The secret to my speed is not just practice -- it is eating right!"

> Eating many **different types of food** every day gives our body everything it needs. This is called a **balanced diet**.

## What Should We Eat?

A healthy thali should look colourful -- the more colours on your plate, the healthier your meal!

| Food Group | Examples | Why Our Body Needs It |
|---|---|---|
| **Grains** (energy foods) | Rice, roti, idli, dosa, ragi, jowar, bajra | Give us the energy to run, play, and study |
| **Vegetables** | Palak, gajar, tamatar, bhindi, lauki, beans | Keep our eyes, skin, and body strong |
| **Fruits** | Kela, aam, seb, amrud, papita, santara | Give vitamins that fight illness |
| **Pulses and Sprouts** (body-building foods) | Moong, toor, masoor, chana, rajma | Help our muscles grow and repair |
| **Milk and Dairy** | Doodh, dahi, paneer, chaas | Make bones and teeth strong |
| **Nuts and Seeds** | Badam, mungfali, akhrot, til | Give healthy fats and extra energy |

## Eating with the Seasons

India has different weather through the year, and our food changes with it. Our body is smart -- it craves what it needs!

| Season | What We Enjoy | Why It Helps |
|---|---|---|
| **Summer** (garmi) | Aam panna, nimbu shikanji, coconut water, watermelon, cucumber raita, kulfi | These foods and drinks **cool our body** and replace the water we lose through sweat |
| **Winter** (sardi) | Gajar ka halwa, sarson ka saag, til ke laddu, hot soups, bajra roti | These foods **warm us up** from inside and give extra energy |
| **Monsoon** (barsaat) | Bhutta (roasted corn), hot pakode with chai, ginger tea | Warm snacks keep us cozy during the rains |

**Summer fruits:** Aam, tarbooz, kharbooja, litchi, falsa

**Winter fruits:** Santara, seb, amrud, strawberry, ber

> When we play and sweat a lot, our body loses water. That is why we should **drink water often** -- especially in summer.

## Where Does Our Food Come From?

Most of our food comes from **plants**. But we eat different parts of different plants!

| Part of the Plant | What We Eat |
|---|---|
| **Leaves** | Palak, methi, sarson, patta gobhi, pudina, dhaniya |
| **Fruits** | Tamatar, bhindi, shimla mirch, aam, seb |
| **Roots** | Gajar, mooli, chukandar, shakarkandi |
| **Stems** | Aloo (yes, potato is an underground stem!), adrak, pyaaz |
| **Seeds** (grains) | Chawal, gehu, bajra, jowar, ragi |
| **Seeds** (pulses) | Moong, masoor, chana, toor |
| **Seeds** (dry fruits) | Badam, akhrot, kaju |
| **Flowers** | Banana flower (kele ka phool), pumpkin flower |

> Did you know? **Tomato** is actually a fruit of the plant, not a vegetable! But we use it as a vegetable in cooking.

## Food from Animals

Some foods come from **animals**, not plants:

| Food | Which Animal Gives It |
|---|---|
| **Milk** | Cow, buffalo, goat |
| **Dahi and paneer** | Made from milk |
| **Ghee and makhan** | Made from milk cream |
| **Honey** (shahad) | Honey bees |
| **Eggs** (ande) | Hen |

> **Fun question:** Two ingredients we use in almost every dish come neither from plants nor from animals. Can you guess? They are **salt** (from the sea or underground mines) and **water** (from rivers, wells, and rain)!

## Drink Plenty of Water

Water is not food, but our body needs it just as much. People often say, **"Paani hi jeevan hai"** (water is life).

We should drink **6 to 8 glasses of water** every day. On hot days, we can also enjoy healthy drinks like:

- **Nariyal paani** (tender coconut water)
- **Nimbu shikanji** (lemon drink with salt and sugar)
- **Aam panna** (raw mango drink)
- **Chaas** (buttermilk)
- **Ganne ka ras** (sugarcane juice)

## Never Waste Food

In India, **Anna Daan** (donating food) is considered one of the greatest acts of kindness. But sadly, a lot of food gets wasted every day. Many children in our country go to bed hungry.

**How to avoid wasting food:**

- Serve yourself only as much as you can **finish**
- If you have leftover roti, share it with **stray animals**
- Eat **ghar ka khaana** (home-cooked food) -- it is healthier and less wasteful
- Do not throw away fruits just because they have a small brown spot

> If your classmate forgot their tiffin at home, share your lunch happily. A shared meal always tastes better!

## Key Points to Remember

- Food gives our body **energy, helps us grow**, and **protects us from illness**.
- A **balanced diet** means eating many types of food -- grains, vegetables, fruits, pulses, dairy, and nuts.
- We eat different foods in **different seasons** -- cooling foods in summer and warming foods in winter.
- Most food comes from **plants** -- we eat their leaves, fruits, roots, stems, seeds, and even flowers.
- Some foods come from **animals** -- like milk, ghee, honey, and eggs.
- **Water** is essential -- drink at least 6-8 glasses every day.
- **Never waste food** -- serve only what you can finish and share with those who need it.
