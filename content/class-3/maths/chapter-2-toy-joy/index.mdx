## 3D Shapes Around Us

Look around your home and school! Many objects have a shape that you can hold, turn, and feel from all sides. These are called **3D shapes** because they have **length**, **width**, and **height** -- they are not flat like a drawing on paper.

Here are five important 3D shapes you should know:

| Shape | What it looks like | Where you see it |
|-------|-------------------|----------|
| **Cube** | A box where every side is the same size | A Ludo dice, a sugar cube, a gift box |
| **Cuboid** | A box shape like a brick | A tiffin box, a book, an eraser |
| **Cylinder** | A tube with round ends | A steel glass, a bangle, a rolling pin (belan) |
| **Cone** | Round at the bottom, pointed at the top | A kulfi mould, a birthday cap, a megaphone |
| **Sphere** | Perfectly round like a ball | A laddu, a cricket ball, a globe |

> **Remember:** A **cube** is actually a special **cuboid** where every side is exactly the same size!

---

## Faces, Edges, and Corners

Each 3D shape is made up of **faces**, **edges**, and **corners** (also called vertices).

- A **face** is any flat or curved surface you can touch on the shape.
- An **edge** is the line where two faces come together.
- A **corner** is the sharp point where edges meet.

| Shape | Faces | Edges | Corners |
|-------|:-----:|:-----:|:-------:|
| **Cube** | 6 flat | 12 | 8 |
| **Cuboid** | 6 flat | 12 | 8 |
| **Cylinder** | 2 flat + 1 curved | 2 circular | 0 |
| **Cone** | 1 flat + 1 curved | 1 circular | 1 (the pointed tip) |
| **Sphere** | 1 curved | 0 | 0 |

> **Think about it:** A **sphere** has no edges and no corners at all. That is why a cricket ball rolls so smoothly in every direction!

---

## Flat Faces and Curved Faces

Some 3D shapes have only flat surfaces, some have only curved surfaces, and some have a mix of both!

| Type | Shapes |
|------|--------|
| Only flat faces | Cube, Cuboid |
| Only curved faces | Sphere |
| Both flat and curved faces | Cylinder, Cone |

## Making Things with Shapes

You can join different 3D shapes together to build fun objects!

**A toy rocket** can be built using:
- A **cylinder** for the body
- A **cone** for the nose at the top
- Small **cuboids** for the fins at the bottom

**A miniature temple** can be made with:
- A **cuboid** for the main hall
- A **cone** for the shikhar (tower) on top
- **Cylinders** for the pillars at the entrance

```
  /\          <-- Cone (shikhar)
 /  \
|    |        <-- Cuboid (hall)
|    |
------
```

> **Activity:** Gather empty dabbas (boxes), bottles, and paper cones from your home. Use them to build a toy car, a house, or a temple!

## The Dice

A **dice** is a perfect **cube**. It has 6 faces, and each face has dots showing a number from 1 to 6.

Here is a special rule about a dice:
- The side opposite **1** always shows **6** (because 1 + 6 = 7)
- The side opposite **2** always shows **5** (because 2 + 5 = 7)
- The side opposite **3** always shows **4** (because 3 + 4 = 7)

> **Rule:** On a standard dice, any two opposite faces always add up to **7**!

## Joining Cubes

When you stick cubes together, you can create new shapes.

- Stick **2 cubes** side by side -- you get a **cuboid**.
- Stick **3 cubes** -- you can arrange them in a straight line or in an L-shape.
- Stick **4 cubes** -- even more arrangements are possible!

```
2 cubes in a row:
+---+---+
|   |   |   <-- This is a cuboid!
+---+---+

3 cubes in an L-shape:
+---+---+
|   |   |
+---+---+
|   |
+---+
```

> **Try this:** Take 4 sugar cubes. How many different shapes can you make by joining them? Try to make at least 3 different arrangements!

## Key Words to Remember

| Word | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **3D shape** | A solid shape that has length, width, and height -- not flat |
| **Cube** | A 3D shape with 6 equal square faces (like a dice) |
| **Cuboid** | A 3D shape with 6 rectangular faces (like a tiffin box) |
| **Cylinder** | A 3D shape with 2 flat circular faces and 1 curved face (like a steel glass) |
| **Cone** | A 3D shape with 1 flat circular face and 1 curved face, pointed at one end |
| **Sphere** | A perfectly round 3D shape (like a laddu) |
| **Face** | A flat or curved surface on a 3D shape |
| **Edge** | The line where two faces of a shape meet |
| **Corner** | The point where edges come together (also called a vertex) |
