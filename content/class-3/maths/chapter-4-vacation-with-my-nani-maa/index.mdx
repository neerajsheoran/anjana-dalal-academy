## Nani Maa's Magic Trick

**Aisha** and **Kabir** visit their **Nani Maa** in her village near Varanasi every summer. Nani Maa loves playing number games!

She fills a basket with **tamarind seeds** and says: "I will cover some seeds with my dupatta. Can you tell me how many I hid?"

If there are **18 seeds** in total and **11 are visible**, then the hidden ones are:

**18 - 11 = 7 seeds hidden!**

> **Key idea:** When you know the total and one part, just **subtract** to find the missing part.

| Total Seeds | Seeds You Can See | Hidden Seeds |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 18 | 11 | 7 |
| 14 | 6 | 8 |
| 22 | 13 | 9 |
| 19 | 5 | 14 |

## Adding Small Numbers

Aisha picked **8 guavas** from the garden and Kabir picked **6 guavas** for Nani Maa.

How many guavas altogether?

```
Aisha:    🟢🟢🟢🟢🟢🟢🟢🟢         = 8
Kabir:    🟢🟢🟢🟢🟢🟢               = 6
Together: 🟢🟢🟢🟢🟢🟢🟢🟢🟢🟢🟢🟢🟢🟢 = 14
```

**8 + 6 = 14 guavas**

## Adding on the Number Line

The children had **18 postcards**. Nani Maa gave them **9 more**. How many postcards do they have now?

You can add by **jumping forward** on a number line:

```
Start at 18, jump forward 9:
        +2          +7
  ---|---------|---------|---
    18         20        27
```

**18 + 9 = 27 postcards**

> **Shortcut:** Jump to the nearest round number first (like 20), then jump the rest. It makes adding much easier!

## Subtracting on the Number Line

The children have **46 stickers**. **29** of them are animal stickers. How many are flower stickers?

Jump **backward** on the number line:

```
     -9          -3
  |---------|-----|---
  29        40    46
```

Count the backward jumps: 6 + 10 + 1 = **17 stickers** are flower stickers.

Wait, let us think of it another way: from 29, how many jumps to reach 46?

```
     +1      +10      +6
  |---|---------|------|---
  29  30        40     46
```

1 + 10 + 6 = **17**

**46 - 29 = 17**

> **Another way:** Instead of subtracting, you can think: "29 + ? = 46". Count forward from 29 to 46 to find the answer!

## Skip Counting (Jumping by 10)

A grasshopper starts on the number **4** and hops **10 spaces** each time:

```
4 → 14 → 24 → 34 → 44 → 54 → 64 → 74 → 84 → 94
```

See the pattern? The **ones digit stays at 4**, and the **tens digit increases by 1** every hop!

Starting at **16**, hopping by 10:
```
16 → 26 → 36 → 46 → 56 → 66 → 76 → 86 → 96
```

**Hopping backward** by 10 from **83**:
```
83 → 73 → 63 → 53 → 43 → 33 → 23 → 13 → 3
```

## Adding Bigger Numbers

Nani Maa harvested **28 tomatoes** from one row and **34 tomatoes** from another row. How many tomatoes in total?

### Method 1: Splitting into tens and ones

```
  28  =  2 tens + 8 ones
+ 34  =  3 tens + 4 ones
------
         5 tens + 12 ones
       = 5 tens + 1 ten + 2 ones
       = 6 tens + 2 ones
       = 62
```

### Method 2: Jumping on the number line

```
Start at 28, add 34:
    +2       +10       +10       +10    +2
|---|---------|---------|---------|------|---
28  30        40        50        60     62
```

**28 + 34 = 62 tomatoes**

## Adding Numbers That Cross 100

Aisha collected **78 shells** from the riverbank and Kabir found **54 shells**.

```
Start at 78, add 54:
    +22            +32
|---|--------------|---
78  100            132
```

**78 + 54 = 132 shells**

> **Strategy:** When the total goes past 100, jump to 100 first, then add whatever is remaining.

## Box Diagrams

A **box diagram** is a drawing that helps you see the parts of a problem clearly.

**Addition example:** 78 shells + 54 shells = ?

```
+---------------------------+
|    78     |      54       |
+---------------------------+
          ? (total)
```

**Subtraction example:** Who found more shells, and by how much?

```
+---------------------------+
|    78     |  54  |   ?    |
+-----------+------+--------+
  Aisha     Kabir   difference
```

**78 - 54 = 24** (Aisha found 24 more shells)

## More Than 100 or Less Than 100?

Before solving, **guess** whether the answer will be more or less than 100. This is called **estimating**.

| Problem | Estimate | Answer |
|---------|----------|:------:|
| 60 + 40 + 3 | More than 100 | 103 |
| 35 + 28 | Less than 100 | 63 |
| 140 - 38 | More than 100 | 102 |
| 57 - 19 | Less than 100 | 38 |
| 88 + 15 + 2 | More than 100 | 105 |

> **Tip:** Round each number to the nearest ten to make a quick estimate before solving!

## Key Words to Remember

| Word | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Addition** | Combining numbers to find the total (using +) |
| **Subtraction** | Taking one number away from another (using -) |
| **Total / Sum** | The result of adding numbers together |
| **Difference** | The result of subtracting one number from another |
| **Estimate** | A smart guess about the answer before solving |
| **Number line** | A line with numbers in order, used for jumping forward or backward |
| **Box diagram** | A picture with boxes showing the parts and the whole |
| **Skip counting** | Counting by jumping the same amount each time (like by 10s) |
