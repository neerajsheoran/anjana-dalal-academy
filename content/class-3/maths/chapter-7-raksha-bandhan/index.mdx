## Repeated Addition

**Aarav** and **Tara** are preparing gift packets for Raksha Bandhan. Each packet needs **1 ribbon**, **3 chocolates**, and **5 decorative stars**.

If they prepare **4 packets**, how much material do they need?

**Ribbons:** 1 + 1 + 1 + 1 = **4 ribbons**

**Chocolates:** 3 + 3 + 3 + 3 = **12 chocolates**

**Stars:** 5 + 5 + 5 + 5 = **20 stars**

When you add the **same number over and over**, it is called **repeated addition**.

---

## What is Multiplication?

Adding the same number many times can be written in a shorter way using **multiplication**.

Instead of writing 5 + 5 + 5 + 5, we write:

**4 times 5** = **4 x 5 = 20**

The symbol **x** means "times" or "multiplied by".

| Repeated Addition | Multiplication | Answer |
|---|---|:---:|
| 2 + 2 + 2 + 2 | 4 x 2 | 8 |
| 3 + 3 + 3 | 3 x 3 | 9 |
| 5 + 5 + 5 + 5 | 4 x 5 | 20 |
| 6 + 6 + 6 + 6 + 6 | 5 x 6 | 30 |
| 4 + 4 + 4 | 3 x 4 | 12 |

> **Remember:** 4 x 5 means "4 groups of 5" or "add 5, four times".

## Counting in Groups

Counting in equal groups is faster than counting one by one!

**Biscuits on a plate:** If a plate has 4 rows with 5 biscuits in each row:

```
🍪 🍪 🍪 🍪 🍪
🍪 🍪 🍪 🍪 🍪
🍪 🍪 🍪 🍪 🍪
🍪 🍪 🍪 🍪 🍪

4 rows x 5 biscuits = 4 x 5 = 20 biscuits
```

**Three plates** of 20 biscuits each:
- 20 + 20 + 20 = 60, or **3 x 20 = 60 biscuits**

> **Shortcut:** When items are arranged in rows, count one row and multiply by the number of rows!

## What is Division?

**Division** means splitting a number into **equal groups** or **sharing equally**.

If there are **24 gulab jamuns** and **6 guests**, how many does each guest get?

Give 1 gulab jamun to each guest. Then another. Keep going until all are given out. Each guest gets **4**!

**24 shared equally among 6 = 4 each**

We write: **24 / 6 = 4**

The symbol **/** means "divided by".

| Total | Shared Among | Each Gets |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 24 gulab jamuns | 6 guests | 4 each |
| 15 pedas | 3 children | 5 each |
| 30 kaju katlis | 5 people | 6 each |
| 12 rasgullas | 4 friends | 3 each |

> **Connection:** Division is the reverse of multiplication! If 6 x 4 = 24, then 24 / 6 = 4.

## Skip Counting and Times Tables

**Skip counting** means jumping forward by the same number each time. This builds a **times table**!

### Skip counting by 4:

```
0, 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 28, 32, 36, 40
```

This gives us the **times-4 table**:

| Multiplication | Answer |
|:-----:|:------:|
| 1 x 4 | 4 |
| 2 x 4 | 8 |
| 3 x 4 | 12 |
| 4 x 4 | 16 |
| 5 x 4 | 20 |
| 6 x 4 | 24 |
| 7 x 4 | 28 |
| 8 x 4 | 32 |
| 9 x 4 | 36 |
| 10 x 4 | 40 |

## Patterns in Times Tables

### Times-5 table pattern:

```
5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 35, 40, 45, 50
```

The last digit is always **0** or **5**!

- Multiply 5 by an **even number** (2, 4, 6, 8, 10) and the answer ends in **0**.
- Multiply 5 by an **odd number** (1, 3, 5, 7, 9) and the answer ends in **5**.

### A neat trick with tables:

The **times-2 table** and the **times-3 table** can be combined to give the **times-5 table**!

| Times-2 | + | Times-3 | = | Times-5 |
|:---:|---|:---:|---|:---:|
| 1 x 2 = 2 | + | 1 x 3 = 3 | = | 1 x 5 = 5 |
| 2 x 2 = 4 | + | 2 x 3 = 6 | = | 2 x 5 = 10 |
| 3 x 2 = 6 | + | 3 x 3 = 9 | = | 3 x 5 = 15 |

## Multiplying Bigger Numbers

A rickshaw driver needs to fix **30 wheels**. Each wheel requires **4 nuts**.

Break it into parts:
- First 10 wheels: 10 x 4 = **40 nuts**
- Next 10 wheels: 10 x 4 = **40 nuts**
- Last 10 wheels: 10 x 4 = **40 nuts**
- Total: 40 + 40 + 40 = **120 nuts**

So, **30 x 4 = 120**

| Wheels | Nuts (x 4) |
|:------:|:------------:|
| 10 | 40 |
| 20 | 80 |
| 30 | 120 |
| 40 | 160 |
| 50 | 200 |

## Division with Bigger Numbers

Kavita has **96 bindis**. She wants to put them in packets of **24 each**. How many packets can she make?

Keep subtracting 24:
- 96 - 24 = **72** (1 packet)
- 72 - 24 = **48** (2 packets)
- 48 - 24 = **24** (3 packets)
- 24 - 24 = **0** (4 packets)

**96 / 24 = 4 packets**

> **Method:** For big numbers, keep subtracting the same number until you reach 0 (or cannot subtract anymore). Count how many times you subtracted -- that is the answer!

| Problem | Operation | Answer |
|---------|-----------|:------:|
| An octopus has 8 arms. How many arms do 4 octopuses have? | 4 x 8 | 32 arms |
| 27 wheels are in a shop. Each cycle has 3 wheels (tricycles). How many tricycles? | 27 / 3 | 9 tricycles |

## Key Words to Remember

| Word | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Multiplication** | A quick way to do repeated addition (using the x symbol) |
| **Times** | Another word for multiplication (4 times 5 = 4 x 5) |
| **Division** | Splitting into equal groups or sharing equally (using the / symbol) |
| **Times table** | A list of a number multiplied by 1 through 10 |
| **Skip counting** | Jumping forward by the same number each time (like 4, 8, 12, 16...) |
| **Groups** | Equal sets of items placed together |
| **Repeated addition** | Adding the same number again and again (like 5 + 5 + 5 + 5) |
| **Repeated subtraction** | Subtracting the same number over and over to divide |
