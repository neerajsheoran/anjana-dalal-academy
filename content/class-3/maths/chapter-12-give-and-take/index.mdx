## Adding 3-Digit Numbers

Meena's mother runs a flower stall. She had **248 marigold flowers** in the morning and her helper brought **173 more** from the market. How many flowers does she have now?

We need to find: **248 + 173 = ?**

### Using a box diagram:

```
+------+------+
| 248  | 173  |
+------+------+
      ???
```

### Using Hundreds, Tens, and Ones:

| | Hundreds | Tens | Ones |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 248 | 2 | 4 | 8 |
| + 173 | 1 | 7 | 3 |
| **Answer** | **4** | **2** | **1** |

**How?**
- Ones: 8 + 3 = 11 ones = **1 ten and 1 one** (carry the 1 ten over)
- Tens: 4 + 7 + 1 (carried) = 12 tens = **1 hundred and 2 tens** (carry the 1 hundred over)
- Hundreds: 2 + 1 + 1 (carried) = 4 hundreds

**248 + 173 = 421 flowers**

> **Key idea:** When ones or tens add up to 10 or more, we regroup. 10 ones become 1 ten. 10 tens become 1 hundred.

## Subtracting 3-Digit Numbers

Meena received an order for **315 roses**. She has already packed **148**. How many more does she need to pack?

We need to find: **315 - 148 = ?**

### Using a box diagram:

```
+----------+----------+
|   148    |    ???   |
+----------+----------+
         315
```

### Using Hundreds, Tens, and Ones:

We cannot take 8 from 5 ones! So we **borrow**:
- Change 1 ten into 10 ones: Now we have **0 tens** and **15 ones**.
- 15 - 8 = 7 ones.

Next, we cannot take 4 tens from 0 tens! So we **borrow** again:
- Change 1 hundred into 10 tens: Now we have **2 hundreds** and **10 tens**.
- 10 - 4 = 6 tens.

| | Hundreds | Tens | Ones |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 315 | 3 | 1 | 5 |
| - 148 | 1 | 4 | 8 |
| **Answer** | **1** | **6** | **7** |

**315 - 148 = 167 roses**

> **Remember:** When you cannot subtract a digit, borrow from the next place to the left. 1 hundred = 10 tens. 1 ten = 10 ones.

## Adding and Subtracting Mentally

Here are some tricks for doing maths quickly in your head:

### Adding 100, 10, or 1:

| Start | + 100 | + 10 | + 1 |
|:-----:|:-----:|:----:|:---:|
| 345 | 445 | 355 | 346 |
| 578 | 678 | 588 | 579 |

> **Notice:** Adding 100 changes only the **hundreds** digit. Adding 10 changes only the **tens** digit. Adding 1 changes only the **ones** digit.

## Money: Notes and Coins

Long ago, there was no money. People **swapped** things directly. A potter would give pots and get grain in return. This is called **barter**.

Today, we use **money** -- notes and coins -- to buy and sell things.

### Indian Currency:

| Notes | Coins |
|-------|-------|
| ₹10 | ₹1 |
| ₹20 | ₹2 |
| ₹50 | ₹5 |
| ₹100 | ₹10 |
| ₹200 | ₹20 |
| ₹500 | |

### Useful facts:

```
₹500 = 5 notes of ₹100
₹100 = 2 notes of ₹50
₹100 = 5 notes of ₹20
₹100 = 10 notes of ₹10
```

![Indian currency notes and coins of different denominations](indian-notes-and-coins.png)

> **Quick question:** How many ₹50 notes make ₹200? Answer: **4 notes**!

## Solving Money Problems

### Example 1: How much was earned?

Raju chacha had **₹328** in the morning. By evening he had **₹615**. How much did he earn during the day?

```
Box diagram:
+------+---------+
| 328  |   ???   |
+------+---------+
       615
```

**615 - 328 = ₹287** earned.

### Example 2: Getting change

Kavita bought vegetables for **₹185**. She gave a **₹500** note. How much change should she receive?

**500 - 185 = ₹315** change.

### Example 3: Saving regularly

Arjun puts ₹50 in his piggy bank every week. He started with **₹200**.

## Estimating Answers

Before solving, make a **smart guess** (estimate). Round each number to the nearest hundred.

| Problem | Estimate (nearest hundred) |
|---------|:-:|
| 182 + 47 | About 200 + 0 = 200 |
| 310 + 195 | About 300 + 200 = 500 |
| 600 - 289 | About 600 - 300 = 300 |
| 412 + 398 | About 400 + 400 = 800 |

> **Why estimate?** It helps you check if your answer makes sense. If your actual answer is far from your estimate, you may have made a calculation mistake!

## Comparing Without Calculating

Sometimes you can figure out which answer is bigger **without solving**:

| Problem A | Problem B | Which is bigger? |
|-----------|-----------|:-:|
| 250 + 30 | 250 + 55 | B (because 55 > 30) |
| 480 - 20 | 480 - 65 | A (subtracting less leaves more) |
| 700 + 12 | 690 + 50 | B (690 + 50 = 740 vs 712) |

> **Rule of thumb:** Adding **more** to the same number gives a **bigger** answer. Subtracting **more** from the same number gives a **smaller** answer.

## Key Words to Remember

| Word | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Addition** | Combining two or more numbers to get a total |
| **Subtraction** | Taking one number away from another |
| **Regroup / Borrow** | Changing 1 ten into 10 ones (or 1 hundred into 10 tens) when you cannot subtract |
| **Carry over** | Moving extra tens or hundreds to the next place when adding |
| **Box diagram** | A picture showing parts and whole of a problem |
| **Estimate** | A smart guess about the answer before solving |
| **Money** | Notes and coins used to buy things |
| **Barter** | Swapping goods directly without using money |
| **Change** | The money returned when you pay more than the price |
