## Flat Shapes (2D Shapes)

In Chapter 2, we explored solid 3D shapes you can hold in your hands. Now let us learn about **flat shapes** -- the ones you can draw on paper or see on the floor. These flat shapes are called **2D shapes** because they have only **length** and **width** (no height).

When your mother draws a **rangoli** on Diwali, she uses lots of flat shapes -- circles, triangles, squares, and more!

---

## Rectangle

A **rectangle** has **4 straight sides** and **4 corners**. Its special feature is that the **opposite sides are equal** -- the two longer sides match each other, and the two shorter sides match each other.

```
+----------+
|          |
|          |
+----------+
```

### What makes a rectangle special?

- It has exactly **4 sides** and **4 corners**.
- All 4 corners are **right angles** (like the corner of your textbook).
- The two long sides are equal. The two short sides are equal.

**Where can you spot rectangles in your home?**
- A door
- A notebook
- A TV screen
- A chocolate bar

> **Activity:** Place your eraser on the table and trace around it with a pencil. Lift the eraser -- you will see a rectangle!

---

## Square

A **square** is a rectangle with a twist -- **all 4 of its sides are exactly the same length**!

```
+------+
|      |
|      |
|      |
+------+
```

### How is a square different from a rectangle?

| | Rectangle | Square |
|---|---|---|
| Number of sides | 4 | 4 |
| Number of corners | 4 | 4 |
| Type of corners | Right angles | Right angles |
| Sides | Opposite sides are equal | **All 4 sides are equal** |

**Where can you spot squares?**
- A carrom board
- A floor tile
- Each small square on a chess board
- A handkerchief

> **Key fact:** Every square is a rectangle, but not every rectangle is a square!

---

## Triangle

A **triangle** has **3 straight sides** and **3 corners**. The word "tri" means **three**, so a triangle is a shape with three angles.

```
    /\
   /  \
  /    \
 /______\
```

**Where can you spot triangles?**
- A slice of pizza
- A samosa
- A clothes hanger
- The roof of a hut
- Rangoli designs

> **Fun fact:** If you fold a square piece of paper from one corner to the opposite corner, you get two triangles!

---

## Circle

A **circle** is a perfectly round shape. It has **no straight sides** and **no corners**. It is made of one smooth curved line.

Every circle has a **center** -- the exact middle point.

**Where can you spot circles?**
- A roti or chapati
- A bangle
- A one-rupee coin
- A clock face
- The wheel of a bicycle

### How to find the center of a circle

Take a round piece of paper (like a paper plate). Fold it in **half**. Then fold it in half once more. Open it up. The point where the two fold lines cross is the **center** of the circle!

---

## Corners: Right Angles and Others

The corner of a square or a rectangle is called a **right angle** (also known as a square corner). It looks exactly like the corner of a book.

Not every corner is a right angle:

| Type | What it looks like |
|------|-------------------|
| **Right angle (square corner)** | Exactly like the corner of a book or notebook |
| **Less than a right angle** | A sharper, more narrow corner |
| **More than a right angle** | A wider, more open corner |

> **How to check:** Hold the corner of a book against any corner you want to test. If they match perfectly, it is a right angle!

## Comparing Shapes

| Shape | Sides | Corners | Type of Sides |
|-------|:-----:|:-------:|---------------------|
| **Rectangle** | 4 | 4 (all right angles) | Straight |
| **Square** | 4 | 4 (all right angles) | Straight |
| **Triangle** | 3 | 3 | Straight |
| **Circle** | 0 | 0 | Curved |

## Joining Shapes to Make New Shapes

Shapes can be combined to create bigger and more interesting shapes!

- Place **2 squares** side by side and you get a **rectangle**.
- Join **2 identical triangles** along their longest side and you can form a **square** or a **rectangle**.
- Arrange many triangles and squares together to create a beautiful **rangoli** pattern.

```
Two triangles joined together:

 /|      |\        +------+
/ |  +   | \  =   |      |
/__|      |__\     +------+
```

A **tangram** is a famous puzzle with 7 flat pieces. You can rearrange these 7 pieces to make shapes of animals, houses, boats, and much more!

---

## Lines and Patterns

Shapes are built from **lines**. Lines come in two types:

- **Straight lines** -- like the edge of a ruler or a door frame
- **Curved lines** -- like the rim of a plate or the letter C

The borders of sarees, mehndi patterns, and rangoli designs all use beautiful arrangements of straight and curved lines.

> **Activity:** Draw a pattern using circles and triangles placed one after another in a row. Colour them to make a border design!

## Key Words to Remember

| Word | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Rectangle** | A 4-sided flat shape with right-angle corners; opposite sides are equal |
| **Square** | A 4-sided flat shape where all sides are equal and all corners are right angles |
| **Triangle** | A 3-sided flat shape with 3 corners |
| **Circle** | A round flat shape with no sides and no corners |
| **Corner** | The point where two sides of a shape meet |
| **Right angle** | A corner that looks like the corner of a book (also called a square corner) |
| **Side** | A straight line that forms the boundary of a shape |
| **Center** | The exact middle point of a circle |
| **Tangram** | A puzzle made of 7 flat pieces that can be rearranged into many shapes |
