## A Trip to Mawlynnong

Tucked away in the green hills of Meghalaya lies a tiny village called Mawlynnong. Known as **Asia's Cleanest Village**, it is also lovingly called **God's Own Garden**. Around 77 families live here, all belonging to the Khasi community. The village sits about 79 km from the capital city Shillong.

In this chapter, we will sharpen our **addition and subtraction** skills through stories inspired by a school trip to this wonderful village!

## Addition — Putting Numbers Together

### Adding 2-Digit Numbers with Regrouping

Sometimes when we add, the ones column totals **10 or more**. When that happens, we **regroup** — carry the extra ten over to the tens column.

**Example:** 37 + 45 = ?

| Step | What we do |
|------|-----------|
| 1. Add the **Ones**: 7 + 5 = 12 | 12 Ones = **1 Ten and 2 Ones** |
| 2. Write 2 in the Ones column | Carry 1 Ten to the Tens column |
| 3. Add the **Tens**: 3 + 4 + 1 (carry) = 8 | Write 8 in the Tens column |

```
    T  O
    3  7
  + 4  5
  ------
    8  2
```

**Answer: 37 + 45 = 82**

### Adding 3-Digit Numbers with Regrouping

**Example:** 365 + 478 = ?

| Step | What we do |
|------|-----------|
| 1. Add the **Ones**: 5 + 8 = 13 | 13 Ones = 1 Ten and 3 Ones. Write 3, carry 1. |
| 2. Add the **Tens**: 6 + 7 + 1 = 14 | 14 Tens = 1 Hundred and 4 Tens. Write 4, carry 1. |
| 3. Add the **Hundreds**: 3 + 4 + 1 = 8 | Write 8 in the Hundreds column |

```
    H  T  O
    3  6  5
  + 4  7  8
  ---------
    8  4  3
```

**Answer: 365 + 478 = 843**

> **Golden rule:** When any column adds up to 10 or more, **regroup** by carrying 1 to the next column on the left. 10 Ones = 1 Ten. 10 Tens = 1 Hundred. 10 Hundreds = 1 Thousand.

## Estimating Before You Calculate

Before you start calculating, make a quick **estimate** (a rough guess) to check if your final answer looks sensible.

**How to estimate:**
- Round each number to the nearest ten or hundred
- Add or subtract the rounded numbers

**Example:** 365 + 478
- 365 is close to 370, and 478 is close to 480
- 370 + 480 = 850
- Our actual answer (843) is close to 850 — makes sense!

## Subtraction — Finding the Difference

### Subtracting 2-Digit Numbers with Regrouping

When the top digit in the ones column is **smaller** than the bottom digit, we **borrow** from the tens column.

**Example:** 72 - 38 = ?

| Step | What we do |
|------|-----------|
| 1. Ones: 2 is less than 8, so borrow | Take 1 Ten from 7 Tens. Now: 6 Tens and 12 Ones |
| 2. Subtract Ones: 12 - 8 = 4 | Write 4 |
| 3. Subtract Tens: 6 - 3 = 3 | Write 3 |

```
    T  O
    6  12
    7  2
  - 3  8
  ------
    3  4
```

**Answer: 72 - 38 = 34**

### Subtracting 3-Digit Numbers with Regrouping

**Example:** 503 - 267 = ?

| Step | What we do |
|------|-----------|
| 1. Ones: 3 is less than 7. Borrow from Tens. | But Tens is 0! So borrow from Hundreds first. |
| 2. Regroup: 5 H becomes 4 H + 10 T. Then borrow 1 T for Ones: 9 T + 13 O. | Now: 4 H, 9 T, 13 O |
| 3. Subtract Ones: 13 - 7 = 6 | |
| 4. Subtract Tens: 9 - 6 = 3 | |
| 5. Subtract Hundreds: 4 - 2 = 2 | |

```
    H  T  O
    4  9  13
    5  0  3
  - 2  6  7
  ---------
    2  3  6
```

**Answer: 503 - 267 = 236**

> **Golden rule:** When a top digit is smaller than the bottom digit, **borrow** 1 from the next column to the left. 1 Ten = 10 Ones. 1 Hundred = 10 Tens.

## Returning Change — A Real-Life Skill

When you buy something and pay more than the price, the shopkeeper returns **change** (balance). You find the change by **subtracting** the cost from what you paid.

**Change = Amount Paid - Cost**

| Cost | Paid | Change |
|------|------|---------|
| ₹127 | ₹200 | ₹73 |
| ₹348 | ₹500 | ₹152 |
| ₹216 | ₹300 | ₹84 |
| ₹189 | ₹500 | ₹311 |

> **At the market:** Rina and Amit visit Kamla Aunty's fruit stall. 1 kg of apples costs ₹62 and 1 kg of grapes costs ₹48. The total is 62 + 48 = ₹110. They hand over ₹200. Change = 200 - 110 = ₹90.

## Adding and Subtracting Costs

### Adding Costs

When buying multiple items, **add** the prices to find the total bill:

| Item | Cost per kg |
|------|------------|

| Bananas | ₹40 |
| Grapes | ₹48 |
| Oranges | ₹55 |
| Guava | ₹35 |
| Pomegranate | ₹80 |

- 2 x ₹35 + 3 x ₹40 = ₹70 + ₹120 = **₹190**

**Example:** 1 kg of pomegranate and 2 kg of oranges
- 1 x ₹80 + 2 x ₹55 = ₹80 + ₹110 = **₹190**

## Quick Mental Strategies

Sometimes you can solve problems in your head without writing anything down:

| Problem | Strategy | Answer |
|---------|----------|--------|
| 453 + 30 | Just add 3 tens | **483** |
| 582 + 8 | Just add 8 to ones | **590** |
| 620 - 15 | 620 - 10 = 610, then 610 - 5 | **605** |
| 307 - 10 | Just subtract 1 ten | **297** |
| 725 + 99 | Add 100, then subtract 1: 825 - 1 | **824** |
| 860 - 29 | Subtract 30, then add 1: 830 + 1 | **831** |

> **Smart shortcut:** Adding 99 is the same as adding 100 and taking away 1. Subtracting 29 is the same as subtracting 30 and adding back 1. These tricks speed up your mental maths!

## Word Problems — Think Before You Calculate

When you see a word problem, follow these five steps:

1. **Read** the problem carefully — do not rush
2. **Decide** — should you add or subtract?
3. **Estimate** the answer roughly
4. **Calculate** carefully
5. **Check** — does your answer make sense?

### When to Add
- Finding the **total** of two or more amounts
- Finding **how many altogether**

### When to Subtract
- Finding **how many more** or **how many fewer**
- Finding **how many are left**
- Finding the **difference** between two amounts

> **Example:** Sunita Aunty harvested 318 tomatoes last month and 456 this month. How many **more** tomatoes did she pick this month? This needs subtraction: 456 - 318 = **138 more tomatoes**.

## Key Words to Remember

| Word | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Addition** | Combining numbers to find the total (sum) |
| **Subtraction** | Taking one number away from another to find the difference |
| **Regrouping** | Moving units from one place to the next (e.g., 10 Ones = 1 Ten) |
| **Estimate** | A rough guess made before calculating the exact answer |
| **Sum** | The result you get after adding |
| **Difference** | The result you get after subtracting |
| **Balance / Change** | Money returned when you pay more than the cost |
| **Carry over** | Moving extra units to the next column when adding |
| **Borrow** | Taking units from the next column when subtracting |
| **Word problem** | A maths question written as a story or real-life situation |
