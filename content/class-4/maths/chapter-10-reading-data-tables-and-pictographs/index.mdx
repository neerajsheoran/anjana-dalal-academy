## India's Amazing Wildlife

India's forests are home to incredible animals that are found almost nowhere else. Here are some fascinating numbers:

- India has around **30,000 elephants** spread across its southern and north-eastern states
- The country's tiger count has risen to over **3000** thanks to conservation efforts
- More than **13,000 leopards** roam India's jungles, farmlands, and hills

In this chapter, we will work with real wildlife numbers to sharpen our skills in **adding and subtracting 4-digit numbers**!

---

## Addition of 4-Digit Numbers

Adding 4-digit numbers follows the same rules as smaller numbers — start from the **ones place** and move left, **carrying over** whenever a column adds to 10 or more.

### Example: Elephants in Two Southern States

Tamil Nadu has **2761** elephants. Karnataka has **6395** elephants. What is the combined total?

```
    Th   H   T   O
     2   7   6   1
  +  6   3   9   5
  -----------------
     9   1   5   6
```

**Step by step:**
1. Ones: 1 + 5 = 6. Write 6.
2. Tens: 6 + 9 = 15. Write 5, carry 1.
3. Hundreds: 7 + 3 + 1 = 11. Write 1, carry 1.
4. Thousands: 2 + 6 + 1 = 9. Write 9.

**Answer: 2761 + 6395 = 9156 elephants**

## Adding Three 4-Digit Numbers

### Example: Leopard Counts in Three States

Rajasthan has **1776** leopards, Maharashtra has **1690**, and Tamil Nadu has **1023**. What is the total?

```
    Th   H   T   O
     1   7   7   6
     1   6   9   0
  +  1   0   2   3
  -----------------
     4   4   8   9
```

**Step by step:**
1. Ones: 6 + 0 + 3 = 9.
2. Tens: 7 + 9 + 2 = 18. Write 8, carry 1.
3. Hundreds: 7 + 6 + 0 + 1 = 14. Write 4, carry 1.
4. Thousands: 1 + 1 + 1 + 1 = 4.

**Answer: 1776 + 1690 + 1023 = 4489 leopards**

> **Carrying rule refresher:**
> - 10 Ones = 1 Ten
> - 10 Tens = 1 Hundred
> - 10 Hundreds = 1 Thousand

## Subtraction of 4-Digit Numbers

Subtraction starts from the **ones** and moves left, **borrowing** whenever the top digit is smaller than the bottom one.

### Example: Tiger Numbers in Two Regions

Central India has **4684** tigers. North India has **1889** tigers. How many more tigers does Central India have?

4684 - 1889 = ?

```
    Th   H   T   O
     3  15  17  14
     4   6   8   4
  -  1   8   8   9
  -----------------
     2   7   9   5
```

**Step by step:**
1. Ones: 4 - 9? Cannot! Borrow 1 ten: 14 - 9 = 5.
2. Tens: 7 (after borrowing) - 8? Cannot! Borrow 1 hundred: 17 - 8 = 9. Wait, let me redo: 8-1=7 tens, still 7-8 won't work. Borrow from hundreds: 17-8=9.
3. Hundreds: 5 (after borrowing) - 8? Cannot! Borrow 1 thousand: 15 - 8 = 7.
4. Thousands: 3 (after borrowing) - 1 = 2.

**Answer: 4684 - 1889 = 2795 more tigers in Central India**

## The Addition Grid

An addition grid shows the sum of any two numbers from 0 to 12 arranged in rows and columns.

### Interesting Patterns

1. Every **diagonal line** (going from top-right to bottom-left) contains the same number repeated
2. The grid is **symmetric** — swapping the row and column number gives the same sum (because 5 + 8 = 8 + 5)
3. Pick any small square of 4 numbers inside the grid. The two diagonal pairs always give the same total!
4. Adding any number to 0 gives back the same number (the zero row and zero column are special)

## Reverse and Add

Try this fun pattern with any 2-digit number:

1. Pick a number: **34**
2. Write it backwards: **43**
3. Add them: 34 + 43 = **77**

| Number | Its reverse | Sum |
|--------|-----------|-----|
| 34 | 43 | 77 |
| 16 | 61 | 77 |
| 25 | 52 | 77 |
| 49 | 94 | 143 |
| 58 | 85 | 143 |
| 67 | 76 | 143 |

> **Hidden pattern:** Numbers whose digits add up to the same total always give the same "reverse and add" answer! 3+4 = 7, 1+6 = 7, 2+5 = 7 — and all three give 77.

## Mental Maths Strategies

Clever shortcuts help you solve problems in your head:

| Problem | Think this way | Answer |
|---------|---------------|--------|
| 5555 - 99 | Subtract 100, add back 1 | **5456** |
| 3280 + 203 | Add 200, then add 3 | **3483** |
| 6738 + 52 | Add 50, then add 2 | **6790** |
| 2990 + 415 | Add 10 to reach 3000, then add 405 | **3405** |
| 4567 - 30 | Subtract 30 from the tens place | **4537** |
| 7999 + 125 | Add 1 to get 8000, then add 124 | **8124** |

## Comparing Without Calculating

You can often tell which expression is bigger **without** doing the full sum:

- **63 + 88 vs 63 + 79**: Same first number, but 88 > 79, so the first is bigger
- **95 - 42 vs 80 - 42**: Subtracting the same number from a larger starting number leaves more
- **55 - 28 vs 55 - 31**: Subtracting more takes away more, so the first is bigger

> **Simple rule:** If one number stays the same and the other one changes, focus only on the change.

| 245 + 368 = 613 | 613 - 368 = ? | **245** (reverse the addition) |
| 920 - 456 = 464 | 920 - 457 = ? | **463** (taking 1 more away leaves 1 less) |

## Key Words to Remember

| Word | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **4-digit number** | A number from 1000 to 9999 |
| **Carrying (regrouping)** | Moving a group of 10 to the next column during addition |
| **Borrowing** | Taking 1 from the next column during subtraction |
| **Estimate** | A rough guess to check if your answer is reasonable |
| **Mental maths** | Solving problems in your head using smart shortcuts |
| **Deposit slip** | A form listing notes and coins you are putting in the bank |
| **Reverse and add** | Flipping a number's digits and adding them to the original |
