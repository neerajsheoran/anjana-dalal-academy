## Our Neighbourhood

Step outside your front door and take a good look around. You will notice many places that make daily life possible -- the **bus stand** where people catch rides to work, the **vegetable market** buzzing with shoppers, the **clinic** where Dr. Sharma checks on patients, and the **bank** where your parents keep their savings safe. Each place has a job to do, and together they form the heartbeat of your neighbourhood.

---

## Communication -- Then and Now

**Communication** is the way people share thoughts, news and feelings with one another. The methods humans have used to communicate have changed dramatically over the centuries.

| Era | How People Sent Messages |
|---|---|
| Ancient times | Beat drums, carved symbols on cave walls |
| Hundreds of years ago | Sent letters through runners or trained pigeons |
| About 150 years ago | Used the postal service to mail letters and parcels |
| About 100 years ago | Spoke through telephones, heard news on the radio |
| Today | Send instant messages on smartphones, make video calls, use the internet |

Long ago, a letter from Delhi to Chennai might take **several weeks** to arrive. Today, a text message reaches in **less than a second**!

![Evolution of communication: drumbeats, letters by pigeon, telephone, and mobile phone](communication-evolution.png)

> **Did you know?** India's postal network is one of the biggest in the world. With over 1.5 lakh post offices, it reaches even the most remote villages in the hills and deserts.

## The Post Office

A **post office** is a public place that handles letters, parcels and many other services. Here is the journey of a letter:

1. Kavya writes a letter to her grandmother in Varanasi and drops it in the **red letterbox** near her school.
2. The **postman** empties the letterbox and takes all the letters to the nearest post office.
3. At the post office, letters are **sorted** -- grouped by city and region.
4. The sorted letters travel by mail vans or trains to the correct city.
5. The local postman in Varanasi finally **delivers** the letter to Kavya's grandmother.

Apart from letters, post offices offer **speed post** (faster delivery), **money orders** (sending money), and even **savings accounts** for people who want to save small amounts safely.

## Modes of Transport

To move from one place to another, people use different **modes of transport**:

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Road transport** | Bicycle, auto-rickshaw, bus, car, truck |
| **Rail transport** | Local train, express train, metro |
| **Water transport** | Boat, ferry, ship |
| **Air transport** | Aeroplane, helicopter |

Cities like Delhi and Bengaluru have **metro rail** systems that zip through underground tunnels and elevated tracks, carrying thousands of passengers each day. **Flyovers** lift vehicles above crowded crossroads, cutting travel time.

> Building new highways and bridges is important, but we must be mindful. Construction can disturb trees, birds and small animals. That is why planners often plant **twice as many trees** alongside new roads to replace the ones that were removed.

## The Hospital

A **hospital** is one of the most vital places in any neighbourhood. It is where people go when they are unwell or hurt. Hospitals also hold **health camps** -- free check-up events for things like eyesight, blood pressure and dental health.

| Person | What They Do |
|---|---|
| **Doctor** | Examines patients, finds out what is wrong, and prescribes treatment |
| **Nurse** | Cares for patients, gives injections and keeps records |
| **Pharmacist** | Provides the right medicines based on the doctor's prescription |
| **Lab Technician** | Collects blood or other samples and runs tests |
| **Ambulance Driver** | Rushes patients to the hospital during emergencies |

All these people work as a team so that every patient gets the best possible care.

## Banks and Saving Money

A **bank** is a safe place to keep your money. Instead of storing cash at home in a box, people deposit it in a bank account where it is protected and even earns a little extra money called **interest**.

**Useful things to know about banks:**

- An **ATM** (Automated Teller Machine) is a machine outside the bank that lets you withdraw cash at any hour, even at midnight!
- A **passbook** is a small booklet the bank gives you. It records every rupee that goes into or comes out of your account, along with your account number and IFSC code.
- Today, many people transfer money using apps on their **mobile phones** -- no need to stand in a long queue.

> **A little history:** Before money was invented, people used the **barter system** -- they swapped one thing for another. A potter might give a pot to a farmer in exchange for a sack of wheat. But what if the farmer did not need a pot? Finding the right swap was tricky. So, coins and notes were invented to make trading simple and fair.

**Why is saving a good habit?**

- Saved money helps during sudden needs, like a medical bill.
- It lets you plan for something special, like a new bicycle.
- Even saving 5 or 10 rupees regularly adds up over time!

## Directions and Maps

A **map** is a drawing of a place as seen from above, like a bird looking down. On every map, the four main directions are:

| Direction | Where It Sits on a Map |
|---|---|
| **North** | Top |
| **South** | Bottom |
| **East** | Right side |
| **West** | Left side |

![A compass rose showing North, South, East, and West directions](compass-directions.png)

**Easy trick to find East:** Stand outside early in the morning and face the rising Sun. You are now looking **East**. Your back is towards **West**, your left hand points **North**, and your right hand points **South**.

**Landmarks** are easily recognisable places that help people give and follow directions. A tall clock tower, a Hanuman temple, a blue water tank or a famous sweet shop can all serve as landmarks. When telling someone the way, you might say, "Walk straight past the gurudwara, turn left at the petrol pump, and the school is on your right."

## Community Services

Several services work behind the scenes to keep our neighbourhood safe and orderly:

| Service | Key People | Vehicle Used |
|---|---|---|
| **Police Station** | Police officers, head constable | Patrol jeep, motorcycle |
| **Hospital** | Doctors, nurses, ward attendants | Ambulance |
| **Fire Station** | Firefighters, fire officer | Fire engine with water tank |
| **Post Office** | Postman, sorting clerk | Mail van, bicycle |
| **Library** | Librarian, assistant | -- |

If there is a fire, we dial **101**. For police help, we dial **100**. For a medical emergency, we dial **102** or **108**. Knowing these numbers can save lives.

## Key Points to Remember

- **Communication** has evolved from cave carvings and drum signals to smartphones and video calls.
- **Post offices** handle letters, parcels, money orders and even savings accounts.
- The four **modes of transport** are road, rail, water and air.
- **Hospitals** rely on a team of doctors, nurses, pharmacists and others to treat patients.
- **Banks** keep our money safe; ATMs and mobile apps make banking convenient.
- **Maps** use four directions (North, South, East, West) and **landmarks** to help us navigate.
- **Community services** like the police, fire brigade and hospitals protect us every day.
