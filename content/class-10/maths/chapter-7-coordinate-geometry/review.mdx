## Key Concepts

- **Distance formula**: PQ = **root[(x2 - x1)^2 + (y2 - y1)^2]** -- derived from Pythagoras theorem
- Distance from point (x, y) to origin = **root(x^2 + y^2)**
- **Section formula**: point dividing segment from A(x1, y1) to B(x2, y2) in ratio m1:m2 is **((m1.x2 + m2.x1)/(m1+m2), (m1.y2 + m2.y1)/(m1+m2))**
- **Mid-point formula** (ratio 1:1): M = **((x1+x2)/2, (y1+y2)/2)**
- **Area of triangle** with vertices (x1,y1), (x2,y2), (x3,y3) = **(1/2)|x1(y2-y3) + x2(y3-y1) + x3(y1-y2)|**
- If the area formula gives **zero**, the three points are **collinear**
- Three points are collinear if the sum of two pairwise distances equals the third (AB + BC = AC)
- To find ratio in which a point divides a segment: let ratio = k:1, use section formula, solve for k
- **Trisection points** divide a segment into 3 equal parts at ratios 1:2 and 2:1
- Area of a quadrilateral = split into two triangles using a diagonal and add their areas
- Use distance formula to classify shapes: parallelogram, rectangle, rhombus, or square

## Important Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Abscissa** | x-coordinate (horizontal distance from y-axis) |
| **Ordinate** | y-coordinate (vertical distance from x-axis) |
| **Collinear points** | Points lying on the same straight line |
| **Section formula** | Gives coordinates of a point dividing a segment in a given ratio |

## Quadrilateral Identification

| Shape | Condition |
|-------|-----------|
| Parallelogram | Opposite sides equal |
| Rectangle | Opposite sides equal AND diagonals equal |
| Rhombus | All four sides equal |
| Square | All four sides equal AND diagonals equal |

## Remember These

- The distance formula is just Pythagoras theorem on the coordinate plane -- horizontal and vertical gaps form the legs
- In the section formula, the **closer** point's coordinates get the **larger** weight from the farther endpoint
- Always use **absolute value** in the area formula to ensure a positive result
- For "find value of p if points are collinear" questions, set the area formula = 0 and solve
- The perpendicular bisector of a segment is the locus of points equidistant from both endpoints
