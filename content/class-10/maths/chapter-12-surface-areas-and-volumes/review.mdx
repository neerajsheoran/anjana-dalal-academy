## Key Concepts

- Real-world objects are **combinations** of basic solids (cylinder, cone, sphere, hemisphere, cuboid)
- **Surface area** of composite solid = sum of **exposed** surfaces only (exclude hidden/joined faces)
- **Volume** of composite solid = sum of volumes of all parts (no subtraction needed)
- When a solid is **melted and recast**, **volume is conserved** -- set volume of original = volume of new shape
- **Frustum** of a cone: the portion between the base and a plane cut parallel to the base
- Frustum has two circular faces with radii r1 (larger) and r2 (smaller), height h, slant height l
- Slant height of frustum: l = **root(h^2 + (r1 - r2)^2)**

## Essential Formulas

| Solid | CSA | TSA | Volume |
|-------|-----|-----|--------|
| Cylinder | 2 pi r h | 2 pi r(r + h) | pi r^2 h |
| Cone | pi r l | pi r(r + l) | (1/3) pi r^2 h |
| Sphere | -- | 4 pi r^2 | (4/3) pi r^3 |
| Hemisphere | 2 pi r^2 | 3 pi r^2 | (2/3) pi r^3 |
| Frustum | pi(r1+r2)l | pi(r1+r2)l + pi r1^2 + pi r2^2 | (1/3) pi h(r1^2 + r2^2 + r1.r2) |

**Cone slant height**: l = root(r^2 + h^2)

## Important Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **CSA** | Curved Surface Area (excludes flat faces) |
| **TSA** | Total Surface Area (all surfaces) |
| **Frustum** | Lower portion of a cone cut by a plane parallel to the base |
| **Composite solid** | Shape formed by combining two or more basic solids |

## Remember These

- For surface area, always ask: **which surfaces are visible?** Joined faces are internal and must be excluded
- For volume, simply **add all component volumes** -- there are no hidden volumes
- Unit conversions: **1 litre = 1000 cm^3**, 1 m^3 = 1000 litres, 1 cm^3 = 1 ml
- In water-flow problems: volume per second = cross-sectional area of pipe x flow speed
- A capsule = cylinder + 2 hemispheres; cylindrical height = total length - diameter (2r)
