## Key Concepts

- **Trigonometric ratios** are defined for acute angles in a right triangle
- sin P = opposite/hypotenuse, cos P = adjacent/hypotenuse, tan P = opposite/adjacent
- **Reciprocal ratios**: cosec P = 1/sin P, sec P = 1/cos P, cot P = 1/tan P
- **Quotient relations**: tan P = sin P/cos P, cot P = cos P/sin P
- Trigonometric ratios depend only on the **angle**, not the triangle's size (similar triangles give the same ratios)
- Knowing **one ratio** lets you find all others using Pythagoras theorem
- **Complementary angle relations** (P + Q = 90):
  - sin(90-P) = cos P, cos(90-P) = sin P
  - tan(90-P) = cot P, sec(90-P) = cosec P
- **Three fundamental identities** (all from Pythagoras theorem):
  - sin^2 P + cos^2 P = **1**
  - 1 + tan^2 P = **sec^2 P**
  - 1 + cot^2 P = **cosec^2 P**
- sin P and cos P always lie between **0 and 1** (inclusive) for 0 to 90 degrees
- As angle increases from 0 to 90: sin **increases**, cos **decreases**, tan **increases**
- To prove identities: start from the complex side, convert to sin and cos, apply the three identities

## Standard Values Table

| Angle | sin | cos | tan |
|-------|-----|-----|-----|
| 0 | 0 | 1 | 0 |
| 30 | 1/2 | root(3)/2 | 1/root(3) |
| 45 | 1/root(2) | 1/root(2) | 1 |
| 60 | root(3)/2 | 1/2 | root(3) |
| 90 | 1 | 0 | not defined |

**Memory trick for sin**: root(0)/2, root(1)/2, root(2)/2, root(3)/2, root(4)/2. For cos, read **backwards**.

## Important Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Hypotenuse** | Longest side, opposite the right angle |
| **Complementary angles** | Two angles adding up to 90 degrees |
| **Trigonometric identity** | An equation true for all permissible values of the angle |

## Remember These

- "sin" alone has no meaning -- it must be **sin of an angle** (sin 30, sin P, etc.)
- tan 90 and sec 90 are **not defined** (division by zero); similarly cosec 0 and cot 0
- The "co" in cosine, cotangent, cosecant stands for **complement** -- each equals the corresponding ratio of the complementary angle
- For identity proofs, if stuck, convert **everything** to sin and cos -- it almost always works
- sec^2 P - tan^2 P = 1 and cosec^2 P - cot^2 P = 1 are useful factorisation shortcuts
