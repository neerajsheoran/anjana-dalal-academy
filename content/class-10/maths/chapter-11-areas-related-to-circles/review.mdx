## Key Concepts

- Circumference = **2 pi r**, Area of circle = **pi r^2**
- A **sector** is bounded by two radii and an arc (like a pizza slice)
- A **segment** is bounded by a chord and an arc
- **Arc length** = (theta/360) x 2 pi r
- **Area of sector** = **(theta/360) x pi r^2**
- **Area of segment** = Area of sector **minus** Area of triangle formed by the two radii and the chord
- Area of triangle in a sector = **(1/2) r^2 sin(theta)**
- For theta = 90: triangle area = (1/2)r^2; for theta = 60: triangle area = (root(3)/4)r^2 (equilateral)
- **Major sector angle** = 360 - theta; **major sector area** = pi r^2 - minor sector area
- **Major segment area** = pi r^2 - minor segment area
- For combination figures: decompose into sectors, triangles, rectangles, semicircles, then add or subtract
- Use **pi = 22/7** unless the question specifies otherwise

## Key Formulas

| Quantity | Formula |
|----------|---------|
| Arc length | (theta/360) x 2 pi r |
| Sector area | (theta/360) x pi r^2 |
| Segment area | Sector area - Triangle area |
| Major sector area | pi r^2 - Minor sector area |

## Important Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Sector** | Region between two radii and an arc |
| **Minor sector** | Smaller sector (angle < 180) |
| **Major sector** | Larger sector (angle > 180) |
| **Segment** | Region between a chord and its arc |
| **Minor segment** | Smaller region (between chord and minor arc) |

## Remember These

- The fraction **(theta/360)** appears in both arc length and sector area formulas -- it represents what fraction of the full circle is covered
- **Segment = Sector - Triangle** is the single most important relationship in this chapter
- For a **quarter circle** (90-degree sector): area = pi r^2 / 4, arc = pi r / 2
- Clock problems: the minute hand sweeps 360 degrees in 60 minutes, so the angle in t minutes = 6t degrees
- Goat tethered to a corner of a rectangle grazes a **quarter circle** (90-degree sector)
