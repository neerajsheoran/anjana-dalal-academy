## Key Concepts

- **Theoretical probability**: P(E) = **Number of favourable outcomes / Total number of equally likely outcomes**
- This formula works **only** when all outcomes are equally likely
- **0 <= P(E) <= 1** for every event E
- P(E) = 0 means **impossible event**; P(E) = 1 means **certain event**
- **Complementary events**: P(E) + P(not E) = 1, so **P(not E) = 1 - P(E)**
- All elementary event probabilities sum to **1**
- An **elementary event** has exactly one outcome

## Important Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Random experiment** | Action with unpredictable result |
| **Outcome** | A possible result of an experiment |
| **Event** | A collection of outcomes |
| **Elementary event** | Event with exactly one outcome |
| **Complementary event** | "Not E" -- all outcomes not in E |
| **Equally likely** | No outcome is favoured over another |

## Remember These

- For 2 coins: HT and TH are **different** outcomes -- do not count them as one (common mistake)
- "At least one head" means HH, HT, TH (includes cases with more than one head); "exactly one head" means only HT, TH
- If your probability is **negative or greater than 1**, something is wrong -- recheck
- For "not E" questions, it is often easier to compute P(E) first and then subtract from 1
- Two dice: the outcomes are **ordered pairs** (die1, die2) -- (2,3) and (3,2) are distinct
