## Key Concepts

- **Oersted's discovery**: a current-carrying conductor produces a **magnetic field** around it
- **Magnetic field lines**: closed loops from N to S (outside magnet); never cross; closer lines = stronger field
- Straight wire carrying current: field lines are **concentric circles** around the wire
- **Right-Hand Thumb Rule**: thumb points in direction of current; curled fingers show direction of magnetic field
- A **circular loop** carrying current acts like a short bar magnet; field at centre is nearly uniform
- A **solenoid** (long coil) behaves like a **bar magnet**; uniform field inside (parallel lines)
- Solenoid + soft iron core = **electromagnet** (can be switched on/off, strength adjustable, polarity reversible)
- A current-carrying conductor in a magnetic field experiences a **force** (basis of the electric motor)
- **Fleming's Left-Hand Rule** (for motors/force): Forefinger = Field, Middle finger = Current, Thumb = Force/motion
- Force is maximum when current is **perpendicular** to the field; zero when **parallel**
- **Electric motor**: converts electrical energy → mechanical energy; uses a **split ring commutator** to reverse current every half turn for continuous rotation
- Motor parts: armature coil, permanent magnets, split ring commutator, carbon brushes
- **Electromagnetic induction** (Faraday): changing magnetic field through a coil **induces current**
- **Fleming's Right-Hand Rule** (for generators/induction): Forefinger = Field, Thumb = Motion, Middle finger = Induced current
- **Electric generator**: converts mechanical energy → electrical energy
- **AC generator** uses **slip rings** (complete rings) → alternating current
- **DC generator** uses **split ring commutator** → direct current
- India's mains supply: **AC, 220 V, 50 Hz** (current reverses direction 100 times per second)
- **Domestic circuit**: 3 wires — live (red, ~220 V), neutral (black, ~0 V), earth (green, safety)
- **Fuse**: melts and breaks circuit on overcurrent; **MCB**: trips automatically, can be reset
- **Earthing**: provides low-resistance path to ground if live wire touches metal casing
- **Short circuit**: live and neutral touch directly → very high current; **Overloading**: too many appliances on one circuit

## Important Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Magnetic field | Region around a magnet or current-carrying conductor where magnetic force acts |
| Solenoid | Long coil of wire that behaves like a bar magnet when current flows through it |
| Electromagnet | Solenoid with a soft iron core; a temporary, controllable magnet |
| Electric motor | Device that converts electrical energy into mechanical (rotational) energy |
| Electromagnetic induction | Production of electric current by changing the magnetic field linked with a conductor |
| Electric generator | Device that converts mechanical energy into electrical energy |
| Commutator | Split ring device that reverses current direction every half rotation in a motor/DC generator |

## Remember These

- **Left-Hand Rule** = motor/force (electricity → motion); **Right-Hand Rule** = generator/induction (motion → electricity)
- AC generator uses **slip rings**; DC generator uses **split ring commutator** — don't confuse them
- The **split ring commutator** in a motor ensures the coil keeps rotating in **one direction**
- Electromagnets are preferred over permanent magnets because they can be **switched off** and their **strength/polarity controlled**
- 50 Hz means the current changes direction **100 times** per second (2 direction changes per cycle)
