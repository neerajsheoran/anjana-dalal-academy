## Key Concepts

- **Life processes**: nutrition, respiration, transportation, excretion — keep organisms alive continuously
- **Autotrophic nutrition** (photosynthesis): 6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ (requires sunlight + chlorophyll)
- Photosynthesis steps: light absorption by chlorophyll → water splitting (releases O₂) → carbon fixation (builds glucose)
- **Stomata**: tiny pores on leaves for gas exchange; **guard cells** open/close them
- **Heterotrophic nutrition**: saprophytic (fungi on dead matter), parasitic (cuscuta on host), holozoic (ingestion + digestion)
- **Amoeba** feeds using **pseudopodia**; **Paramoecium** uses **cilia**
- **Human digestion**: Mouth (salivary amylase breaks starch) → Oesophagus (peristalsis) → Stomach (HCl + pepsin break proteins + mucus protects wall) → Small intestine (bile emulsifies fats + pancreatic juice + intestinal juice; **villi** absorb nutrients) → Large intestine (absorbs water) → Anus
- **Bile** from liver emulsifies fats but has **no enzymes**
- **Aerobic respiration** (in mitochondria): glucose → CO₂ + H₂O + ~38 ATP
- **Anaerobic respiration**: in yeast → ethanol + CO₂ (fermentation); in muscle cells → lactic acid (causes cramps)
- **Alveoli**: thin-walled balloon-like sacs in lungs; total surface area ~80 m²; site of gas exchange
- **Haemoglobin** in RBCs carries oxygen from lungs to tissues
- **Blood components**: plasma, RBCs, WBCs, platelets
- **Arteries**: thick-walled, carry blood away from heart; **Veins**: thin-walled with valves, carry blood to heart; **Capillaries**: one-cell thick, site of exchange
- **Double circulation**: pulmonary (heart → lungs → heart) + systemic (heart → body → heart); keeps oxygenated and deoxygenated blood separate
- **Xylem**: transports water and minerals upward (root pressure + transpiration pull); passive process
- **Phloem**: transports food (sucrose) bidirectionally by **translocation**; requires ATP (active process)
- **Kidneys** filter blood through ~1 million **nephrons** per kidney
- Urine formation: **filtration** (in glomerulus/Bowman's capsule) → **selective reabsorption** (in tubules) → excretion
- **Haemodialysis**: artificial kidney filters blood when kidneys fail
- Plants excrete through stomata (gases), transpiration (water), leaf fall, resins/gums, and vacuolar storage

## Important Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Autotroph | Organism that makes its own food (e.g., green plants) |
| Heterotroph | Organism that depends on others for food |
| Photosynthesis | Process by which plants make glucose using CO₂, water, sunlight, and chlorophyll |
| Peristalsis | Wave-like muscular contractions that push food through the alimentary canal |
| Villi | Finger-like projections in the small intestine that increase surface area for absorption |
| Aerobic respiration | Breakdown of glucose with oxygen in mitochondria to release energy |
| Anaerobic respiration | Breakdown of glucose without oxygen, yielding less energy |
| Double circulation | Blood passes through the heart twice in one complete cycle |
| Transpiration | Loss of water vapour from leaves through stomata |
| Translocation | Transport of food through phloem from leaves to other parts |
| Nephron | Functional unit of the kidney |
| Excretion | Removal of metabolic waste products from the body |

## Remember These

- Bile **emulsifies** fats but contains **no digestive enzymes**
- Aerobic respiration yields **~38 ATP**; anaerobic yields only **2 ATP** per glucose
- **Lactic acid** buildup causes muscle cramps during intense exercise
- Plants open stomata at night in deserts to conserve water (CAM pathway)
- Blood pressure: normal = **120/80 mm Hg**
