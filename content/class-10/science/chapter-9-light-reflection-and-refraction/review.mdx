## Key Concepts

- Light travels in **straight lines** (rectilinear propagation)
- **Laws of reflection**: angle of incidence = angle of reflection; incident ray, reflected ray, and normal lie in the same plane
- **Plane mirror** image: virtual, upright, same size, laterally inverted, at same distance behind mirror
- **Concave mirror**: reflecting surface curves inward; **converges** light; used in shaving mirrors, headlamps, solar concentrators
- **Convex mirror**: reflecting surface curves outward; **diverges** light; used in rear-view mirrors (wider field of view)
- **Key terms**: Pole (P), Centre of curvature (C), Radius of curvature (R), Principal focus (F), Focal length (f)
- **f = R/2** (focal length is half the radius of curvature)
- Concave mirror image depends on object position — can be real/virtual, enlarged/diminished/same size
- Convex mirror always forms: **virtual, upright, diminished** image
- **Mirror formula**: 1/v + 1/u = 1/f
- **Magnification (mirrors)**: m = h'/h = −v/u; positive m = upright (virtual); negative m = inverted (real)
- **Refraction**: bending of light when it passes from one medium to another (due to speed change)
- Light bends **towards normal** in denser medium; **away from normal** in rarer medium
- **Snell's Law**: n = sin i / sin r (refractive index)
- Refractive index: water ≈ 1.33, glass ≈ 1.5, diamond ≈ 2.42
- **Convex lens** (converging): thicker at centre; **Concave lens** (diverging): thinner at centre
- Convex lens image depends on object position (similar pattern to concave mirror)
- Concave lens always forms: **virtual, upright, diminished** image
- **Lens formula**: 1/v − 1/u = 1/f
- **Magnification (lenses)**: m = h'/h = v/u
- **Power of a lens**: P = 1/f (f in metres, P in dioptres D); convex = +ve power, concave = −ve power

## Important Formulas

| Formula | Applies To |
|---------|-----------|
| 1/v + 1/u = 1/f | Spherical mirrors |
| 1/v − 1/u = 1/f | Spherical lenses |
| m = −v/u | Mirror magnification |
| m = v/u | Lens magnification |
| f = R/2 | Mirrors (focal length and radius) |
| P = 1/f | Power of a lens (f in metres) |
| n = sin i / sin r | Snell's Law (refractive index) |

## Important Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Focal length (f) | Distance from pole/optical centre to the principal focus |
| Refractive index | Ratio of speed of light in vacuum to speed in the medium |
| Real image | Image formed by actual convergence of light rays; can be caught on a screen |
| Virtual image | Image from which rays only appear to diverge; cannot be caught on a screen |
| Power of a lens | Degree to which a lens converges or diverges light; measured in dioptres (D) |

## Remember These

- Mirror formula has **plus** (1/v + 1/u = 1/f); lens formula has **minus** (1/v − 1/u = 1/f)
- Mirror magnification has a **negative sign** (−v/u); lens magnification does **not** (v/u)
- For concave mirror, f is **negative**; for convex mirror, f is **positive** (New Cartesian convention)
- Convex lens with f = +0.5 m has power = **+2 D**; concave lens with f = −0.67 m has power = **−1.5 D**
- A coin in water appears **raised** and a pool appears **shallower** — both due to refraction
