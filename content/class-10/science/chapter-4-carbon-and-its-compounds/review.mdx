## Key Concepts

- Carbon forms **millions of compounds** due to **catenation** (forming C–C chains/rings) and **tetravalency** (bonding with 4 atoms)
- Carbon compounds have **covalent bonds** (shared electron pairs); they have low melting/boiling points and poor electrical conductivity
- **Single bond** (C–C): one shared pair; **Double bond** (C=C): two shared pairs; **Triple bond** (C≡C): three shared pairs
- **Allotropes of carbon**: diamond (hard, 3D lattice), graphite (soft, conducts electricity, layered), fullerene (C₆₀, cage-like)
- **Saturated hydrocarbons (alkanes)**: only single bonds; general formula **CₙH₂ₙ₊₂**
- **Unsaturated hydrocarbons**: alkenes (C=C, **CₙH₂ₙ**), alkynes (C≡C, **CₙH₂ₙ₋₂**)
- **Structural isomers**: same molecular formula, different arrangements (e.g., n-butane and isobutane)
- **Functional groups** determine chemical properties: –OH (alcohol), –CHO (aldehyde), –CO– (ketone), –COOH (carboxylic acid), –Cl/–Br (haloalkane)
- **Homologous series**: same functional group, each member differs by –CH₂– (14 u); similar chemical properties, gradual change in physical properties
- **IUPAC naming**: root word (C count: meth-, eth-, prop-, but-) + suffix for functional group (-ol, -al, -one, -oic acid)
- **Combustion**: carbon compounds + O₂ → CO₂ + H₂O + heat; saturated → clean blue flame; unsaturated → yellow, sooty flame
- **Oxidation**: alcohol → carboxylic acid (using alkaline KMnO₄ or acidified K₂Cr₂O₇)
- **Addition reaction**: unsaturated + H₂ → saturated (hydrogenation; converts vegetable oil → vanaspati ghee)
- **Substitution reaction**: saturated + Cl₂ (sunlight) → chlorinated compound + HCl
- **Ethanol** (C₂H₅OH): reacts with Na to give sodium ethoxide + H₂; dehydrated by conc. H₂SO₄ at 443 K to give ethene
- **Ethanoic acid** (CH₃COOH): 5–8% solution = vinegar; pure form freezes at 16.6°C = glacial acetic acid; weak acid
- **Esterification**: acid + alcohol → ester + water; **Saponification**: ester + NaOH → soap + alcohol
- **Soap** has hydrophilic head + hydrophobic tail; cleans by forming **micelles** that trap grease
- Soap fails in **hard water** (forms scum with Ca²⁺/Mg²⁺); **detergents** work in both hard and soft water

## Important Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Covalent bond | Bond formed by sharing of electron pairs |
| Catenation | Ability of carbon to form bonds with other carbon atoms |
| Tetravalency | Carbon's ability to form four bonds |
| Homologous series | Family of compounds with same functional group, differing by –CH₂– |
| Functional group | Atom or group of atoms that determines the chemical properties of a compound |
| Hydrogenation | Addition of H₂ to unsaturated compounds using a catalyst (Ni/Pd) |
| Esterification | Reaction of carboxylic acid with alcohol to form ester and water |
| Saponification | Hydrolysis of ester with NaOH to form soap (sodium salt of fatty acid) |
| Micelle | Spherical cluster of soap molecules trapping grease inside |

## Remember These

- **Methanol** (CH₃OH) is highly toxic — even small amounts cause blindness or death
- Saturated compounds undergo **substitution**; unsaturated compounds undergo **addition**
- Soap cannot test for hard water if replaced by detergent — detergents don't form scum
- Black soot on cooking vessels = **incomplete combustion** (adjust air supply)
- Denatured alcohol = ethanol + methanol + dye (made undrinkable)
