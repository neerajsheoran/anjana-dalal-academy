## Key Concepts

- **Perimeter** = total distance around the boundary of a flat shape (measured in cm, m, km)
- **Area** = total flat surface enclosed within a shape (measured in cm², m², km²)
- **Perimeter of rectangle** = **2(l + b)**
- **Perimeter of square** = **4s**
- **Perimeter of triangle** = **a + b + c**; equilateral triangle = **3s**
- **Perimeter of any polygon** = sum of all side lengths
- **Area of rectangle** = **l x b**
- **Area of square** = **s²**
- Area by counting squares on grid paper: full square = 1, half-covered = 1/2, barely touching = skip
- Among rectangles with the **same perimeter**, the **square encloses the greatest area**
- Among rectangles with the **same area**, the one closest to a square has the **smallest perimeter**
- **Compound shapes** (L, T, U-shaped): split into simple rectangles, calculate each, then add
- Alternative for compound shapes: calculate full enclosing rectangle minus the missing pieces
- To find a **missing side**: rearrange the perimeter or area formula

## Important Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Perimeter** | Total distance around the boundary of a flat shape |
| **Area** | Total flat surface enclosed within a shape |
| **Unit square** | A square of side 1 unit; building block for measuring area |
| **cm²** | Square centimetre -- a unit of area |
| **m²** | Square metre -- a unit of area |
| **Hectare** | Land area unit = 10,000 m² |
| **Compound shape** | A shape formed by joining two or more simple shapes |

| 1 Hectare | 10,000 m² |
| 1 km² | 100 hectares |

## Remember These

- Perimeter is a **length** (cm, m); area is a **surface** (cm², m²) -- never mix up units
- Same perimeter does NOT mean same area, and same area does NOT mean same perimeter
- For maximum area with fixed fencing, always choose a **square**
- When finding a missing side from perimeter: divide perimeter by 2 first (for rectangles), then subtract the known side
- For compound shapes, always try BOTH methods (adding parts vs. subtracting missing parts) to verify
