## Key Concepts

- India has about **6 lakh villages**, **8,000 towns**, and **4,000+ cities**
- About **two-thirds** of Indians live in rural areas
- **Panchayati Raj** is India's system of local government for villages
- The system is **three-tiered** (village → block → district)

## The Three Tiers

| Level | Name |
|---|---|
| **Village** | Gram Panchayat |
| **Block** | Panchayat Samiti / Block Panchayat / Mandal Parishad |
| **District** | Zila Parishad |

## Gram Sabha and Gram Panchayat

- **Gram Sabha** — all adult villagers registered as voters
- The Gram Sabha **elects** members of the Gram Panchayat
- Head of the Gram Panchayat is called **Sarpanch** or **Pradhan**
- Supported by **Panchayat Secretary** (admin) and **Patwari** (land records)

## What Does the Panchayat Do?

- Agriculture support
- Roads and infrastructure
- Water resources management
- Education (primary schools)
- Healthcare (primary health centres)
- Social welfare
- Cultural activities
- Resolving local disputes

## Three Inspiring Sarpanchs

### Dnyaneshwar Kamble (Maharashtra)
- Transgender Sarpanch of Tarangfal village
- Motto: **lok seva, gram seva** ("Service to the village is service to the public")

### Vandana Bahadur Maida (Madhya Pradesh)
- Tribal woman (Bhil community), first female Sarpanch of her village
- Worked on education and sanitation

### Popatrao Pawar (Maharashtra)
- Transformed drought-affected Hiware Bazar village
- Used rainwater harvesting, watershed conservation, tree planting
- **Padma Shri** in 2020

## Child-Friendly Panchayats

- **Bal Sabhas** (children's assemblies) and **Bal Panchayats** (children's panchayats) give children a voice
- Maharashtra Bal Panchayats: ended child labour, prevented child marriage, brought children back to school
- **Sangkhu Radhu Khandu, Sikkim**: declared child-friendly Panchayat (school walls, hygienic kitchens)
- **Children's Parliament, Rajasthan** (Bunker Roy's Barefoot College): won World's Children's Honorary Award in 2001

## Reservation in Panchayats

- **One-third of seats reserved for women** (50% in many states)
- Seats also reserved for **Scheduled Castes (SCs)** and **Scheduled Tribes (STs)**
- Has empowered women, Dalits, tribals to become elected leaders

## Mahatma Gandhi's Vision

> "The real India lives in its villages."

Gandhi believed in **Gram Swaraj** — village self-rule.

## Ancient Indian Tradition

- The **Arthashastra** by **Kautilya** (~2,300 years ago) described a similar four-tier system
- Local self-governance has been an Indian practice for thousands of years

## Common Confusions

- **Gram Sabha vs Gram Panchayat:** The Gram Sabha is the assembly of ALL adult villagers — it is the source of authority. The Gram Panchayat is the elected council that the Gram Sabha chooses.
- **Sarpanch is just one of the panchayat members:** Although the Sarpanch leads, important decisions are made together by the panchayat.
- **Panchayat does not handle ALL village matters:** Major issues (serious crime, large-scale floods, big infrastructure) are still handled by the state or central government. The panchayat handles **local matters**.
- **Reservations don't mean unqualified people win:** They ensure that women, SCs, and STs get representation. The elected people still have to work hard and earn the villagers' trust.
