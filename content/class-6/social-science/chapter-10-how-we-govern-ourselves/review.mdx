## Key Concepts

- **Governance** = process of making rules and ensuring they are followed
- **Government** = the system or group that does governance
- **Laws** = the most important and binding rules
- Without rules, society cannot function

## The Three Organs of Government

### 1. Legislature — Makes laws
- **National**: Parliament (Lok Sabha + Rajya Sabha)
- **State**: Vidhan Sabha (+ Vidhan Parishad in some states)
- Made up of elected representatives

### 2. Executive — Implements laws
- **National**: led by President (nominal head) + Prime Minister (real head)
- **State**: led by Governor (nominal) + Chief Minister (real)
- Includes ministers and agencies like police

### 3. Judiciary — Interprets laws, delivers justice
- **Supreme Court** (highest, national)
- **High Court** (each state)
- District and local courts

## Separation of Powers

- The three organs are kept **separate** but work together
- Provides **checks and balances** — each can check the others
- Prevents any one group from becoming too powerful

## The Three Levels of Government

| Level | Handles |
|---|---|
| **Central (Union)** | Defence, foreign affairs, currency, atomic energy, inter-state trade |
| **State** | Police, public health, agriculture, irrigation, education |
| **Local** | Water supply, garbage, local roads, parks |

A small flood → handled by local government.
A bigger flood → state government steps in.
A massive flood → central government may also help.

## Cybercrime Example — All Three Organs in Action

- **Legislature** passes a law against cybercrime
- **Executive** (cyber police) catches the criminal
- **Judiciary** (courts) decides guilt and punishment

## Democracy

- **Demos** (people) + **kratos** (rule) = "rule of the people"
- **Representative democracy** — people elect representatives who govern
- **Direct democracy** — people vote on issues directly (like classroom voting)
- **Grassroots democracy** — democracy involving ordinary citizens at the local level

## India as a Democracy

- World's **largest democracy** — about **970 million voters** (2024)
- Every citizen aged **18+** can vote
- Elected representatives: **MPs** (Members of Parliament) at national level, **MLAs** (Members of Legislative Assembly) at state level

- **Government of India**: **Satyameva Jayate** — "Truth alone triumphs"
- **Supreme Court of India**: **Yato Dharmastato Jayah** — "Where there is dharma, there is victory"

## Common Confusions

- **President vs Prime Minister**: The President is the **nominal head** (mostly ceremonial). The Prime Minister is the **real executive head** who actually runs the government.
- **Lok Sabha vs Rajya Sabha**: Both are houses of Parliament. Lok Sabha (House of the People) has members directly elected by people. Rajya Sabha (Council of States) has members elected by state legislatures.
- **Court system isn't part of the government?** It IS — the judiciary is one of the three organs of government. People sometimes think the courts are "outside" the government, but they are a vital part of it.
- **Local government is not less important than central:** Local government handles day-to-day issues that affect citizens most directly. The level isn't a measure of importance — different levels just do different jobs.
