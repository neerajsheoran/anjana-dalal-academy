## Key Concepts

- The **economy** is the entire web of economic activities in a country
- Economic activities are divided into **three sectors**

## The Three Sectors

### 1. Primary Sector — Natural Resources

Activities that depend directly on nature.

| Activity | Example |
|---|---|
| Agriculture | Growing rice, wheat, vegetables |
| Livestock rearing | Cows for milk, sheep for wool |
| Fishing | Catching fish from rivers and seas |
| Forestry | Gathering wood, fruits, medicinal plants |
| Mining | Coal, iron ore, salt |

### 2. Secondary Sector — Making Goods

Transforms raw materials into finished products.

| Activity | Example |
|---|---|
| Manufacturing | Cars, mobile phones, clothes, furniture |
| Construction | Houses, roads, bridges |
| Power generation | Electricity from coal, hydro, solar |
| Utilities | Water supply, gas |

**Indian manufacturing scale (2022):**
- Two-wheelers: 2 crores
- Cars: 45 lakhs
- Trucks: 10.3 lakhs

### 3. Tertiary Sector — Services

Provides services rather than goods.

| Activity | Example |
|---|---|
| Transport | Trucks, buses, trains, autos |
| Trade | Shops, markets, e-commerce |
| Banking | Banks, ATMs, payments |
| Healthcare | Doctors, nurses, hospitals |
| Education | Teachers, schools, colleges |
| IT and software | Software development, app companies |
| Hospitality | Hotels, restaurants |
| Government | Police, fire, courts |

## How the Three Sectors Are Connected

The classic example: from a wheat seed to your roti
- **Primary**: Farmer grows wheat
- **Secondary**: Mill grinds wheat into flour
- **Tertiary**: Truck carries flour, shop sells it, your family buys it

Each sector adds value. All three are needed.

## AMUL — A Great Indian Example

- **Founded in 1946** in Anand district, Gujarat
- Started after Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel advised farmers to form a **cooperative**
- Farmers sell milk directly to AMUL (primary)
- AMUL processes the milk into many products (secondary)
- AMUL transports and sells these products across India (tertiary)
- Today AMUL is one of the **world's largest dairy cooperatives**
- India is the **world's largest milk producer**

## Where Is India's Economy Today?

| Sector | % of workers | % of national income |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | ~40% | ~20% |
| Secondary | ~25% | ~25% |
| Tertiary | ~35% | over 50% |

**Services (tertiary) is now the largest contributor** to India's GDP. But **agriculture (primary) still employs the most people**.

## Key Modern Initiatives

- **Make in India** — boost manufacturing
- **Agricultural modernisation** — better seeds, irrigation, marketing
- **Digital economy** — UPI, e-commerce, food delivery, ride-sharing apps

## Kautilya's Wisdom

From the Arthashastra (~2,300 years ago):

> "The root of prosperity is economic activity, the lack of it brings material distress."

A strong economy is the foundation of a strong country.

## Common Confusions

- **A factory worker is in the secondary sector** (making things), not primary. Primary means working directly with nature.
- **Even a teacher is in the tertiary sector** — even though teaching feels different from running a shop. Teaching is a service, hence tertiary.
- **All three sectors employ people** — neither is "better" than the others. A modern country needs strong primary, secondary, AND tertiary sectors.
- **AMUL is not a private company in the usual sense** — it is a cooperative, owned by the farmers themselves. The profits go back to the farmers, not to outside shareholders.
- **More people work in agriculture than services in India** — but services generate more national income. This is because each farmer produces a small share of GDP, while services per worker produce more.
