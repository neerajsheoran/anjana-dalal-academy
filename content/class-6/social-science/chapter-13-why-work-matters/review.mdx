## Key Concepts

- **Work** = any activity that produces value
- Two big categories: **Economic** and **Non-economic** activities

## Economic Activities

- Involve **money** or money's worth
- Examples: farmer selling vegetables, lawyer charging a fee, driver getting paid for transport, factory workers earning salaries
- Payment can be in different forms:
  - **Salary** — fixed monthly payment
  - **Wage** — payment for a specific period (often daily/hourly)
  - **Fee** — for professional services
  - **Payment in kind** — paid in goods (e.g., rice) instead of money

## Non-Economic Activities

- Done **without payment**, out of love, care, duty, or service
- Examples: mother cooking for family, helping with homework, caring for grandparents, volunteering, seva at gurudwaras
- Don't involve money but are **deeply valuable**

## Three Types of Economic Activities

### 1. Primary Activities
Working directly with natural resources:
- Farming
- Fishing
- Mining
- Forestry

### 2. Secondary Activities
Turning raw materials into finished goods:
- Manufacturing (cars, clothes, electronics)
- Construction (buildings, roads)
- Industrial work

### 3. Tertiary Activities
Providing services:
- Teaching, healthcare
- Banking
- Transport, IT services
- Shops and restaurants

## Value Addition Example

**Rajesh, the carpenter:**
- Buys wood for **₹600**
- Makes a chair
- Sells the chair for **₹1,000**
- The **₹400 difference** = value added by his skill, time, and effort

Every economic activity adds value at each step.

## The Importance of Non-Economic Work

Some examples of valuable unpaid work:
- **Care within families** — cooking, cleaning, caring for the young and old
- **Seva** — selfless service (langar at gurudwaras, prasad at temples)
- **Community movements** — Swachh Bharat Abhiyan, Van Mahotsav, donation drives
- Without unpaid family and community work, society would collapse

## Swami Vivekananda's Idea

> "When you are doing any work, do not think of anything beyond. Do it as worship, as the highest worship, and devote your whole life to it for the time being."

**Work itself is the highest worship** when done with full attention and care.

## Common Confusions

- **"Work" doesn't only mean a job in office.** Cooking, studying, caring for elders, volunteering — all are work.
- **Non-economic work isn't "less important":** Just because no money changes hands doesn't mean the work has less value. Often the opposite.
- **Primary, secondary, tertiary:** Primary = raw natural resources. Secondary = making goods. Tertiary = providing services.
- **Payment in kind is real payment:** A farm labourer who receives mangoes for his work has been paid — just in goods rather than money.
- **Value addition isn't about cheating customers:** Adding ₹400 of value to ₹600 wood by making a chair is real value creation, not "tricking" the buyer. The buyer is paying for both the materials and the skill.
