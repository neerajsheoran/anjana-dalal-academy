## Key Concepts

- Earth is called the **Blue Planet** because about **three-fourths** of its surface is water
- Only about **one-fourth** of Earth's surface is land
- About **97%** of all water on Earth is salty (oceans); only about **3%** is fresh water
- Most fresh water is locked in **glaciers and ice caps**

## The Five Oceans (Largest to Smallest)

1. **Pacific Ocean** — the largest
2. **Atlantic Ocean**
3. **Indian Ocean**
4. **Southern Ocean** (Antarctic Ocean)
5. **Arctic Ocean** — the smallest

All five oceans are **connected** — the boundaries between them are just conventions.

## India and the Indian Ocean

- India lies on the **northern edge** of the Indian Ocean
- **Arabian Sea** — part of the Indian Ocean to the **west** of India
- **Bay of Bengal** — part of the Indian Ocean to the **east** of India
- Two important island groups:
  - **Andaman and Nicobar Islands** (in the Bay of Bengal)
  - **Lakshadweep Islands** (in the Arabian Sea)

## Continents (Most Common Count: Seven)

In order of size:
1. **Asia** — largest, contains India
2. **Africa**
3. **North America**
4. **South America**
5. **Antarctica** — ice-covered, no permanent population
6. **Europe**
7. **Australia** — smallest

The five inhabited continents are represented by the **Olympic rings**.

## Marine Life

- **Marine flora** = plant life in the sea (algae, seaweeds)
- **Marine fauna** = animal life in the sea (fish, dolphins, whales, sharks, corals)

## Oceans and the Weather

- Ocean water evaporates → forms clouds → falls as rain
- **Monsoon rains** in India come from the Indian Ocean
- Without the monsoon, Indian agriculture would not survive

## Ocean Disasters

- **Cyclones** — violent storms from warm ocean water; common on India's east coast (Bay of Bengal)
- **Tsunamis** — huge waves caused by underwater earthquakes
- The **2004 Indian Ocean Tsunami** killed over 2 lakh people across 14 countries; severely affected Andaman and Nicobar Islands, Tamil Nadu, Kerala
- After 2004, India joined the **Indian Ocean Tsunami Warning System**
- **National Disaster Management Authority** in India handles all kinds of disasters

## Common Confusions

- **Sea vs Ocean:** A sea is usually smaller and partly enclosed by land (like the Arabian Sea). An ocean is larger and more open. The Arabian Sea is technically part of the Indian Ocean.
- **Continent count:** It can be 4, 5, 6, or 7 depending on how you group landmasses. Seven is the most common system used in schools.
- **Drinking ocean water:** Even though Earth has lots of water, ocean water is salty and not drinkable. Fresh water is rare and precious.
