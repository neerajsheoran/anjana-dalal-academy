## Key Concepts

- A **star** is a huge ball of hot gas (hydrogen and helium) that produces light and heat through **nuclear fusion**
- The **Sun** is our nearest star — ~150 million km away; light takes ~8 min 20 sec to reach Earth
- **Light year** = distance light travels in one year ≈ 9.46 lakh crore km — used to measure vast cosmic distances
- **Constellations** are recognised patterns of stars; IAU recognises **88 constellations**
- Key constellations: **Orion** (3 belt stars), **Ursa Major / Saptarishi** (Big Dipper), **Canis Major** (contains Sirius, brightest star)
- **Pole Star (Dhruva Tara / Polaris)** stays nearly fixed above Earth's North Pole — locate it using the pointer stars of the Big Dipper
- Indian sky system divides the Moon's path into **27 nakshatras**; **Jantar Mantar** observatories measured celestial positions without telescopes
- **Solar System**: Sun + 8 planets + moons + asteroids + comets, held by gravity
- Planet order: **Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune** (Meri Very Energetic Maa Joyfully Saw Uttar Nakshatra)
- **Rotation** = spinning on axis (causes day/night); **Revolution** = orbiting the Sun (defines a year)
- **Inner planets** (Mercury–Mars): small, rocky; **Outer planets** (Jupiter–Neptune): large, gaseous
- **Venus** is the hottest planet (runaway greenhouse effect) and brightest object after Moon — called Morning/Evening Star but is a planet
- **Pluto** was reclassified as a **dwarf planet** in 2006
- **Moon**: Earth's natural satellite, ~3.84 lakh km away, no atmosphere, gravity = 1/6 of Earth's, reflects sunlight
- **Moon phases** cycle over ~29.5 days: New Moon → Waxing Crescent → First Quarter → Full Moon → Waning → repeat
- **Asteroids**: rocky bodies mostly in the **Asteroid Belt** (between Mars and Jupiter)
- **Comets**: icy "dirty snowballs" with tails that always point **away from the Sun**; **Halley's Comet** returns every ~76 years (next: 2061)
- **Milky Way (Akasha Ganga)**: our spiral galaxy with 200–400 billion stars; Sun is ~26,000 light years from centre
- **Universe**: ~13.8 billion years old, originated in the **Big Bang**, still expanding

## Important Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Star** | Massive sphere of hot gas producing light/heat through nuclear fusion |
| **Light year** | Distance light travels in one year (~9.46 x 10¹² km) |
| **Constellation** | A recognised pattern of stars in the sky |
| **Planet** | Large body orbiting a star, nearly spherical, that has cleared its orbital zone |
| **Rotation** | Spinning of an object on its own axis (causes day and night) |
| **Revolution** | Movement of one body around another (e.g., Earth around Sun = one year) |
| **Natural satellite** | A natural body orbiting a planet (Moon orbits Earth) |
| **Asteroid** | Rocky object orbiting the Sun, smaller than a planet |
| **Comet** | Icy, dusty body with a glowing tail when near the Sun |
| **Galaxy** | Vast system of billions of stars held together by gravity |
| **Exoplanet** | A planet orbiting a star other than our Sun |

## Remember These

- The Moon does **not produce its own light** — moonlight is reflected sunlight
- **Venus** is hotter than Mercury despite being farther from the Sun, because of its thick CO₂ atmosphere (greenhouse effect)
- A comet's tail always points **away from the Sun** (blown by solar wind), not behind the comet
- **Chandrayaan-3 (2023)**: India became the **first nation to land near the Moon's south pole**; landing site named **Shiv Shakti Point**
- ISRO's **Gaganyaan** will send Indian astronauts (called **Vyomanauts**) into orbit
