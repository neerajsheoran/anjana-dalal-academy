## Key Concepts

- **Natural resources** are materials and energy sources provided by nature that all living organisms depend on
- **Air composition**: Nitrogen ~78%, Oxygen ~21%, Argon ~0.9%, CO₂ ~0.04%, plus variable water vapour
- Oxygen powers **respiration**; CO₂ fuels **photosynthesis**; nitrogen is essential for building **proteins**
- Only **3%** of Earth's water is freshwater; most of that is locked in glaciers — accessible freshwater is very scarce
- **Water cycle**: Evaporation → Condensation (clouds form) → Precipitation (rain/snow) → Collection → repeat
- Ancient Indian water conservation: **Stepwells (baoli/vav)**, **kunds**, **johads**, **bamboo drip irrigation** (Meghalaya)
- **Rainwater harvesting** collects rooftop rain into storage tanks or recharge pits — compulsory in Chennai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad
- The **Sun** is the primary energy source for nearly all life on Earth
- Solar energy devices: **solar panels** (electricity), **solar cookers** (cooking), **solar water heaters** (hot water)
- **Forests** provide fresh air, clean water, soil protection, climate stability, biodiversity, and livelihoods
- **Decomposers** break down dead matter on the forest floor into **humus**, enriching the soil
- **Deforestation** causes habitat loss, soil erosion, reduced rainfall, and accelerated global warming
- **Chipko Movement (1973)** — villagers in Garhwal hugged trees to stop logging; "Chipko" means "to cling"
- **Soil** forms over centuries through **weathering** of rocks + decomposition of organic matter
- Soil types: **Sandy** (drains fast, Rajasthan), **Clayey** (holds water, good for rice), **Loamy** (balanced, most fertile)
- **Fossil fuels** (coal, petroleum, natural gas) formed from ancient organisms over millions of years — **non-renewable**
- **Renewable resources** (sunlight, wind, water, forests) regenerate naturally but can still be degraded if misused

## Important Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Natural resource** | Material or energy source found in nature, used by living organisms |
| **Renewable resource** | Resource that nature regenerates continuously (sunlight, wind, water) |
| **Non-renewable resource** | Resource that took millions of years to form and cannot be replaced once used up |
| **Fossil fuel** | Energy-rich substance (coal, petroleum, natural gas) formed from ancient organisms |
| **Deforestation** | Large-scale removal or clearing of forest cover |
| **Humus** | Dark, nutrient-rich material in soil from decomposition of organic matter |
| **Weathering** | Gradual breakdown of rocks by wind, water, temperature, and biological activity |
| **Rainwater harvesting** | Collection and storage of rainwater for later use or groundwater recharge |
| **CNG** | Compressed Natural Gas — cleaner vehicle fuel |
| **LPG** | Liquefied Petroleum Gas — widely used cooking fuel |

## Renewable vs Non-Renewable Comparison

| Renewable | Non-Renewable |
|-----------|---------------|
| Sunlight, wind, water, forests, soil | Coal, petroleum, natural gas, minerals |
| Nature regenerates continuously | Took millions of years to form |
| Available indefinitely if used wisely | Gone once exhausted |

## Remember These

- Even **renewable resources** can be degraded beyond recovery if misused (forests cleared faster than regrown, rivers polluted beyond self-cleaning)
- **Petroleum** gives us petrol, diesel, kerosene, LPG, bitumen, plastics, and medicines — all from one raw material
- Earthworms are called **"farmer's best friends"** because their castings are rich natural fertiliser and their tunnelling aerates soil
- India's **Bhadla Solar Park** (Rajasthan) is one of the world's largest solar installations
- **Rajendra Singh** revived johads in Rajasthan and earned the title **"Waterman of India"**
