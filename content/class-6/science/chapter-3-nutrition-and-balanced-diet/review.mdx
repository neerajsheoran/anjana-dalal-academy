## Key Concepts

- **Nutrients** are chemical substances in food used by the body for energy, growth, repair, and protection
- There are **7 nutrient groups**: carbohydrates, proteins, fats, vitamins, minerals, dietary fibre, and water
- **Carbohydrates** are the body's primary fuel — found in rice, wheat, potato, sugar, fruits
- **Proteins** are the body's building blocks — found in dal, pulses, eggs, milk, paneer, fish
- **Fats** provide concentrated energy (more than double that of carbohydrates) and help absorb vitamins A, D, E, K
- **Vitamins** are needed in small amounts but regulate vital processes (A for eyesight, C for immunity, D for bones)
- **Minerals**: calcium for bones, iron for haemoglobin, iodine for thyroid, phosphorus for energy
- **Dietary fibre** (roughage) adds bulk to food and helps intestines push food along — prevents constipation
- **Water** makes up two-thirds of body weight and is needed for all chemical reactions in the body
- A **balanced diet** provides all 7 nutrient groups in appropriate quantities for a person's age and activity level
- **Deficiency diseases** are caused by prolonged lack of a specific nutrient — they are entirely preventable
- Being overweight can also be **malnutrition** — eating too many chips/biscuits but lacking vitamins and minerals is "hidden hunger"
- Cooking kills germs, makes nutrients accessible, and improves absorption — but overcooking destroys vitamins
- **Food preservation** methods: sun-drying, pickling, salting/sugaring, refrigeration, boiling and sealing

## Important Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Nutrients** | Chemical substances in food used for energy, growth, repair, and regulation |
| **Carbohydrates** | Energy-providing nutrients found in grains, potatoes, fruits, sugar |
| **Proteins** | Body-building nutrients found in dal, eggs, milk, meat |
| **Fats** | Concentrated energy nutrients found in oils, ghee, nuts, seeds |
| **Vitamins** | Nutrients needed in small amounts to regulate body processes |
| **Minerals** | Inorganic nutrients (calcium, iron, iodine) essential for body functions |
| **Dietary fibre** | Indigestible plant material that aids digestion |
| **Balanced diet** | A diet with all nutrients in appropriate proportions |
| **Deficiency disease** | A disease caused by prolonged lack of a specific nutrient |
| **Malnutrition** | A condition from eating too little, too much, or the wrong combination of nutrients |

## Deficiency Diseases Table

| Disease | Nutrient Missing | Key Symptom |
|---------|-----------------|-------------|
| **Night blindness** | Vitamin A | Cannot see in dim light |
| **Scurvy** | Vitamin C | Bleeding gums, slow healing |
| **Rickets** | Vitamin D + Calcium | Soft, bent bones in children |
| **Anaemia** | Iron | Tiredness, pale skin, breathlessness |
| **Goitre** | Iodine | Swollen neck (enlarged thyroid) |
| **Kwashiorkor** | Protein (severe) | Swollen belly, thin limbs |
| **Marasmus** | Protein + Calories | Extreme thinness, wasted muscles |

## Nutrient Tests

| Test | Detects | Positive Result |
|------|---------|-----------------|
| **Iodine test** | Starch | Blue-black colour |
| **Paper rub test** | Fat | Translucent greasy patch that does not dry |
| **Biuret test** | Protein | Violet/purple colour |

## Remember These

- **Vitamins A, D, E, K** are fat-soluble — without fat in the diet, the body cannot absorb them
- **Iodised salt** prevents goitre — this is why the government promotes it across India
- **Kwashiorkor** = some calories but no protein (swollen belly); **Marasmus** = lacking both calories and protein (extreme thinness)
- Quality and **variety** matter more than quantity — three plates of plain rice still means deficiency
- Replacing whole wheat roti with **maida** products removes fibre and causes digestive problems
