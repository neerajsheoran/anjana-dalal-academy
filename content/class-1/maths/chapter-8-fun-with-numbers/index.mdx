## Place Value — Numbers 21 to 30 (Packing Laddoos)

Rohan and his Dadi are packing laddoos. Each box can hold **10 laddoos**.

**Example:** 21 laddoos
- Put 10 laddoos in one box — **1 Ten**
- Put another 10 laddoos in a second box — **1 Ten**
- Total = **2 Tens**
- Now 1 laddoo is left — **1 One**

So:
- How many Tens? **2 Tens**
- How many Ones? **1 One**
- Number = **21** (2 Tens and 1 One)

Kavita is counting flowers.

**Example:** 34 flowers

- 34 means **3 Tens and 4 Ones**

## Numbers 51 to 100

Similarly, we can write other numbers using Tens and Ones.

**Example:** 56
- **5 Tens** and **6 Ones**
- 56 means 5 Tens and 6 Ones

More examples:
- 54 = 5 Tens + 4 Ones
- 59 = 5 Tens + 9 Ones
- 61 = 6 Tens + 1 One
- 67 = 6 Tens + 7 Ones
- 72 = 7 Tens + 2 Ones
- 77 = 7 Tens + 7 Ones
- 83 = 8 Tens + 3 Ones
- 88 = 8 Tens + 8 Ones
- 95 = 9 Tens + 5 Ones
- 99 = 9 Tens + 9 Ones

### Special Number: 100

- **10 Tens** and **0 Ones**
- 100 = **One Hundred**

> **100** is a very special number! It is made of **10 groups of ten**. We call it **one hundred**.

## Finding Missing Numbers

Numbers always follow a fixed order. If a few are hidden, we can figure them out!

- 42, **43**, 44, **45**, 46
- 77, 78, **79**, **80**, 81
- 95, 96, **97**, 98, **99**, 100

> **Going backwards is fun too!** 50, 49, 48, 47, 46, 45...

---

## Counting Backwards

We can also say numbers from big to small. This is called **counting backwards** — like a rocket countdown!

- From 30: 30, 29, 28, 27, 26, 25...
- From 50: 50, 49, 48, 47, 46, 45...
- From 100: 100, 99, 98, 97, 96, 95...

## Key Words to Remember

| Word | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Tens** | Groups of 10 — the number 34 has 3 tens |
| **Ones** | What is left after making groups of 10 — the number 34 has 4 ones |
| **Place value** | The value of a digit based on its position (Ones place or Tens place) |
| **Hundred** | The number 100 — the same as 10 tens |
| **Counting backwards** | Saying numbers from big to small (like 10, 9, 8, 7...) |
