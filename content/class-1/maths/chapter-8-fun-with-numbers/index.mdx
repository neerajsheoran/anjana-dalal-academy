## Understanding Tens and Ones

Every number is built from two things — **tens** and **ones**. Think of tens like full bags, and ones like the loose items left over.

- **10** = 1 ten and 0 ones. One full bag, nothing loose.
- **23** = 2 tens and 3 ones. Two full bags and 3 loose items.
- **35** = 3 tens and 5 ones.
- **47** = 4 tens and 7 ones.

Imagine a **bead string** (mala) with beads grouped in sets of 10. For the number 23, slide over **2 groups of 10 beads** and then **3 more beads**.

| Number | Tens | Ones |
|--------|------|------|
| 21 | 2 | 1 |
| 29 | 2 | 9 |
| 30 | 3 | 0 |
| 34 | 3 | 4 |
| 41 | 4 | 1 |
| 50 | 5 | 0 |

> **Try this:** Break the number **38** into tens and ones. It has **3 tens and 8 ones**!

## Finding Missing Numbers

Numbers always follow a fixed order. If a few are hidden, we can figure them out!

- 42, **43**, 44, **45**, 46
- 77, 78, **79**, **80**, 81
- 95, 96, **97**, 98, **99**, 100

> **Going backwards is fun too!** 50, 49, 48, 47, 46, 45...

---

## Counting Backwards

We can also say numbers from big to small. This is called **counting backwards** — like a rocket countdown!

- From 30: 30, 29, 28, 27, 26, 25...
- From 50: 50, 49, 48, 47, 46, 45...
- From 100: 100, 99, 98, 97, 96, 95...

A bead string (mala) is a great tool to slide beads one by one while counting forwards or backwards.

## Madhubani Painting

**Madhubani painting** is a beautiful folk art from Bihar. Artists use bright colours and simple shapes like **lines**, **circles**, and **curves** to draw fish, peacocks, flowers, and the sun.

You can count the fish, flowers, and birds inside a Madhubani painting!

> **Activity:** Try drawing your own Madhubani fish using circles for the eyes, triangles for the tail, and curved lines for the scales!

## Key Words to Remember

| Word | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Tens** | Groups of 10 — the number 34 has 3 tens |
| **Ones** | What is left after making groups of 10 — the number 34 has 4 ones |
| **Bead string** | A mala with beads used for counting forwards and backwards |
| **Hundred** | The number 100 — the same as 10 tens |
| **Counting backwards** | Saying numbers from big to small (like 10, 9, 8, 7...) |
| **Madhubani painting** | A folk art from Bihar using bright colours and simple shapes |
