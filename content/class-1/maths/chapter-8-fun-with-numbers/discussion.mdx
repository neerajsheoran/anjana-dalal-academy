## Packing Oranges — Numbers 21 to 30

**Child:** Maa, today teacher told us to count to 30. But I always get confused after 20!

**Mother:** That is okay, beta! Let me show you a trick. Do you see those bangles in my box?

**Child:** Yes, Maa! So many bangles!

**Mother:** Let us put them in groups of 10. Here — one group of 10, and another group of 10. That is 20 bangles.

**Child:** And there are still some left!

**Mother:** Yes! 1 more bangle makes 21 — twenty-one. 2 more makes 22 — twenty-two. See? After twenty, we just say "twenty" and then the extra bangles!

**Child:** Oh! So twenty-three means 20 and 3 more?

**Mother:** Exactly! All the way up to twenty-nine. And when we add one more to 29, we get a new group of 10. Now we have 3 groups of 10 — that is 30, which we call **thirty**.

**Child:** Maa, that is so easy! Packing bangles teaches numbers!

## Understanding Tens and Ones

**Child:** Maa, teacher drew some boxes with dots in them. She called them tens and ones. What does that mean?

**Mother:** Think of it like our kitchen shelf. I keep biscuits in packets of 10. If we have 2 full packets and 5 loose biscuits, how many biscuits do we have?

**Child:** Hmm... 10 and 10 is 20, and 5 more... 25 biscuits!

**Mother:** See, you just broke 25 into tens and ones! 2 tens and 5 ones make 25.

**Child:** What about 30, Maa? That is 3 tens and... no loose biscuits?

**Mother:** Bilkul sahi! 3 tens and 0 ones. The ones place is empty — just full packets, nothing loose.

**Child:** And what about 47?

**Mother:** 4 packets of 10 and 7 loose biscuits. 4 tens and 7 ones!

**Child:** I want to try! 38 is... 3 tens and 8 ones!

**Mother:** Shabash, beta! You have got it!

## The Tens Family

**Child:** Maa, teacher showed us 10, 20, 30, 40... all the way to 100. She called them the "Tens Family". Why?

**Mother:** Because they are all made of only tens — with zero ones. Like, 10 is 1 ten, 20 is 2 tens, 30 is 3 tens. They are all nice and round numbers.

**Child:** What about 100? Is that in the Tens Family too?

**Mother:** Yes! 100 is **10 tens**. That is a very special number. We call it **one hundred**. When Dadi makes 100 laddoos for a wedding, she makes 10 trays of 10!

**Child:** Wow, 10 trays of 10! That is a lot of laddoos!

## Counting Backwards

**Child:** Maa, I can count 1, 2, 3 all the way to 50 now. But teacher said we should also count backwards. That is hard!

**Mother:** It is like walking back on a number line! Start at any number and keep going one step back. Let us try from 30. I will start — 30, 29, 28...

**Child:** 27, 26, 25!

**Mother:** Keep going!

**Child:** 24, 23, 22, 21, 20! I did it, Maa!

**Mother:** Now try from 50!

**Child:** 50, 49, 48, 47, 46, 45, 44... This is like the countdown before a rocket launch!

**Mother:** Ha ha, yes! And one day you will count backwards from 100 — 100, 99, 98, 97... like a real space scientist!

**Child:** That will be so cool, Maa!

## Finding Missing Numbers

**Child:** Maa, teacher gave us a puzzle. Some numbers were missing and we had to fill them in. Like 42, blank, 44, blank, 46.

**Mother:** That is like a game! Numbers always go in order. After 42 comes 43, then 44. So the first blank is 43. And after 44?

**Child:** 45! The second blank is 45!

**Mother:** Very good! Now try this one — 77, 78, blank, blank, 81.

**Child:** 77, 78... then 79, 80, 81! The blanks are 79 and 80!

**Mother:** Bahut accha! You are becoming a number detective!
