## Place Value — Counting Beyond 10

When we write numbers, they can have **1 digit or 2 digits**. Each digit has a **place value** which helps us read and write numbers properly. Place values start from **right to left**.

- First place (right side) is called **Ones**
- Second place is called **Tens**

**Example:** The number **12**
- **1** is at the Tens place
- **2** is at the Ones place

Suppose you have **12 oranges** and you want to put them in boxes.
- In 1 box you put **10 oranges** — this box is called **1 Ten**
- **2 oranges** go in another box — these are your **Ones**

So: **1 Ten** and **2 Ones** = **12**

## Counting Toys

Imagine walking into a big toy shop! There are shelves full of different toys. Let us count what is on one shelf:

| Toy | How Many? |
|-----|----------|
| Toy trucks | 5 |
| Stuffed rabbits | 3 |
| Spinning tops | 7 |
| Kites | 4 |

Now we can **compare** these numbers:

- **Spinning tops** (7) are **more than** **toy trucks** (5).
- **Stuffed rabbits** (3) are **less than** **kites** (4).
- **Spinning tops** (7) are the **most** on the shelf.
- **Stuffed rabbits** (3) are the **least** (fewest).

## Comparing Groups (More, Less, Equal)

When we look at two groups and see which is bigger, smaller, or the same, we use these special words:

| Word | What It Means | Example |
|------|--------------|---------|
| **More than** | A bigger number | 7 is more than 4 |
| **Less than** | A smaller number | 3 is less than 5 |
| **Equal to** | Exactly the same number | 4 is equal to 4 |

## Fruit Baskets in the Kitchen

Maa brought home three baskets of fruit from the sabzi mandi:

| Fruit | How Many? |
|-------|----------|
| Guavas | 6 |
| Bananas | 6 |
| Apples | 3 |
| Chikoos | 8 |

Let us answer:
- Which fruit has the **most** pieces? **Chikoos** (8).
- Which fruit has the **fewest** pieces? **Apples** (3).
- Are there more bananas than guavas? They are **equal** — both have 6!
- Are there more apples than chikoos? **No!** 3 is less than 8.

## Collecting and Organising Data

When we count things and write the numbers down neatly, we are collecting **data**. Data helps us understand the world around us.

**Example:** Ask your classmates about their favourite animal and note down the answers:

| Animal | Number of Children |
|--------|--------------------|
| Dog | 5 |
| Cat | 3 |
| Parrot | 4 |
| Rabbit | 2 |

From this data we can see:
- **Dog** is the favourite animal of the most children (5).
- **Rabbit** is liked by the fewest children (2).
- More children like **parrot** (4) than **cat** (3).

> **Activity:** Ask 10 friends what their favourite colour is. Write their answers in a table. Which colour is the most popular?

## Comparing Groups

Whenever we look at groups of things, we can ask smart questions:

1. Which group has the **most** items?
2. Which group has the **least** (fewest) items?
3. Are any two groups **equal**?
4. Is one group **more than** or **less than** another?

**Example:** Dadi's jewellery box has:
- Gold bangles: **5**
- Silver bangles: **3**
- Glass bangles: **5**

Gold bangles and glass bangles are **equal** — both have 5. Silver bangles are the **least** (3). Gold and glass bangles are **more than** silver bangles.

## Organising Information

Writing information in a **table** makes it very easy to compare. Tables have neat rows and columns.

**Example:** Favourite snack of 12 children:

| Snack | Number of Children |
|-------|--------------------|
| Samosa | 4 |
| Pakora | 3 |
| Chaat | 3 |
| Vada Pav | 2 |

From this table we can quickly see:
- **Samosa** is the most popular snack.
- **Vada Pav** is liked by the fewest children.
- **Pakora** and **Chaat** are **equal** — both chosen by 3 children.

## True or False?

Using the fruit basket data from above (Guavas: 6, Bananas: 6, Apples: 3, Chikoos: 8), let us check:

- "Chikoos are more than guavas." — **True** (8 is more than 6).
- "Apples are more than bananas." — **False** (3 is less than 6).
- "Guavas and bananas are equal." — **True** (both are 6).

## Key Words to Remember

| Word | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Place value** | The value of a digit based on its position (Ones or Tens) |
| **Data** | Information we collect by counting or observing things |
| **More than** | A bigger number or amount |
| **Less than** | A smaller number or amount |
| **Equal to** | The same number or amount |
| **Most** | The biggest count in a group |
| **Least** | The smallest count in a group — the fewest |
| **Table** | A neat arrangement of rows and columns to show information |
| **Compare** | To look at two or more things and see how they differ |
