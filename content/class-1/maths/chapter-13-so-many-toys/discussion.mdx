## Comparing Groups (More, Less, Equal)

**Child:** Maa, I have 5 crayons and Riya has 3 crayons. Teacher said I have "more than" Riya. What does that mean?

**Mother:** It means your number is bigger than hers. 5 is bigger than 3, so you have **more** crayons. And Riya has **less** crayons than you.

**Child:** What if we both had the same number?

**Mother:** Then we would say you have an **equal** number. Like, you have 2 eyes and I have 2 eyes — we are equal!

**Child:** Oh! And I have 10 fingers and Maa also has 10 fingers — equal!

**Mother:** Perfect example, beta! Now tell me — Dadi has 7 bangles on one hand and 4 bangles on the other hand. Which hand has more?

**Child:** 7 is more than 4. So the hand with 7 bangles has more!

**Mother:** And which hand has less?

**Child:** The one with 4 bangles!

**Mother:** Shabash! You understand more, less, and equal like a champ!

## Collecting and Organising Data

**Child:** Maa, teacher asked us to find out everyone's favourite fruit and write it in a table. She called it "data". What is data?

**Mother:** Data is just information that you collect by asking questions or counting things. When you ask your friends "What is your favourite fruit?" and write down their answers — that is collecting data!

**Child:** I asked 8 friends. 3 said mango, 2 said apple, 2 said banana, and 1 said grapes.

**Mother:** Now you have data! Let us put it in a neat table so it is easy to read.

**Child:** Okay — Mango: 3, Apple: 2, Banana: 2, Grapes: 1.

**Mother:** Now I can ask you questions. Which fruit is the most popular?

**Child:** Mango! Because 3 is the biggest number.

**Mother:** Which is liked by the fewest friends?

**Child:** Grapes — only 1 friend said grapes.

**Mother:** And are any two fruits equal?

**Child:** Yes! Apple and banana — both have 2!

**Mother:** See how a table makes everything so clear? Without a table, all the answers would be jumbled up in your head. But when you write them neatly, you can compare easily.

**Child:** I love tables, Maa! They are like magic organisers!

**Mother:** Ha ha, that is a great way to put it, beta. Data and tables help us understand the world better.
