## Meet Kavya

Namaste! I am **Kavya** and I am 6 years old. I live in Jaipur with my family. Come, let me show you what my whole day looks like!

---

## Parts of the Day

Our day is divided into four parts. Each part has its own feeling:

| Part of the Day | What the Sky Shows | What We Usually Do |
|----------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| **Morning** | The sun peeks up over the rooftops | Wake up, freshen up, eat breakfast |
| **Afternoon** | The sun blazes right above our heads | Go to school, eat lunch |
| **Evening** | The sun dips low and paints the sky orange | Play, do homework |
| **Night** | The moon and twinkling stars come out | Eat dinner, hear stories, sleep |

> **Easy trick:** Look at the sky! The sun tells you what time of day it is.

---

## Morning

The **sun climbs up** into the sky. A new day begins!

Here is what Kavya does every morning:

1. She **wakes up** when she hears the temple bells.
2. She drinks a glass of **warm milk**.
3. She **brushes her teeth** and takes a **bath**.
4. She does a few **stretches** with her Dadi.
5. She eats **breakfast** — today it is poha! Then she leaves for school.

> **Your turn:** What is the first thing you do when you wake up?

---

## Afternoon

The **sun is right above us** now. It is the hottest part of the day!

- Kavya is at school, learning and singing with her classmates.
- At lunchtime, she opens her tiffin — rice, dal, and a little pickle.
- After school, the auto-rickshaw brings her home.
- She washes her hands and rests for a while.

---

## Evening

The **sun is going down**. The sky turns orange and pink!

- Kavya runs out to play with her friends in the park.
- They play kho-kho and skip rope.
- When it starts getting dark, she comes inside.
- She sits down to study and finish her homework.

---

## Night

The **moon** is shining and **stars** twinkle in the sky. Everything is quiet.

- Kavya eats **dinner** with her whole family.
- Dadi tells her a **story** about a clever fox.
- Kavya **brushes her teeth** again and changes into pyjamas.
- She says goodnight and goes to **sleep**.

---

## Which Takes Longer or Shorter?

Some things we do are over in a flash, and some take a long time.

| Takes Longer | Takes a Shorter Time |
|-------------|---------------------|
| Sleeping through the night | Putting on your shoes |
| Eating a full meal | Drinking a glass of water |
| Travelling to school | Combing your hair |
| Taking a bath | Washing your hands |

> **Think about it:** Does drawing a picture take longer or shorter than sharpening a pencil? Drawing takes **longer**!

## Seasons of India

The weather in India changes through the year. We have **four seasons**, and each brings something special.

### Summer (Garmi)

- The sun burns **hot**. We feel thirsty and sweaty.
- We wear **light cotton kurtas** and drink lots of **nimbu paani**.
- Yummy fruits like **mangoes**, watermelons, and litchis arrive!

### Monsoon (Baarish)

- Dark clouds roll in and it **pours** with rain.
- We carry **chhata** (umbrellas) and wear **raincoats**.
- Puddles form on the streets and sometimes we spot a **rainbow**!
- Trees and fields turn bright **green**.

### Winter (Sardi)

- The air turns **cold** and mornings are foggy.
- We bundle up in **sweaters**, **mufflers**, and **topi** (caps).
- We sip **hot chocolate** and munch on **gajar ka halwa** and **moongfali**.
- Festivals like **Diwali**, **Christmas**, and **Lohri** make winter cheerful.

### Spring (Basant)

- The weather becomes **just right** — not hot, not cold.
- **Flowers bloom** on every tree and butterflies dance around.
- We celebrate **Holi** and **Basant Panchami** with colour and music.

## Seasons and What We Wear

| Season | What We Wear |
|--------|-------------|
| **Summer** | Cotton clothes, caps, sunglasses |
| **Monsoon** | Raincoat, umbrella, gumboots |
| **Winter** | Sweater, jacket, muffler, gloves, cap |
| **Spring** | Comfortable light clothes |

---

## Seasons and What We Eat

| Season | Favourite Foods |
|--------|----------------|
| **Summer** | Mango, watermelon, kulfi, lassi |
| **Monsoon** | Pakora, masala chai, bhutta (roasted corn) |
| **Winter** | Gajar ka halwa, gajak, hot soup, moongfali |
| **Spring** | Fresh fruits, thandai, gujiya (during Holi) |

## Key Words to Remember

| Word | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Morning** | The early part of the day when the sun rises |
| **Afternoon** | The middle part of the day when the sun is highest |
| **Evening** | The later part of the day when the sun sets |
| **Night** | The dark time when the moon and stars are out |
| **Season** | A stretch of time with its own kind of weather |
| **Summer** | The hot season |
| **Monsoon** | The rainy season |
| **Winter** | The cold season |
| **Spring** | The pleasant season when flowers bloom |
