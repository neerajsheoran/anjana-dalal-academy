## Parts of the Day

**Child:** Maa, how do people know if it is morning or evening? What if there is no clock?

**Mother:** Very smart question, beta! Before clocks were invented, people just looked at the sky. When the sun comes up, it is **morning**. When the sun is right above our heads, it is **afternoon**. When the sun goes down and the sky turns orange, it is **evening**. And when the moon and stars appear, it is **night**.

**Child:** Oh! So the sun is like a big clock in the sky!

**Mother:** Exactly! Farmers in villages still use the sun to decide when to work and when to rest. Even birds know — they sing in the morning and go back to their nests at night.

**Child:** The rooster near Nani's house crows every morning! Is that his alarm clock?

**Mother:** Ha ha, yes! The rooster is nature's alarm clock. He crows when the sun is about to rise.

## Which Takes Longer or Shorter?

**Child:** Maa, teacher asked us what takes longer — eating lunch or drinking water. That is easy! Eating lunch takes much longer!

**Mother:** Good! But how do you know?

**Child:** Because when I drink water, it is just gulp gulp gulp — done! But eating lunch means chewing each bite, and rice and dal take so many bites.

**Mother:** Very good thinking! Some things are over quickly and some take a long time. Let us play a game. I will say two things, and you tell me which takes longer. Ready?

**Child:** Yes, Maa!

**Mother:** Sleeping at night or tying your shoelaces?

**Child:** Sleeping takes much, much longer! Shoelaces are done in one minute!

**Mother:** Going to school or washing your hands?

**Child:** Going to school! The auto-rickshaw takes so long, especially in traffic!

**Mother:** Bahut accha! You understand time really well, beta.

## Seasons of India

**Child:** Maa, why does it get so hot in May and so cold in December? Why does the weather change?

**Mother:** Because India has different **seasons**. The earth moves around the sun, and as it moves, the weather changes. In **summer**, the sun shines very strongly, so everything gets hot. In **winter**, the sun is gentler, so it gets cold.

**Child:** What about when it rains and rains?

**Mother:** That is the **monsoon** — the rainy season! Dark clouds bring water from the sea and pour it on the land. That is why farmers are so happy when monsoon comes — their crops need rain to grow.

**Child:** And what about when the flowers come out? Like during Holi?

**Mother:** That is **spring** — basant! The weather is perfect, flowers bloom everywhere, and butterflies come out to play. That is why we celebrate Holi in spring — the world is already so colourful!

**Child:** I love all the seasons, Maa. Summer for mangoes, monsoon for paper boats, winter for hot chocolate, and spring for colours!

**Mother:** Each season brings its own gifts, beta. That is the beauty of India!
