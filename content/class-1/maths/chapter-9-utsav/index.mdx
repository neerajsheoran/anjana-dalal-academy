## Festivals of India

India loves celebrations! When the cold winter says goodbye and warm sunny days arrive, people across the country burst into joy.

Different states have their own special names for this happy time:

| Festival | Where People Celebrate |
|----------|----------------------|
| **Makar Sankranti** | Maharashtra, Karnataka, and many other states |
| **Pongal** | Tamil Nadu |
| **Bihu** | Assam |
| **Lohri** | Punjab |
| **Uttarayan** | Gujarat |

People fly colourful kites, share sweets, dance, and sing. They dress up in bright clothes and fill their homes with beautiful **patterns** and decorations.

## What is a Pattern?

A **pattern** is a design or arrangement that keeps repeating in the same order.

Open your eyes and look — patterns are hiding everywhere!

- The **stripes** on a tiger go: dark, light, dark, light.
- The **tiles** on your bathroom floor repeat the same square again and again.
- The **bangles** on Maa's wrist follow a colour order — red, gold, red, gold.
- The **border** on your school uniform repeats a small design.

## Colour Patterns

Ananya is making a garland with paper flowers for Diwali:

**Yellow, Pink, Yellow, Pink, Yellow, Pink...**

What colour should she glue next? **Yellow!** The pattern keeps going: Yellow, Pink, Yellow, Pink...

Here are some more colour patterns to try:

- **Orange, White, Orange, White, Orange, White...** — Next is **Orange**.
- **Blue, Blue, Green, Blue, Blue, Green...** — Next is **Blue**.
- **Red, Yellow, Yellow, Red, Yellow, Yellow...** — Next is **Red**.

> **The secret:** Find the small group that repeats. Then just continue that group!

## Number Patterns

Numbers love making patterns too!

| Pattern | What Follows? |
|---------|--------------|
| 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, **?** | **6** — going up by 1 each time |
| 2, 4, 6, 8, **?** | **10** — jumping by 2 each time |
| 5, 10, 15, 20, **?** | **25** — jumping by 5 each time |
| 10, 20, 30, 40, **?** | **50** — jumping by 10 each time |
| 1, 1, 2, 2, 3, 3, **?** | **4** — every number appears twice |

> **Brain tickler:** In the pattern 4, 8, 12, 16, what follows? The answer is **20** because each number is 4 more than the one before!

## Patterns in Nature

Step outside and you will find stunning patterns everywhere!

- **Butterfly wings** — both halves look like mirror images.
- **Sunflower seeds** — arranged in beautiful spirals.
- **Honeycomb** — tiny six-sided shapes packed tightly together.
- **Peacock feathers** — round eye-shaped spots that repeat.
- **Leaf veins** — thin lines spreading outward from the centre.

> **Activity:** Go into your garden or a park. Look at leaves, petals, and insects. Draw any pattern you find!

## Patterns in Indian Art

Indian artists have been using patterns for thousands of years.

### Rangoli and Kolam

In Tamil Nadu, people draw **kolam** patterns at their doorstep every single morning using rice flour and dots. In other parts of India, we call this art **rangoli**.

Kolam starts with a grid of dots. Then graceful curved lines loop around the dots, making a repeating design.

During festivals like **Diwali** and **Onam**, families make large, colourful rangoli using flower petals, coloured powder, or even rice grains.

### Patterns on Temples and Fabrics

Old temples, mosques, and palaces across India are covered in carved stone patterns — repeating flowers, vines, and geometric shapes. Even the block-printed fabrics of Jaipur and the silk borders of Kanchipuram sarees use repeating patterns!

## Making Your Own Patterns

You can create patterns using everyday items at home:

- **Clapping pattern:** Clap, stomp, clap, stomp, clap, stomp...
- **Spoon pattern:** Big spoon, small spoon, big spoon, small spoon...
- **Leaf pattern:** Neem leaf, tulsi leaf, neem leaf, tulsi leaf...
- **Bindi pattern:** Red bindi, black bindi, red bindi, black bindi...

> **Activity:** Gather bottle caps, buttons, or dal grains. Lay them out in a repeating pattern. Challenge your family to continue it!

## Key Words to Remember

| Word | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Pattern** | A design that repeats in the same order |
| **Repeat** | To happen again and again the same way |
| **Rangoli** | A colourful floor pattern made during festivals |
| **Kolam** | A dot-and-line pattern drawn at doorsteps, popular in South India |
| **Sequence** | Things placed in a particular order, one after another |
