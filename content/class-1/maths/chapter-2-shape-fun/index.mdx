## Shapes of Things We See

Everything around us looks like one shape or another. Let us learn four important shapes.

### Ball Shape (Sphere)

A **cricket ball**, a **coconut**, and a **rasgulla** — all are round from every side. This shape is called a **sphere**.

More examples: marble, globe, sweet boondi laddu

### Box Shape (Cuboid)

A **brick**, a **tiffin box**, and a **soap bar** — all look like a box. This shape is called a **cuboid**.

More examples: almirah, notebook, pencil box

### Tube Shape (Cylinder)

A **steel glass**, a **rolling pin** (belan), and a **baans** (bamboo stick) — all look like a tube. This shape is called a **cylinder**.

More examples: water pipe, candle, log of wood

### Cone Shape (Cone)

A **kulfi cone**, a **party topi**, and a **mehendi cone** — all look like a pointed cap. This shape is called a **cone**.

More examples: carrot tip, funnel, temple shikhar

## Rolling and Sliding

Have you noticed that some things **roll** on the ground and some things **slide** along?

### What is Rolling?

When something turns over and over and keeps moving, it is **rolling**. Round objects roll very well.

- A **football** rolls on the ground.
- A **marble** rolls across the floor.
- A **coconut** rolls when you push it.

### What is Sliding?

When something moves smoothly on a surface without turning over, it is **sliding**. Flat things slide well.

- A **book** slides on a table.
- An **eraser** slides when you push it.
- A **matchbox** slides on a flat surface.

### The Clever Grandmother's Trick

Long ago, a little goat was walking to her Nani's house. A big jackal was chasing her! The clever Nani put the goat inside a big **matka** (clay pot) and rolled it down the hill. The round matka rolled very fast and the jackal could not catch it!

> **Why did the matka roll?** Because it is **round**! Round things roll easily, especially on a slope.

## Roll, Slide, or Both?

Some objects can do **both** — they can roll and also slide!

| Object          | Rolls? | Slides? |
| --------------- | ------ | ------- |
| Cricket ball    | Yes    | No      |
| Notebook        | No     | Yes     |
| Coin            | Yes    | Yes     |
| Belan (rolling pin) | Yes | Yes     |
| Soap bar        | No     | Yes     |
| Bangle          | Yes    | No      |
| Steel glass     | Yes    | Yes     |

A **coin** stands on its edge and rolls, but it also slides flat on a table. A **belan** rolls sideways but can also slide.

> **Activity:** Take a ball, a book, a coin, and a belan from your kitchen. Try rolling and sliding each one. What happens?

## Building with Shapes

You can stack different shapes to **build** things.

- Stack **boxes** (cuboids) to make a tower.
- Put a **cone** on top to make a roof.
- Stand **cylinders** (tubes) as pillars.

> **Try this:** Use your lunch box, pencil box, water bottle, and a paper cone to build a mini house!

**Which shape is best for stacking?** The cuboid! It has flat tops and bottoms, so pieces sit nicely on each other. Spheres roll away and cannot be stacked at all.

## Key Words to Remember

| Word         | Meaning                                              |
| ------------ | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| **Long**     | Stretched out in one direction, like a rope           |
| **Round**    | Shaped like a circle or ball                          |
| **Sphere**   | A ball shape — round on all sides                     |
| **Cuboid**   | A box shape — has flat sides                          |
| **Cylinder** | A tube shape — round and long                         |
| **Cone**     | A cap shape — round at bottom, pointed at top         |
| **Roll**     | To turn over and over while moving                    |
| **Slide**    | To move smoothly without turning over                 |
