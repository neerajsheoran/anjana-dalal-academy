## Let Us Count! — A Chat with Maa

**Child:** Maa, today teacher asked us to count things. I can count up to 9! But sometimes I skip numbers by mistake.

**Mother:** That is okay, beta! Counting takes practice. Let us count together using things in our kitchen. See these guavas in the basket? Let us count them one by one.

**Child:** One, two, three, four, five! There are 5 guavas!

**Mother:** Perfect! Now each time we say a number, it is **one more** than the number before. After 5 comes?

**Child:** Six!

**Mother:** And after 6?

**Child:** Seven! Then eight, then nine!

**Mother:** Wonderful! You counted from 1 to 9 without skipping a single number!

## Counting with Our Fingers

**Child:** Maa, teacher said we can use our fingers to count. But my friend showed 4 in a different way than me!

**Mother:** Both of you are correct! You can show 4 by keeping your thumb folded, or by keeping your little finger folded instead. There are many ways to show the same number.

**Child:** That is funny! Same number but different fingers!

**Mother:** Try this — show me 3 fingers. Now show me 3 different fingers. See? Same number, different way. The important thing is that the total count is the same.

## More, Less, or Equal

**Child:** Maa, Kavya brought 6 toffees to school and Rohan brought 4. She said she has more. How do we know?

**Mother:** Simple! Put them side by side. If Kavya puts her 6 toffees in a row and Rohan puts his 4 next to hers, whose row is longer?

**Child:** Kavya's row!

**Mother:** Yes! She has 2 leftover toffees after matching. So she has **more**. Rohan has **less**. But if both had the same number, we would say they are **equal**.

**Child:** Like when you give me and Didi the same number of biscuits?

**Mother:** Exactly! When I give both of you 3 biscuits each, that is equal. Fair and square!

## Counting Things Around Us

**Child:** Maa, teacher told us to count things at home. Can we do that?

**Mother:** Sure! Tell me — how many wheels does Papa's auto-rickshaw have?

**Child:** Three!

**Mother:** And how many toes on your one foot?

**Child:** Let me count... one, two, three, four, five! Five toes!

**Mother:** And how many colours are there in a rainbow?

**Child:** Seven! VIBGYOR — Violet, Indigo, Blue, Green, Yellow, Orange, Red!

**Mother:** Very impressive, beta! See, counting is useful everywhere. When Dadi makes laddus, she counts them. When Papa counts the passengers, he counts them. Numbers are all around us!

---

## Number Order and Missing Numbers

**Child:** Maa, teacher wrote some numbers on the board with blanks and we had to fill them in. Like 3, blank, 5. I knew it was 4!

**Mother:** Very good! Numbers always come in a fixed order. If you know 3 comes before and 5 comes after, the missing number must be 4.

**Child:** Can we count backwards too? Like 9, 8, 7...

**Mother:** Of course! Let us do it together like a rocket launch: 9, 8, 7, 6, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1 — blast off!

**Child:** Ha ha! That was so much fun, Maa! Counting backwards is like going from the biggest number to the smallest!

**Mother:** That is exactly right, beta.
