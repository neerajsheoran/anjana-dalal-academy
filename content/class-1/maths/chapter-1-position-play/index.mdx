## Where Is the Dog?

Tara has a puffy brown dog named Puffy. But Puffy is very naughty! He loves to play hide-and-seek all day long...

Puffy hops **on** the sofa. Then he sneaks **inside** the big clay pot. Now he crawls **under** the cot! Oh wait, he dashes **outside** the kitchen door!

Puffy jumps to the **top** of the bookshelf. He peeks from **above** the curtain rod. Then he curls up **below** the dining table.

Silly Puffy! We finally found you!

> **Think about it:** If you had a pet, where would it like to hide in your home?

## Position Words

We use special words to describe **where** something is. These words are called **position words**.

| Word        | What It Means                                                     |
| ----------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **On**      | Resting on top of something. The chapati is **on** the tawa.      |
| **Under**   | Below something. The shoes are **under** the bed.                 |
| **Inside**  | Within something. The pickle is **inside** the jar.               |
| **Outside** | Not within something. The buffaloes are **outside** the byre.     |
| **Above**   | Higher than something, not touching. The kite flies **above** us. |
| **Below**   | Lower than something. The roots are **below** the ground.         |

## Inside and Outside

Look around your home. You will find some things are **inside** and some things are **outside**.

* Mother stores the flour **inside** the container.
* We put our toys **inside** the toy basket.
* The washer man dries clothes **outside** on a rope.
* Cows and goats stand **outside** the gate.

> **Try this:** Open your tiffin box. What is **inside** it? What do you see **outside** it on the bench?

---

## Near and Far

Some things are **close to us** (near) and some things are **a long way away** (far).

* Your water bottle is **near** you on the desk.
* The mountains may be **far** from your city.
* The teacher's chair is **near** the blackboard.

## Our National Flag — Tiranga

India's flag is called **Tiranga** because it has three beautiful stripes.

| Position   | Colour           |
| ---------- | ---------------- |
| **Top**    | Saffron (kesari) |
| **Middle** | White            |
| **Bottom** | Green            |

The **Ashoka Chakra** sits **in the middle** of the white stripe. It is dark blue and has 24 lines.

We proudly hoist our Tiranga on **Republic Day** (26 January) and **Independence Day** (15 August).

## Before and After

Picture a long fruit train going **chuk-chuk-chuk** on the railway tracks!

A train has a **Mango** as the engine at the front and **Banana**, **Orange**, and **Watermelon** as coaches behind it.

* The Mango engine is **before** all the Banana, Orange, and Watermelon coaches.
* Some coaches come **before** the Watermelon coach.
* Some coaches come **after** the Banana coach.

> **Think about it:** In your school assembly line, who stands **before** you? Who stands **after** you?

## Sorting and Grouping

We can collect things that are **similar** and put them together in a group. This is called **sorting**.

* Put all the **seeds** in one pile.
* Put all the **buttons** in another pile.
* Put all the **bottle caps** in a third pile.

We can sort the same things in different ways:

* By **colour** — all green bangles together, all red bangles together.
* By **size** — big stones in one group, small stones in another.
* By **shape** — square tiles together, round coins together.

> **Activity:** Take a handful of dal from the kitchen. Can you sort the different types by colour?

## Key Words to Remember

| Word        | Meaning                                   |
| ----------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| **On**      | Resting on the surface of something       |
| **Under**   | Below or beneath something                |
| **Inside**  | Within a space or container               |
| **Outside** | Not within; in the open                   |
| **Above**   | Higher up than something                  |
| **Below**   | Lower down than something                 |
| **Near**    | Close to something                        |
| **Far**     | A long way from something                 |
| **Before**  | In front of something in a line           |
| **After**   | Behind something in a line                |
| **Sorting** | Putting things that are alike into groups |
