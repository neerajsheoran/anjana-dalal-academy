## Key Concepts

- **Gravitation** is the mutual attractive force between any two masses in the universe
- **Newton's Universal Law of Gravitation**: F = G x M x m / d²
- **G** (gravitational constant) = 6.673 x 10⁻¹¹ N m²/kg²
- Gravitational force is always **attractive**, acts along the line joining centres, and is mutual (Newton's 3rd law)
- **Free fall** = motion under gravity alone (no air resistance); all objects fall with the same acceleration regardless of mass
- **Acceleration due to gravity (g)** = GM/R² = **9.8 m/s²** at Earth's surface
- **Equations of motion for free fall**: replace 'a' with 'g' and 's' with 'h'; for dropped objects, u = 0; for objects thrown up, v = 0 at the top
- **Mass** = quantity of matter (scalar, constant everywhere, SI unit: kg, measured with beam balance)
- **Weight** = gravitational force on an object = **mg** (vector, varies with location, SI unit: Newton, measured with spring balance)
- Weight on Moon = 1/6 of weight on Earth (Moon's gravity is weaker)
- **Thrust** = force perpendicular to a surface; **Pressure** = force / area; SI unit: Pascal (Pa)
- Smaller area with same force = greater pressure (sharp knife cuts easily)
- **Buoyant force (upthrust)** = upward push by a fluid on a submerged/floating object
- Object floats if its density < fluid density; sinks if density > fluid density
- **Archimedes' Principle**: buoyant force = weight of fluid displaced
- **Relative density** = density of substance / density of water (no unit); > 1 sinks, < 1 floats

## Important Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Gravitation | Mutual attraction between any two masses in the universe |
| Gravity | Earth's gravitational pull on nearby objects |
| Free fall | Motion under gravity alone, with no other forces |
| Mass | Quantity of matter; constant everywhere |
| Weight | Gravitational force on an object (W = mg); varies with location |
| Thrust | Force acting perpendicular to a surface |
| Pressure | Force per unit area |
| Buoyant force | Upward push of a fluid on an immersed object |
| Archimedes' Principle | Buoyant force equals the weight of displaced fluid |
| Relative density | Ratio of substance's density to water's density |

## Remember These

- Mass stays the same on Moon; weight becomes **1/6th** because g on Moon is about 1.63 m/s²
- In a vacuum, a feather and a brick dropped together land at the **same time** (same g for all objects)
- Earth also accelerates toward a falling mango, but Earth's acceleration is negligibly tiny (~10⁻²⁵ m/s²) due to its enormous mass
- **Density of water** = 1000 kg/m³ = 1 g/cm³
- Lactometers work on Archimedes' Principle -- they detect diluted milk by measuring density changes
