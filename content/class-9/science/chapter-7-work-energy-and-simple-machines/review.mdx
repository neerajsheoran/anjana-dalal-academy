## Key Concepts

- **Work** in physics = Force × displacement in the direction of the force. **W = F × s**
- SI unit of work is the **joule (J)**. 1 J = 1 N × 1 m
- No work is done if (a) no displacement, (b) no force, or (c) force is perpendicular to the motion
- **Energy** = capacity to do work. Same unit as work: joule
- **Kinetic energy (KE)** = energy of motion. **KE = ½ × m × v²**
- Doubling the velocity quadruples the kinetic energy (because of v²)
- **Potential energy (PE)** = stored energy due to position or state
- Gravitational potential energy = mgh (m = mass, g = acceleration due to gravity ≈ 10 m/s², h = height)
- Other potential energies: elastic (springs, bows), chemical (food, fuel, batteries), nuclear (atoms)
- **Law of conservation of energy**: Energy can be transformed but not created or destroyed; total stays constant
- A falling object transforms PE to KE; a thrown-up object transforms KE to PE
- Energy "lost" to friction is actually converted to heat and sound — still conserved
- **Power** = work done per unit time. **P = W / t**. SI unit: **watt (W)**. 1 W = 1 J/s
- **Kilowatt-hour (kWh)** = electrical energy unit. 1 kWh = 3.6 × 10⁶ J
- **Six simple machines**: lever, inclined plane, wheel & axle, pulley, wedge, screw
- **Mechanical advantage** = load ÷ effort
- A simple machine reduces FORCE needed, but NOT total work — in reality friction wastes some energy

## Important Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Work | Force applied × displacement in the direction of force |
| Joule | SI unit of work and energy (1 N × 1 m) |
| Energy | The capacity to do work |
| Kinetic energy | Energy possessed by an object due to its motion (½mv²) |
| Potential energy | Energy stored due to position or state |
| Conservation of energy | Total energy in a closed system stays constant; only the form changes |
| Power | Rate of doing work / rate of energy transfer (W/t) |
| Watt | SI unit of power (1 joule per second) |
| Kilowatt-hour | Energy used by 1 kW appliance running for 1 hour (= 3.6 × 10⁶ J) |
| Mechanical advantage | Load divided by effort in a simple machine |
| Simple machine | Device that changes the magnitude or direction of a force to make work easier |

## Remember These

- **Pushing a wall that does not move = NO WORK in physics** (no displacement)
- **Doubling speed quadruples kinetic energy** — why high-speed crashes are far worse
- **Energy is never lost** — when it seems to vanish, it has become heat or sound
- **Simple machines do NOT reduce total work** — they reduce force needed, but require longer distance
- **1 unit of electricity (kWh)** is the energy a 1 kW appliance uses in 1 hour
- A 60 W fan running for 24 hours uses 60 × 24 = 1440 Wh = 1.44 kWh ≈ 1.5 units
- The six simple machines: lever, inclined plane, wheel & axle, pulley, wedge, screw
