## Key Concepts

- A **force** is a push or pull that can change an object's speed, direction, or shape
- **Balanced forces** (equal and opposite) produce no change in motion; **unbalanced forces** cause acceleration
- **Newton's First Law (Law of Inertia)**: An object at rest stays at rest, and an object in uniform motion continues moving, unless acted on by an unbalanced external force
- **Inertia** = tendency of an object to resist change in its state of motion; **mass is the measure of inertia**
- **Momentum (p)** = mass x velocity = **mv**; SI unit: kg m/s; it is a vector quantity
- **Newton's Second Law**: **F = ma**; force equals mass times acceleration
- Equivalently: force = rate of change of momentum = m(v - u) / t
- **1 Newton** = force needed to accelerate 1 kg by 1 m/s²
- **Newton's Third Law**: Every action has an equal and opposite reaction; action and reaction act on **different objects**
- **Conservation of Momentum**: In the absence of external forces, total momentum before = total momentum after
- **m₁u₁ + m₂u₂ = m₁v₁ + m₂v₂**
- Gun recoil: V = -mv/M (gun moves opposite to bullet; heavier gun = less recoil)

## Newton's Three Laws -- Summary

| Law | Statement | Core Idea |
|-----|-----------|-----------|
| First | Object stays at rest or in uniform motion unless an unbalanced force acts | Inertia |
| Second | F = ma | Force-mass-acceleration relationship |
| Third | Every action has an equal and opposite reaction | Action-reaction pairs on different bodies |

## Important Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Force | A push or pull that can alter an object's state of motion or shape |
| Inertia | Natural tendency to resist change in motion |
| Momentum | Product of mass and velocity (p = mv) |
| Newton | SI unit of force; 1 N = 1 kg m/s² |
| Balanced forces | Equal, opposite forces with zero net effect |
| Unbalanced forces | Forces that do not cancel; cause acceleration |

## Remember These

- Action and reaction act on **different objects** -- that is why they do not cancel each other
- A heavier object has more inertia -- harder to start and harder to stop
- Passengers fall forward when a bus brakes suddenly (their body resists the change from motion to rest -- inertia of motion)
- Passengers fall backward when a bus starts suddenly (their body resists the change from rest to motion -- inertia of rest)
- **1 kgf = 9.8 N** (weight of 1 kg mass on Earth's surface)
