## Key Concepts

- **Cone:** formed by rotating a right triangle around one perpendicular side
- Cone dimensions: **r** (base radius), **h** (height), **l** (slant height); related by **l = √(r² + h²)**
- Cone **CSA = πrl**, **TSA = πr(l + r)**, **Volume = (1/3)πr²h**
- A cone holds exactly **1/3** the volume of a cylinder with the same base and height
- **Sphere:** all points at distance r from the centre; **SA = 4πr²**, **Volume = (4/3)πr³**
- **Hemisphere:** half a sphere; **CSA = 2πr²**, **TSA = 3πr²**, **Volume = (2/3)πr³**
- **Cylinder (revision):** **CSA = 2πrh**, **TSA = 2πr(h + r)**, **Volume = πr²h**
- Sphere surface area = curved surface area of its circumscribing cylinder (Archimedes' result)
- Diameter = 2r -- always convert diameter to radius before using formulas

## Important Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Slant height (l)** | Distance from edge of cone base to the tip, along the surface |
| **CSA** | Curved (lateral) surface area -- excludes base(s) |
| **TSA** | Total surface area -- includes base(s) |
| **Hemisphere** | Exactly half of a sphere |

## Master Formula Table

| Solid | CSA | TSA | Volume |
|-------|-----|-----|--------|
| **Cylinder** | 2πrh | 2πr(h + r) | πr²h |
| **Cone** | πrl | πr(l + r) | (1/3)πr²h |
| **Sphere** | 4πr² | 4πr² | (4/3)πr³ |
| **Hemisphere** | 2πr² | 3πr² | (2/3)πr³ |

## Remember These

- For a cone, always find **l** first using l = √(r² + h²) before computing surface area
- Hemisphere TSA = **3**πr² (not 2πr²) -- the flat circular base adds an extra πr²
- Volume of cone = **(1/3)** × volume of cylinder -- the 1/3 factor is the most commonly forgotten part
- Sphere CSA and TSA are the **same** (4πr²) since a sphere has no flat base
- When the problem gives **diameter**, divide by 2 to get radius before substituting into any formula
