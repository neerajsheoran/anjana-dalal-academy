## Key Concepts

- **Collinear points** lie on the same line; **non-collinear points** do not
- **Complementary angles** sum to 90°; **Supplementary angles** sum to 180°
- **Adjacent angles** share a common vertex and arm, with non-common arms on opposite sides
- **Linear pair:** adjacent angles whose non-common arms form a straight line → they sum to **180°**
- **Vertically opposite angles** are always **equal** (formed when two lines intersect)
- A **transversal** is a line cutting two or more lines at distinct points
- When a transversal cuts **parallel lines**, it creates 8 angles with special relationships
- **Corresponding angles** are equal (F-shape)
- **Alternate interior angles** are equal (Z-shape)
- **Co-interior angles** (same-side interior) are supplementary → sum to **180°** (C/U-shape)
- The converses also hold: if corresponding/alternate angles are equal, or co-interior angles sum to 180°, the lines are **parallel**
- Lines parallel to the **same line** are parallel to **each other**
- **Angle sum property:** interior angles of a triangle add up to **180°**
- **Exterior angle theorem:** an exterior angle of a triangle = sum of the two non-adjacent interior angles
- Angles around a point total **360°**

## Important Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Linear pair** | Two adjacent angles forming a straight line (sum = 180°) |
| **Vertically opposite angles** | Non-adjacent angle pairs formed by two intersecting lines |
| **Transversal** | A line that intersects two or more lines at different points |
| **Corresponding angles** | Angles in the same relative position at each intersection |
| **Alternate interior angles** | Angles between the lines, on opposite sides of the transversal |
| **Co-interior angles** | Angles between the lines, on the same side of the transversal |

## Angle Classification

| Type | Range |
|------|-------|
| Acute | 0° < angle < 90° |
| Right | = 90° |
| Obtuse | 90° < angle < 180° |
| Straight | = 180° |
| Reflex | 180° < angle < 360° |

## Remember These

- Vertically opposite angles are **equal**, not supplementary -- a common confusion
- Co-interior angles are **supplementary** (sum 180°), not equal -- do not mix up with alternate angles
- Exterior angle of a triangle is **greater** than either remote interior angle individually
- If two lines are each parallel to a third line, they must be parallel to each other -- useful in multi-step proofs
- Always check whether lines are **parallel** before applying corresponding/alternate angle rules
