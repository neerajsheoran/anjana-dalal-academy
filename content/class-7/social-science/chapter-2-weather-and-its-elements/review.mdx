## Key Concepts

- **Weather** = state of atmosphere at a specific time and place
- **Climate** = average weather pattern over many years
- Weather happens in the **troposphere** (lowest layer of atmosphere, 6–18 km up)

## Five Elements of Weather

| Element | Measured in / by |
|---|---|
| **Temperature** | °C (Celsius) — thermometer |
| **Precipitation** | mm — rain gauge (rain, snow, sleet, hail) |
| **Atmospheric Pressure** | barometer |
| **Wind** | anemometer (speed) + wind vane (direction) |
| **Humidity** | hygrometer (water vapour in air) |

## Why Measure?

- Standard measurements let everyone agree on what "12°C" means
- Helps plan activities, prepare for danger, support farming, transport, fishing
- Saves lives during cyclones, floods, heat waves

## Traditional Indian Weather Prediction

- **Ants moving eggs uphill** → heavy rain coming
- **Frogs croaking** → rain expected
- **Birds flying low** → storm approaching
- **Smoke rising straight** → fair weather
- Many Indian villages still use these natural signs

## Indian Weather Science

- **Aryabhata** (5th century CE) — astronomy and seasons
- **Varahamihira** (6th century CE) — **Brihat Samhita** has detailed sections on rainfall prediction, eclipses, crop forecasts
- Indian astronomers studied weather thousands of years ago

## Modern Tools

- **Thermometer, Rain gauge, Barometer, Anemometer, Hygrometer**
- **Weather satellites** in space
- **Doppler radars**
- **Ocean buoys**
- **India Meteorological Department (IMD)** — combines all data, issues forecasts

## Weather Disasters in India

- **Cyclones** — Bay of Bengal east coast (Amphan 2020)
- **Floods** — Mumbai, Chennai monsoon floods
- **Droughts** — Rajasthan, Vidarbha
- **Heat waves** — north and central India summers
- **Cold waves** — north India winters

Accurate forecasting has reduced disaster deaths significantly.

## Common Confusions

- **Weather ≠ Climate.** Weather is "right now"; climate is the pattern over years.
- **Humidity vs Rain.** Humidity is water vapour in the air; rain is liquid water falling. High humidity often precedes rain but not always.
- **Cyclone vs Tornado.** Both are violent winds, but cyclones form over warm seas and are huge (hundreds of km wide); tornadoes form over land and are small (hundreds of meters wide).
- **°C and °F are not the same.** India uses °C. 0°C = 32°F. 100°C (boiling water) = 212°F. Always check the unit when comparing.
