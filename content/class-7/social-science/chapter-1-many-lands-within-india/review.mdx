## Key Concepts

- India is the **7th-largest country** by area and most populous
- Part of the **Indian Subcontinent** (with Pakistan, Bangladesh, Nepal, Bhutan, Sri Lanka, Myanmar)
- Natural boundaries: Himalayas (N), Thar Desert/Arabian Sea (W), Indian Ocean (S), Bay of Bengal (E)

## Five Geographical Regions

1. **Great Mountain Zone** (Himalayas)
2. **Northern Plains** (Indo-Gangetic)
3. **Thar Desert** (Rajasthan, Gujarat)
4. **Peninsular South** (Deccan Plateau, Western/Eastern Ghats)
5. **Islands** (Andaman & Nicobar, Lakshadweep)

## The Himalayas

- ~2,500 km long, **still growing** ~5 mm/year
- Formed by India colliding with Eurasia ~50 million years ago
- "Himalaya" = **hima** (snow) + **alaya** (abode)

### Three Parallel Ranges

| Range | Local Name | Key Features |
|---|---|---|
| Greater | **Himadri** | Mt. Everest, Kanchenjunga, snow year-round |
| Lower | **Himachal** | Hill stations: Shimla, Manali, Nainital, Darjeeling |
| Outer | **Shivalik** | Rolling hills, dense forests |

### The Water Tower of Asia

Himalayan rivers — Ganga (source: **Gaumukh**), Yamuna (Yamunotri), Indus, Brahmaputra — feed crores of Indians.

### Traditional Wisdom

- **Kath-kuni / dhajji-dewari** — earthquake-resistant wood-and-stone houses of western Himalayas

## Northern Plains

- Fertile **floodplains** of the Ganga and its tributaries
- Wheat, rice, sugarcane, mustard
- Densely populated — UP, Bihar, West Bengal, Punjab, Haryana
- Historical heart of Indian empires (Mauryas, Guptas, Mughals)

## Thar Desert

- Rajasthan and parts of Gujarat
- Cities: **Jaisalmer, Bikaner, Jodhpur**
- Water conservation: **tankas** (underground tanks), **baolis** (step-wells)
- Camel = ship of the desert; trade route prosperity

## Peninsular South

- **Deccan Plateau** — old, volcanic, black soil good for cotton
- **Western Ghats** — heavy monsoon rain, biodiversity hotspot
- **Eastern Ghats** — lower, broken
- Major rivers: Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri
- Dravidian languages: Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam

## Islands

- **Andaman & Nicobar** — Bay of Bengal, ancient tribes (Jarawa, Sentinelese), coral reefs
- **Lakshadweep** — Arabian Sea, coral atolls

## Cultural Link

- **Rakesh Sharma** (1984) — first Indian astronaut — described India from space as **"Sare jahan se achchha"**
- Indian geography → diverse foods, languages, festivals, architecture

## Common Confusions

- **Subcontinent vs Continent:** India is part of Asia (continent) but its own well-defined **subcontinent** because of natural boundaries.
- **"Himachal" range vs Himachal Pradesh (state):** "Himachal" is the Lower Himalayan range. Himachal Pradesh the state is named after it but is a political unit.
- **Himalayas are NOT old mountains:** They are the **youngest** major range on Earth, still growing. Compare with the Aravallis (very old, eroded).
- **The Deccan is a plateau, not a mountain range** — flat-topped, raised land, formed by ancient volcanic activity.
