## In One Paragraph

**Infrastructure** is the physical foundation of modern life — roads, railways, electricity, water, telecom, ports, airports, hospitals, schools — that we mostly take for granted until it fails. India has the **2nd longest road network in the world** (after USA), over 6.6 million km. **Indian Railways** carries 8 billion passenger trips a year over 68,000+ km of track. India has 150+ airports; 12 major and 200+ minor ports handling 95% of foreign trade. India is the world's **3rd largest electricity producer** with a fast-growing renewable share (solar, wind, hydro), aiming for 500 GW non-fossil capacity by 2030 and net-zero by 2070. The **telecom revolution** is dramatic — from 5 million phones in 1990 to 1.15 billion mobile connections today, with the **world's cheapest mobile data** after Jio's 2016 launch. **ISRO** runs a world-class space programme (Chandrayaan, Mangalyaan, Gaganyaan, NavIC). India is the **"pharmacy of the world"** — largest producer of generic drugs. Major schemes have transformed daily life: **Pradhan Mantri Gram Sadak Yojana** (rural roads), **Bharatmala** (highways), **Sagarmala** (ports), **Smart Cities Mission**, **Jal Jeevan Mission** (rural water), **Swachh Bharat** (sanitation). Challenges remain — quality, maintenance, funding, climate adaptation — but India has come a long way since independence and infrastructure continues to grow.

## Key Takeaways

- **Infrastructure** = physical and organisational structures supporting society.
- **Roads**: India has the world's 2nd longest network (6.6M+ km) after USA. National Highways ~145,000 km.
- **Major road projects**: Golden Quadrilateral, Bharatmala, Pradhan Mantri Gram Sadak Yojana.
- **Indian Railways**: 68,000+ km track; 8 billion passenger trips/year; ~1.2 million employees.
- **Major projects**: Vande Bharat, Dedicated Freight Corridors, Mumbai-Ahmedabad bullet train.
- **Air**: 150+ airports; ~340 million passenger flights per year; UDAN scheme.
- **Ports**: 12 major + 200+ minor; handle 95% of foreign trade by volume.
- **Electricity**: 3rd largest producer globally (~450 GW capacity); growing renewable share.
- Goal: **500 GW non-fossil capacity by 2030**, **net-zero by 2070**.
- **International Solar Alliance**: launched by India in 2015.
- **Coal**: India is 2nd largest coal producer (after China).
- **Oil**: 3rd largest oil importer (~85% imported).
- **Telecom**: India is 2nd-largest telecom market; **cheapest mobile data in the world** after Jio (2016).
- **Internet**: 800+ million users; **5G** rolled out from 2022; **NavIC** is India's GPS alternative.
- **Space**: ISRO — Chandrayaan, Mangalyaan, Gaganyaan; world-class space agency.
- **Healthcare**: AIIMS network; world's largest generic drug producer (the "pharmacy of the world").
- **Education**: 1.5M schools; 1,000 universities; IITs (23), IIMs (20), AIIMS (~20).
- **Smart Cities Mission**: 100 cities being modernised.
- **Jal Jeevan Mission**: piped water to all rural households.
- **Swachh Bharat**: 100+ million toilets built; Indore = cleanest city 7 years running.
- Challenges: quality, maintenance, funding gap, environmental impact, inequality, climate change.

## A Phrase to Remember

> **Infrastructure is the silent foundation of everything.**

We rarely notice good infrastructure. We always notice when it fails. Building and maintaining it is one of a country's most important — and most thankless — responsibilities.
