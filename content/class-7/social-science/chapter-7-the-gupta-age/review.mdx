## Key Concepts

- **Gupta Empire** (~320-550 CE) = India's **Classical Age** / **Golden Age**
- Capital: **Pataliputra** (modern Patna)
- Period of extraordinary achievement in math, science, literature, art

## Major Gupta Rulers

| Ruler | Approximate dates | Key achievement |
|---|---|---|
| **Chandragupta I** | 320-335 CE | Founder; Licchavi marriage alliance |
| **Samudragupta** | 335-375 CE | "Napoleon of India"; conquered most of N. India; Allahabad pillar inscription |
| **Chandragupta II / Vikramaditya** | 375-415 CE | Greatest Gupta; defeated Shakas; patron of **Kalidasa** |
| Kumaragupta, Skandagupta | 415-470 CE | Faced Huna invasions |

## Major Achievements

### Mathematics

- **Aryabhata** (476-550 CE) wrote the **Aryabhatiya**
  - Calculated **π (pi)** to 4 decimal places
  - Said Earth **rotates on its axis**
  - Calculated **length of year** accurately
- **Concept of zero (shunya)** developed and used systematically
- **Decimal system** (0-9 digits) — India's gift to world mathematics

### Astronomy

- Earth's rotation explained
- **Eclipses** understood as natural events
- **Varahamihira** wrote **Pancha-Siddhantika**

### Medicine — Ayurveda

- **Sushruta Samhita** — over 300 surgeries, 120 surgical instruments (including cataract surgery, plastic surgery)
- **Charaka Samhita** — internal medicine

### Literature

- **Kalidasa** — greatest Sanskrit poet
  - **Abhijnanashakuntalam** — romantic play (later admired in Europe)
  - **Meghaduta** — "Cloud Messenger"
  - **Kumarasambhava, Raghuvamsa** — epic poems
- **Mudrarakshasa** by Vishakhadatta — political drama
- Compilation of **Puranas**

### Art & Architecture

- **Ajanta caves** — paintings and sculptures
- **Nagara style** of north Indian temples developed
- **Bhitargaon Temple** — earliest surviving brick temple

### Metallurgy

- **Iron Pillar of Delhi** (Mehrauli) — **rust-free for 1,600+ years**
- Bears inscription praising "Chandra" — likely Chandragupta II

## Foreign Visitor

- **Fa-Hien (Faxian)** — Chinese Buddhist pilgrim, early 5th century CE
- His **"Records of Buddhist Kingdoms"** describes:
  - Prosperous society
  - Safe roads
  - State leaving people alone
  - Free hospitals
  - Vegetarianism common
  - Religious tolerance

## Why "Classical Age"?

- Sanskrit literature peaked (Kalidasa)
- Hindu temple architecture developed
- Mathematics and astronomy made fundamental breakthroughs
- Indian art set standards for later ages
- Many patterns of "Indian culture" crystallised here

## Decline

- **Huna invasions** (related to European Huns) from northwest in late 5th century CE
- **Skandagupta** won one battle but the empire weakened
- By **550 CE**, Gupta dynasty ended

## Common Confusions

- **Chandragupta Maurya ≠ Chandragupta I or II.** Maurya was Mauryan dynasty (~321 BCE). Gupta Chandraguptas were ~640+ years later.
- **Samudragupta was a conqueror, not the empire's founder.** That was his father Chandragupta I.
- **Hunas in India ≠ Huns in Europe.** Same family, different branches. Both Central Asian.
- **Gupta empire was NOT all of India.** Even at its peak, it ruled north and central India — south India had its own dynasties (Pallavas, etc.).
- **"Classical" doesn't mean "old-fashioned."** It means a period whose achievements set standards. Like classical Greek or classical music.
