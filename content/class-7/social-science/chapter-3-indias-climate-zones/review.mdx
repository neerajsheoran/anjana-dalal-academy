## Three Important Words

- **Weather** — what we experience day to day
- **Seasons** — yearly recurring patterns (summer, monsoon, winter, etc.)
- **Climate** — long-term weather pattern over many decades

## The Six Indian Ritus (Seasons)

| Ritu | Season | Months |
|---|---|---|
| Vasanta | Spring | Feb-Apr |
| Grishma | Summer | Apr-Jun |
| Varsha | Monsoon | Jun-Aug |
| Sharad | Autumn | Aug-Oct |
| Hemanta | Pre-winter | Oct-Dec |
| Shishir | Winter | Dec-Feb |

Each ritu has its festivals, foods, music, and Ayurvedic guidance.

## Seven Climate Types in India

| Type | Region | Key feature |
|---|---|---|
| **Alpine** | Ladakh, high Himalayas | Cold, snowy winters |
| **Temperate** | Hill stations | Moderate climate |
| **Subtropical** | Northern Plains | Hot summers, cold winters |
| **Arid** | Thar Desert | Hot, very dry |
| **Tropical Wet** | Western coast, Northeast | Heavy rain, warm |
| **Semi-Arid** | Deccan Plateau | Moderate everything |
| **Tropical Wet & Dry** | Most of India | Wet monsoon + dry season |

## The Monsoon

"Monsoon" comes from Arabic **mausim** = "season". It means **seasonal reversing winds**.

### Two Monsoons

**Southwest Monsoon (Jun-Sep)** — the BIG one:
- 75% of India's rainfall
- Hits Kerala ~ June 1, sweeps across India over 4-6 weeks
- Two branches: Arabian Sea and Bay of Bengal

**Northeast Monsoon (Oct-Nov)** — the smaller one:
- Mainly waters Tamil Nadu and parts of Andhra
- Winds from land to sea

### How It Forms

Summer: Land heats up → hot air rises → low pressure over land → moist sea winds rush in → rises over mountains → condenses → rain.

Winter: Reverse — land cools → winds blow from land to sea → mostly dry.

### Why It Matters

- **60% of Indian farms** depend on monsoon (not irrigation)
- A bad monsoon can **shrink India's GDP by 1-2%**
- **Drinking water, hydropower, festivals** all linked

## Wettest Places in India (and the World)

- **Cherrapunji** and **Mawsynram** in Meghalaya
- Among the wettest places on Earth — meters of rain per year

## Climate Shapes Indian Life

- **Food** — wheat (north), rice (wet south), millets (dry plateau), coconut/spices (tropical coast)
- **Houses** — kath-kuni in mountains, sloping roofs in heavy rain, flat roofs in dry regions, thick mud walls in deserts
- **Festivals** — harvest festivals (Pongal, Lohri, Bihu) in winter; Onam in autumn; Holi welcoming spring

## Climate Change

- **Hotter summers** — record heat waves
- **Erratic monsoon** — irregular timing and intensity
- **Glacier loss** — Himalayan glaciers shrinking
- **Stronger cyclones** — warmer oceans
- **More floods AND droughts** — both extremes

Caused by humans burning coal, oil, gas. Major challenge for India's future.

## Common Confusions

- **Weather ≠ Climate.** One day of rain is weather. Decades of regular rain make a climate.
- **Monsoon is not just rain.** It's a system of seasonal reversing winds. Rain happens because of the wind pattern.
- **Six ritus is uniquely Indian.** Most of the world uses four seasons. India added "monsoon" and "pre-winter" as separate seasons.
- **A "good monsoon" means normal rainfall, not the most rainfall.** Too much rain can cause floods.
- **Climate change is real and measurable.** It's not just opinion — temperatures, rainfall patterns, and glacier sizes have been recorded for decades and show clear trends.
