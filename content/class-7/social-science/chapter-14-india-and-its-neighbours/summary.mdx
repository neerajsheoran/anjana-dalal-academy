## In One Paragraph

India has **seven land neighbours** (Pakistan, Afghanistan, China, Nepal, Bhutan, Bangladesh, Myanmar) and immediate **maritime neighbours** (Sri Lanka, Maldives), with **15,100 km of land border** and **11,100 km of coastline**. India sits at the heart of the **Indian Ocean** — through which two-thirds of the world's oil and half of all container ships pass. With China, India shares ancient Buddhist ties (Faxian, Xuanzang, Bodhidharma) but a tense modern relationship over disputed borders (Aksai Chin, Arunachal Pradesh, Galwan 2020). With Pakistan, history is shaped by the painful **Partition of 1947** — the world's largest migration, 1-2 million killed — and four wars. **Bangladesh** (born 1971 with Indian help), **Nepal** (open border, Hindu-Buddhist heritage, Gorkha regiments), **Bhutan** (Gross National Happiness, carbon-negative), and **Sri Lanka** (Tamil-Buddhist links, recent crisis where India helped) are friendly neighbours. **SAARC** (the regional cooperation body, 1985) has been hampered by India-Pakistan tensions, so newer groupings like **BIMSTEC** and **BBIN** have grown. Despite political tensions, South Asia remains one cultural region — sharing language, food, music, films, festivals, and pilgrimage.

## Key Takeaways

- **Land neighbours**: Pakistan, Afghanistan, China, Nepal, Bhutan, Bangladesh, Myanmar.
- **Maritime neighbours**: Sri Lanka (30 km away across Palk Strait), Maldives.
- India's land border: **15,100 km**; coastline: **11,100 km**.
- The **Indian Ocean** carries 2/3 of world's oil and 1/2 of all container ships; 2.7 billion people live in its rim countries.
- **China**: Buddhist ties since the 1st century CE (Faxian, Xuanzang, Bodhidharma); modern border disputes (1962 war, 2017 Doklam, 2020 Galwan).
- **Pakistan**: Born of Partition 1947 — 10-20 million displaced, 1-2 million killed; four wars; Kashmir remains disputed.
- **Bangladesh**: Born in 1971 with India's help; deep cultural ties through shared Bengali heritage; major trading partner.
- **Nepal**: Open border; Lord Buddha was born in Lumbini (Nepal); Sita's father Janak ruled there; Gorkha soldiers serve in Indian Army.
- **Bhutan**: World leader in measuring "Gross National Happiness"; carbon-negative; very friendly with India.
- **Sri Lanka**: Ramayana links (Lanka of Ravana); Buddhism reached SL via Emperor Ashoka's children; recent (2022) economic crisis helped by India.
- **Maldives**: 1,200 islands; tiny population (~500,000) but strategically important.
- **SAARC** (1985): South Asia's regional body — paralysed by India-Pakistan tensions.
- **BIMSTEC**, **BBIN**, **IORA**: newer groupings working around the SAARC paralysis.
- Pilgrimage networks cross borders: Pashupatinath (Nepal), Nankana Sahib (Pakistan), Ajmer (India), Bodh Gaya (India).
- South Asia is **one cultural region** with several political states — borders are recent (1947), but connections are ancient.

## A Phrase to Remember

> **Neighbours are forever; the rest can change.**

India can choose its friends and partners around the world, but its neighbours are fixed by geography. How we get along with them shapes the safety, prosperity, and dignity of hundreds of millions of people across South Asia.
