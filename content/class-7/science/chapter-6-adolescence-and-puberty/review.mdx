## Key Concepts

- Human life stages: **Infancy** (0-2 yrs) --> **Childhood** (2-10 yrs) --> **Adolescence** (10-19 yrs) --> **Adulthood** (19-60 yrs) --> **Old age** (60+)
- **Adolescence** is the transition period between childhood and adulthood
- Physical changes during adolescence: rapid **height increase**, changes in **body shape and weight**, **voice changes**, new **hair growth**, and **acne/pimples**
- In boys: the **voice box (larynx)** enlarges, causing voice to deepen; visible as the **Adam's apple**
- In girls: **breast development** and **widening of hips** occur
- **Acne** occurs because skin oil glands become more active, blocking pores
- **Secondary sexual characteristics** are visible features that distinguish males from females but are not directly related to reproduction
- **Puberty** is the phase when the body develops the capacity for reproduction
- The **menstrual cycle** repeats every **28-30 days** (range: 21-35 days is normal)
- **Menstruation** (the period) typically lasts **3-7 days**
- Menstruation is a **completely natural biological process** -- myths about it have no scientific basis
- Emotional changes include **mood swings**, **heightened sensitivity**, **intense emotions**, and **growing curiosity**
- Three pillars of healthy adolescence: **balanced nutrition**, **personal hygiene**, **regular physical activity**
- Adolescents need extra **calcium**, **iron**, **protein**, and **vitamins** for rapid growth
- **Substance abuse** (tobacco, alcohol, drugs) is dangerous and highly addictive -- say NO firmly
- All adolescent changes are driven by **hormones** -- chemical messengers produced by glands and carried through blood
- **Cyberbullying** is using digital platforms to harass or intimidate -- save evidence and report it
- Government programmes: **MHS** (free sanitary pads), **RKSK** (adolescent health), **Nasha Mukt Bharat** (anti-drug campaign)

## Important Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Adolescence | The transitional period between childhood and adulthood (roughly ages 10-19) |
| Puberty | The developmental phase when the body gains the capacity for reproduction |
| Secondary sexual characteristics | Physical features that distinguish males from females but are not directly involved in reproduction |
| Menstrual cycle | A monthly cycle (~28-30 days) in females involving preparation and shedding of the uterine lining |
| Menstruation | The phase of blood discharge during the menstrual cycle (3-7 days) |
| Adam's apple | The visible bump on the throat caused by the enlarged voice box in boys |
| Hormones | Chemical messengers produced by glands that regulate body changes |
| Cyberbullying | Using phones, computers, or social media to deliberately harass or intimidate someone |

## Remember These

- Physical changes during adolescence vary widely from person to person -- **all variations are normal**
- Menstruation is a **natural, healthy** process -- superstitions about it have no scientific basis
- The time period of puberty differs for everyone; there is no fixed timetable
- **Hormones** are the master controllers behind all physical, emotional, and behavioural changes of adolescence
- National De-addiction Helpline number: **14446**
