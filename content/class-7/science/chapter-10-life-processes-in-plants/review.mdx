## Key Concepts

- Plants **manufacture their own food** -- they do not need to eat like animals
- **Photosynthesis** is the process by which plants make food using sunlight, water, carbon dioxide, and chlorophyll
- **Photosynthesis equation**: Carbon dioxide + Water ---(Sunlight, Chlorophyll)---> Glucose + Oxygen
- **Leaves** are the main site of photosynthesis -- the "food factories" of the plant
- **Chlorophyll** is the green pigment in leaves that captures sunlight energy
- Plants need **both sunlight and water** for healthy growth (without either, the plant wilts or grows pale)
- Food produced by photosynthesis is stored mainly as **starch**
- **Iodine test**: applying iodine to a leaf turns it **blue-black** if starch is present
- Four essentials for photosynthesis: **sunlight**, **water**, **carbon dioxide**, **chlorophyll**
- **Stomata** are tiny pores on leaf surfaces for gas exchange -- CO2 enters, O2 exits
- Stomata also release excess water as vapour (**transpiration**)
- Each stoma is surrounded by two **guard cells** that open/close the pore
- **Xylem** transports water and minerals **upward** from roots to all plant parts
- **Phloem** transports food (sugars) from leaves **to all other parts** of the plant
- Food storage: seeds (wheat, rice), roots (carrot, radish), stems (potato, ginger), fruits (mango, banana)
- Plants **respire** continuously, day and night, in **every living cell**
- **Respiration equation**: Glucose + Oxygen --> Carbon dioxide + Water + Energy
- Photosynthesis happens only in **green parts** and only during **daytime**; respiration happens in **all cells**, **day and night**
- Photosynthesis **stores** energy in glucose; respiration **releases** energy from glucose
- Plants are **primary food producers** -- all food chains begin with plants
- Photosynthesis releases the **oxygen** that all animals need to breathe

## Important Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Photosynthesis | The process by which plants use CO2, water, sunlight, and chlorophyll to make glucose and release oxygen |
| Chlorophyll | The green pigment in leaves that absorbs sunlight for photosynthesis |
| Stomata | Microscopic pores on leaf surfaces that allow gas exchange |
| Guard cells | Kidney-shaped cells flanking each stoma that control its opening and closing |
| Transpiration | The loss of water vapour from leaves through stomata |
| Xylem | Plant tissue that transports water and minerals upward from roots |
| Phloem | Plant tissue that transports food from leaves to all other plant parts |
| Starch | The form in which plants store excess glucose |

## Photosynthesis vs Respiration

| Feature | Photosynthesis | Respiration |
|---------|---------------|-------------|
| Food | Glucose is **produced** | Glucose is **consumed** |
| Gas in | Carbon dioxide | Oxygen |
| Gas out | Oxygen | Carbon dioxide |
| Energy | Sunlight is **stored** | Stored energy is **released** |
| When | Daytime only | Day and night |
| Where | Green parts (mainly leaves) | Every living cell |

## Remember These

- **Iodine test** for starch: blue-black colour = starch present (used to prove photosynthesis)
- **Xylem = water UP**, **Phloem = food DOWN** (and sideways) -- remember the dual transport system
- Photosynthesis needs **all four** essentials -- remove any one and it stops
- Plants respire **24/7** but photosynthesize only during the **day** in **green parts**
- Without photosynthesis, there would be no food and no oxygen for any animal on Earth
