## Key Concepts

- **Science** is a way of exploring the world through questioning, observation, and experimentation
- Science is not about memorising answers -- it is about developing a **habit of inquiry**
- Class 7 Science covers chemistry (acids, bases, metals), physics (electricity, heat, light, motion), biology (adolescence, life processes), and earth science (Earth-Moon-Sun)
- Every branch of science **connects to others** -- e.g., acids and bases link to soil chemistry, which links to plant growth
- The same scientific knowledge can be used for **good or harm** -- responsibility matters
- **Burning fuels** affects the atmosphere; cleaner alternatives include solar and wind energy
- Understanding how **plastic** fails to break down motivates reduce, reuse, and recycle
- Scientific knowledge about **soil health** inspires better farming practices
- Great discoveries begin with **asking "why"** -- C.V. Raman's curiosity about the colour of the ocean led to a Nobel Prize
- Being a good scientist means staying **curious**, doing hands-on experiments, and embracing unexpected results
- Both expected and unexpected experimental results are **equally valuable**
- Science is a **living, evolving** process, not a fixed set of facts

## Important Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Science | A systematic way of exploring the natural world through observation, questioning, and experimentation |
| Observation | Carefully noticing and recording what happens in the world around us |
| Experimentation | Testing ideas through controlled activities to see if they hold true |
| Curiosity | The desire to learn and understand more about the world |

## Remember These

- Science is about **"how" and "why"**, not just "what"
- Every result in an experiment teaches something -- there are no failed experiments, only unexpected discoveries
- Scientific knowledge comes with **responsibility** -- it must be used wisely
