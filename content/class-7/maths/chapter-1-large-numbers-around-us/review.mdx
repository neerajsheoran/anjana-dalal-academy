## Key Concepts

- The **Indian place value system** uses a **3-2-2-2** comma pattern from right to left (thousands, lakhs, crores, arabs)
- The **International system** uses a **3-3-3** comma pattern (thousands, millions, billions)
- **1 lakh** = 1,00,000 (1 followed by 5 zeroes)
- **1 crore** = 1,00,00,000 (1 followed by 7 zeroes)
- **1 million** = 10 lakhs; **1 billion** = 100 crores = 1 arab
- **100 thousands** make 1 lakh; **10 lakhs** make 1 crore
- **Place value** determines the worth of a digit based on its position in the number
- **Expanded form** breaks a number into its place value components: 3,048 = 3 x 1000 + 0 x 100 + 4 x 10 + 8 x 1
- The **digit sum** is the total of all individual digits in a number
- **Rounding up** uses a slightly larger value; **rounding down** uses a slightly smaller value
- **Estimation** helps verify whether an answer is reasonable
- To round to the nearest lakh/crore, look at the digit immediately to the right of that place
- **Multiply by 5:** halve the number, then multiply by 10
- **Multiply by 25:** divide by 4, then multiply by 100
- **Multiply by 125:** divide by 8, then multiply by 1000
- An **m-digit** number times an **n-digit** number gives a product with **(m + n - 1)** or **(m + n)** digits

## Important Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Lakh | 1,00,000 — one followed by five zeroes |
| Crore | 1,00,00,000 — one followed by seven zeroes |
| Arab | 1,00,00,00,000 — one followed by nine zeroes (equals one billion) |
| Million | 10,00,000 — ten lakhs in the International system |
| Billion | 1,00,00,00,000 — 100 crores in the International system |
| Place value | The worth of a digit determined by its position |
| Estimation | Using approximate values to quickly check if an answer is sensible |
| Rounding | Replacing a number with a nearby convenient value |

## Remember These

- Indian system comma pattern is **3-2-2**, International is **3-3-3** -- do not mix them up
- 1 million = 10 lakhs, **not** 1 lakh; 1 billion = 100 crores, **not** 10 crores
- When rounding, if the digit to the right is **5 or more**, round up; if **less than 5**, round down
- The multiplication shortcut for digit count: m-digit x n-digit = **(m+n-1) or (m+n)** digits in the product
