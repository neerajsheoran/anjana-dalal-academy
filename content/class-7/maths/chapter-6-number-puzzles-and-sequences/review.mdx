## Key Concepts

- **Parity** tells whether a number is **even** (divisible by 2) or **odd** (not divisible by 2)
- Even + Even = **Even**; Odd + Odd = **Even**; Even + Odd = **Odd**
- Even x Even = **Even**; Odd x Odd = **Odd**; Even x Odd = **Even**
- The **nth even number** = **2n**; the **nth odd number** = **2n - 1**
- Parity can prove certain outcomes are **impossible** without checking every case
- A **magic square** has every row, column, and diagonal summing to the same **magic constant**
- For a 3x3 magic square with numbers 1-9: magic constant = **15**, centre cell = **5**
- The **digit sum** is found by adding digits repeatedly until a single digit remains
- The **1089 trick:** any 3-digit number (first and last digits differ by 2+) leads to 1089 through reverse-subtract-reverse-add
- A **palindrome** reads the same forwards and backwards (e.g., 252, 4664)
- **Divisibility by 2:** last digit is 0, 2, 4, 6, or 8
- **Divisibility by 3:** digit sum is divisible by 3
- **Divisibility by 4:** last two digits divisible by 4
- **Divisibility by 5:** last digit is 0 or 5
- **Divisibility by 8:** last three digits divisible by 8
- **Divisibility by 9:** digit sum is divisible by 9
- **Divisibility by 10:** last digit is 0
- **Divisibility by 11:** alternating sum of digits is 0 or divisible by 11

## Important Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Parity | Whether a number is even or odd |
| Even number | Divisible by 2; written as 2n |
| Odd number | Not divisible by 2; written as 2n - 1 |
| Magic square | A grid where every row, column, and diagonal share the same total |
| Magic constant | The shared total in a magic square |
| Palindrome | A number that reads the same forwards and backwards |
| Digit sum | The single digit from repeatedly summing a number's digits |
| Divisibility test | A shortcut rule to check if one number divides another exactly |

## Remember These

- **Five odd numbers always sum to an odd number** -- you can never get an even total from an odd count of odd numbers
- For divisibility by **3 and 9**, use the **digit sum**; for **4 and 8**, check the **last 2 or 3 digits**
- For divisibility by **11**, compute the **alternating sum** (subtract and add digits from right to left)
- The **Chautis Yantra** at Khajuraho's Parshvanath temple is one of the oldest known 4x4 magic squares (magic constant = 34)
