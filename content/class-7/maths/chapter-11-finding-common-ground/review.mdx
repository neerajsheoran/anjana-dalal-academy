## Key Concepts

- A **factor** divides a number exactly; a **multiple** is the result of multiplying a number by a positive integer
- **HCF (Highest Common Factor)** is the **largest** number that divides two or more numbers exactly; also called GCD
- **LCM (Lowest Common Multiple)** is the **smallest** positive number that is a multiple of two or more numbers
- A **prime number** has only two factors: 1 and itself (e.g., 2, 3, 5, 7, 11...)
- **Prime factorisation:** every number can be written as a unique product of primes
- To find prime factorisation: keep dividing by the **smallest** possible prime until you reach 1
- **Finding HCF via primes:** take common primes with **minimum** counts and multiply
- **Finding LCM via primes:** take all primes with **maximum** counts and multiply
- **Golden link:** HCF(a, b) x LCM(a, b) = a x b; so LCM = (a x b) / HCF
- **Co-prime** numbers have HCF = 1 (no common prime factors)
- HCF of consecutive numbers is always **1**; HCF of consecutive even numbers is always **2**
- LCM of co-prime numbers = their **product**
- If one number is a multiple of the other: HCF = smaller number, LCM = larger number
- Doubling both numbers **doubles** both HCF and LCM

## Important Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Factor | A number that divides another exactly |
| Multiple | Result of multiplying a number by a positive integer |
| Prime number | A number > 1 with no factors other than 1 and itself |
| Prime factorisation | Writing a number as a product of its prime factors |
| HCF | Highest Common Factor -- largest number dividing two or more numbers exactly |
| LCM | Lowest Common Multiple -- smallest number that is a multiple of given numbers |
| Co-prime | Two numbers with HCF = 1 |

## Remember These

- For HCF: pick **common** primes with **minimum** power; for LCM: pick **all** primes with **maximum** power
- **HCF x LCM = product of the two numbers** -- use this shortcut to find one if you know the other
- HCF of any two consecutive numbers is always **1** (they are always co-prime)
- HCF is used for **equal sharing/dividing**; LCM is used for **synchronising repeating events**
