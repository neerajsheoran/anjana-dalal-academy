We already know how to add and subtract fractions. Now we tackle the next milestone — learning to **multiply** and **divide** fractions. These skills unlock a huge range of everyday calculations involving parts of quantities.

## Multiplication of Fractions

### Whole Number Times a Fraction

Sunita cycles 2 km in one hour. How far does she go in 4 hours? Easy: 4 × 2 = 8 km.

But her younger brother Arun rides his tricycle at only **1/3 km** per hour. How far does he go in 5 hours?

5 × 1/3 = 1/3 + 1/3 + 1/3 + 1/3 + 1/3 = **5/3 km** = 1 2/3 km

> **Rule:** To multiply a whole number by a fraction, multiply the whole number with the numerator and keep the denominator unchanged.
> **n × a/b = (n × a) / b**

### Fraction Times a Whole Number

How far can Sunita (cycling at 2 km/hour) travel in **1/4** of an hour?

Distance = 1/4 × 2

One-fourth of an hour means splitting the hour into 4 equal parts. She covers 2 km in a full hour, so in one-fourth she covers 2/4 = 1/2 km.

How far in **3/4** of an hour?

3/4 × 2 = (3 × 2) / 4 = **6/4 km** = 1 1/2 km

> **Rule:** To multiply a fraction by a whole number:
> **a/b × n = (a × n) / b**

### Real-Life Applications

**Example:** A tailor uses 3/4 metre of cloth per kurta. How much cloth does she need for 6 kurtas?
Total cloth = 6 × 3/4 = 18/4 = **4 1/2 metres**

**Example:** One hour of parking costs Rs 12. What is the charge for 1 1/2 hours?
1 1/2 = 3/2 hours. Charge = 3/2 × 12 = (3 × 12) / 2 = 36 / 2 = **Rs 18**

## Multiplying Two Fractions Together

Arun rides his tricycle at 1/3 km per hour. How far does he go in **half** an hour?

Distance = 1/2 × 1/3

To find half of one-third, picture a chapati. Cut it into 3 equal pieces (each is 1/3). Now cut each piece in half. The chapati now has 6 pieces, and one piece is **1/6** of the whole.

So: **1/2 × 1/3 = 1/6**

### The General Rule

> **To multiply two fractions, multiply the tops (numerators) together and multiply the bottoms (denominators) together:**
>
> **a/b × c/d = (a × c) / (b × d)**

**Worked examples:**

| Multiplication | Working | Result |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| 3/4 × 2/5 | (3 × 2) / (4 × 5) | **6/20 = 3/10** |
| 4/9 × 3/4 | (4 × 3) / (9 × 4) | **12/36 = 1/3** |
| 7/8 × 2/7 | (7 × 2) / (8 × 7) | **14/56 = 1/4** |

> **Shortcut:** Cancel common factors before multiplying. In 7/8 × 2/7, cancel the 7s to get 1/8 × 2/1, and cancel the 2 with 8 to get 1/4 directly.

### Seeing Fraction Multiplication on Paper

Use a square sheet to see 3/4 × 2/5:
1. Fold the sheet into 5 vertical strips and colour 2 of them (that is 2/5)
2. Fold into 4 horizontal strips and colour 3 of them (that is 3/4)
3. The overlap covers 6 out of 20 small rectangles = **6/20 = 3/10**

### Dealing with Mixed Numbers

Convert mixed numbers to **improper fractions** before multiplying.

**Example:** 1 2/3 × 2 1/4

= 5/3 × 9/4 = (5 × 9) / (3 × 4) = 45/12 = **15/4 = 3 3/4**

## What Happens When You Multiply by a Fraction?

Multiplying a number by a **proper fraction** (value less than 1) shrinks it:

- 8 × 1/4 = 2 (smaller than 8)
- 15 × 2/3 = 10 (smaller than 15)

Multiplying by a fraction **greater than 1** (improper fraction) stretches it:

- 5 × 7/4 = 35/4 = 8 3/4 (larger than 5)

### "Fraction of" Means Multiply

- **1/5 of 30** = 1/5 × 30 = 6
- **3/4 of 80** = 3/4 × 80 = 60
- **2/5 of Rs 150** = 2/5 × 150 = **Rs 60**

**Example:** A colony has 480 families. If 3/8 of them subscribe to a newspaper, how many families subscribe?
Subscribers = 3/8 × 480 = (3 × 480) / 8 = 1440 / 8 = **180 families**

## Division of Fractions

### Dividing a Whole Number by a Fraction

How many glasses of 1/4 litre each can be filled from a 2-litre bottle?

We need: 2 ÷ (1/4)

Each litre contains 4 quarter-litre glasses. So 2 litres give 2 × 4 = **8 glasses**.

So: **2 ÷ (1/4) = 2 × 4 = 8**

> **Core Idea:** Dividing by a fraction is the same as **multiplying by its reciprocal** (the fraction turned upside down).

### The Reciprocal

The **reciprocal** of a/b is **b/a** — swap the top and bottom.

| Fraction | Reciprocal |
|----------|-----------|
| 1/3 | 3/1 = 3 |
| 2/5 | 5/2 |
| 4/7 | 7/4 |
| 3 (= 3/1) | 1/3 |

Multiplying any number by its reciprocal always gives **1**:
- 2/5 × 5/2 = 10/10 = 1

### The Division Rule

> **To divide by a fraction, multiply by its reciprocal:**
>
> **a/b ÷ c/d = a/b × d/c**

**Worked examples:**

| Division | Rewritten as Multiplication | Result |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| 4 ÷ (2/5) | 4 × 5/2 | **20/2 = 10** |
| 5/6 ÷ (1/3) | 5/6 × 3/1 | **15/6 = 5/2 = 2 1/2** |
| 3/8 ÷ (5/6) | 3/8 × 6/5 | **18/40 = 9/20** |

### Why Does Flipping Work?

Consider 8 ÷ (2/5). We are asking: how many groups of 2/5 fit inside 8?

- Each unit contains 5/2 groups of 2/5 (because 1 ÷ (2/5) = 5/2)
- So 8 units contain 8 × 5/2 = 20 groups

Therefore, 8 ÷ (2/5) = 8 × 5/2 = **20**

### Practical Division Problems

**Example:** A rope is 5/6 metre long. It is cut into pieces of 1/6 metre each. How many pieces result?

5/6 ÷ (1/6) = 5/6 × 6/1 = 30/6 = **5 pieces**

**Example:** A drum holds 21/2 litres of oil. Each tin can holds 3/4 litre. How many cans are needed to empty the drum?

21/2 ÷ (3/4) = 21/2 × 4/3 = 84/6 = **14 cans**

## Word Problems with Fractions

**Example:** Meena earns Rs 3600 per month. She spends 2/3 of her income and saves the rest. How much does she save?

Amount spent = 2/3 × 3600 = Rs 2400
Amount saved = 3600 − 2400 = **Rs 1200**

Or: Fraction saved = 1 − 2/3 = 1/3. Savings = 1/3 × 3600 = **Rs 1200**

**Example:** An auto-rickshaw covers 31 1/2 km on 4 1/2 litres of CNG. How far does it go per litre?

Mileage = 31 1/2 ÷ 4 1/2 = 63/2 ÷ 9/2 = 63/2 × 2/9 = 126/18 = **7 km per litre**

## Key Words to Remember

| Word | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Fraction** | A number written as a/b where a is the numerator and b is the denominator |
| **Proper fraction** | A fraction whose numerator is smaller than its denominator (value less than 1) |
| **Improper fraction** | A fraction whose numerator is at least as large as its denominator (value 1 or more) |
| **Mixed number** | A number combining a whole number and a proper fraction (e.g., 1 2/3) |
| **Reciprocal** | The fraction formed by swapping numerator and denominator; a/b becomes b/a |
| **Multiplying fractions** | Multiply numerators together and denominators together: (a/b) × (c/d) = (ac)/(bd) |
| **Dividing fractions** | Multiply by the reciprocal: (a/b) ÷ (c/d) = (a/b) × (d/c) |
| **"Fraction of"** | Means multiplication: 3/4 of 80 = 3/4 × 80 = 60 |
