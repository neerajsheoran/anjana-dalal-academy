## Key Concepts

- A **triangle** has 3 vertices, 3 sides, and 3 interior angles
- **Equilateral triangle:** all 3 sides equal, all 3 angles = **60 degrees**
- **Isosceles triangle:** exactly 2 sides equal, base angles are equal
- **Scalene triangle:** no two sides are equal
- **Triangle inequality:** every side must be **shorter than** the sum of the other two sides
- Quick test: check if the **longest side < sum of the other two** -- if yes, triangle is possible
- **Angle sum property:** the three interior angles of any triangle always add up to **180 degrees**
- **Acute triangle:** all angles less than 90 degrees
- **Right triangle:** one angle is exactly 90 degrees
- **Obtuse triangle:** one angle is greater than 90 degrees
- A triangle **cannot** have two right angles or two obtuse angles (would exceed 180 degrees)
- **Exterior angle** = sum of the two non-adjacent interior angles
- To construct a triangle from 3 sides: draw base, then use compass arcs from both endpoints
- To construct an equilateral triangle: all three compass arcs use the **same radius** as the side length

## Important Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Triangle | A closed shape with 3 sides, 3 vertices, and 3 interior angles |
| Equilateral triangle | All three sides (and angles) are equal |
| Isosceles triangle | Exactly two sides are equal |
| Scalene triangle | All three sides are different |
| Triangle inequality | Every side must be shorter than the sum of the other two |
| Angle sum property | Interior angles of a triangle always total 180° |
| Exterior angle | Angle between an extended side and the adjacent side |
| Acute triangle | Every angle is less than 90° |
| Right triangle | One angle is exactly 90° |
| Obtuse triangle | One angle exceeds 90° |

## Remember These

- To check if a triangle can exist, you only need **one check**: longest side < sum of the other two
- If the longest side **equals** the sum of the other two, the points lie in a straight line -- no triangle forms
- Each angle of an equilateral triangle is exactly **60 degrees** (180 / 3)
- The exterior angle of a triangle is always **greater** than either of the two non-adjacent interior angles
